Apple Arcade November: 5 New Games Coming

Apple Arcade's November 2026 lineup is set: five games are joining the subscription service next month, led by the exclusive Crossy Road Rush on November 5.

Why this matters: November's headliner is a brand-new Crossy Road adventure, and every game on the list arrives with no ads and no in-app purchases, as with all Apple Arcade titles.

Apple Arcade November 2026: the key facts

  • Crossy Road Rush (November 5): a new island platform adventure from Hipster Whale, exclusive to Apple Arcade, where players swerve, spin and smash through an arcade island with the Crossy Road Crew
  • NBA 2K27 Arcade Edition (November 5): the latest mobile iteration of the basketball franchise, in sports-arcade style
  • KAMI 2+ (November 5): a calming puzzle game about folding paper into beautiful patterns
  • Food Stylist – Design Game+ (November 5): a cozy design game about plating virtual dishes and tablescapes
  • Football Manager 27 Touch (November 10): the football management sim for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV, with cloud saves carrying over from past titles
  • Platforms: Apple Arcade on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV; no Android versions have been announced

What to watch next

Watch for reviews of Crossy Road Rush once it lands on November 5, and for whether any of November's games get a wider release later. We already covered Football Manager 27 Touch's November 10 launch in detail, and you can find more mobile gaming news on the Top 100 apps page.

Source: Mobile Games Network's November 2026 Apple Arcade lineup (published 2026-10-24). Details as reported there; dates and details may change.
