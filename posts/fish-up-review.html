Fish Up! Review: Cute Fish Sorting (2026)

Quick verdict — 4.0 / 5. Fish Up! is a sweet, relaxing sort-and-match puzzle: tap the bubbles, sort three identical fish into a jar, and slowly build a dream aquarium. It is free, Everyone-rated and already a chart hit in its first month — but be ready for frequent ads, and know that its star rating still rests on only a few dozen reviews.

Fish Up!, from developer iKame Games - Zego Studio, arrived on Google Play in September 2026 and has already reached about 120,000 installs — every one of them in its first 30 days, averaging roughly 4,400 installs a day according to AppBrain's data updated 24 October 2026. It is ranked #1 in Top New Free Puzzle in Poland, with other top-ten puzzle showings in Germany and France. For a brand-new sort puzzle with no big brand behind it, that is a fast start — and the reason it earns a review here this week.

The idea is instantly familiar, and that is the point. Cute cartoon fish float around in bubbles, and your job is to fish them out and sort them into the jars at the top of the screen. Each jar wants three of the same kind: fill it, and it floats away so a new jar can take its place. Clear every bubble and you win the level. It is the same satisfying loop that made the whole sort-puzzle genre popular, dressed up here in a bright underwater world instead of the usual coloured balls or bottles.

How it works

You start each level by looking at the jars along the top. Each jar carries a small tag showing which fish it wants and how many it already has — for example, 1/3 or 2/3. Below them sits a board full of bubbles, each holding a fish. Tap a fish and it hops into its matching jar, or waits in a spare spot if that jar is not open yet. Match three identical fish and the jar clears, freeing space for the next one.

That is the whole core loop, but the levels get trickier as you go. More species appear, the board fills up, and you have to plan a few taps ahead instead of just grabbing the nearest fish. The developer promises hundreds of levels, with new fish-match puzzles added regularly, plus daily rewards for coming back. There is also an offline mode, so you can play on a flight or anywhere without a signal — a real plus for a game meant for short, relaxing sessions.

Building your dream aquarium

Sorting is only half the game. Every successful match helps you collect new species for your collection album and earn decorations to customise your own ocean world. The fish themselves are the stars: orange tabby-style reef fish, pufferfish, jellyfish, seahorses and more, all drawn as round, cheerful cartoon creatures. It is a deliberately gentle look, which fits its Everyone rating.

When a level gets stubborn, the game offers boosters to help you out of a tight spot. The store description does not spell out every booster in detail, so treat them as a helping hand rather than a guaranteed rescue — and, as the cons below explain, some players say the game leans on ad-supported help more heavily than they would like.

Why it is trending

Fish Up! is trending because it arrived at the right moment with a proven formula. Sort puzzles are one of the most popular casual genres on Google Play right now, and a fish theme stands out from the endless coloured-ball and bottle-sorting clones. At the same time, the game is easy to recommend: it is free, it works offline, so it fits neatly into a commute or a coffee break.

The numbers back up the chart story. All 120,000 installs arrived in the first month, and AppBrain calls it “New, Growing fast, Top 10” — its best showing is that #1 Top New Free Puzzle spot in Poland, with Germany at #4 and France at #7 in the same puzzle list. That is strong early traction for a first-month game, even if the rating base is still tiny.

Key features

  • Sort and match-3 gameplay — tap bubble fish and sort three identical fish into each jar to clear the board.
  • Hundreds of levels — more species and busier boards as you progress, with new puzzles added regularly.
  • Dream aquarium to build — collect species in a collection album and earn decorations to customise your ocean world.
  • Daily rewards — a reason to come back each day and keep the collection growing.
  • Handy boosters — help for tricky levels when the board gets crowded.
  • Offline mode — play without an internet connection, anywhere.
  • Cute cartoon presentation — bright, cheerful fish animations in a relaxing underwater setting.
  • Everyone rating — Google Play rates the app's content as suitable for everyone.

Pros and cons

Pros
  • Fast riser: about 120,000 installs in its first month, #1 Top New Free Puzzle in Poland
  • Instantly understandable sort-and-match gameplay, with no learning curve
  • Relaxing, family-friendly look — cute fish, Everyone rating
  • Offline play and daily rewards make it easy to dip into for a few minutes
  • Collection album and aquarium decorations give sorting a longer-term goal
Cons
  • Contains ads, and player reviews complain they are frequent — some say several ads are needed to clear a level
  • Some players report bubbles not moving as expected, or a needed fish not appearing on the board
  • Only a few dozen ratings so far, so the roughly 4-star average is early and will move
  • About 130 MB is large for a simple sort puzzle — a heavy download for a light game
  • AppBrain lists 15 permissions for the app, worth a glance before installing

Pricing

Fish Up! is free to download on Google Play, and it contains ads. The listing data checked for this review shows it as free with ads, with no in-app purchase price range stated in that data, so check any offer you meet inside the game before accepting it. The current version is 1.7.0, updated 12 October 2026, and the download is about 130 MB — bigger than most sort puzzles, so a Wi-Fi download is the sensible choice.

Download

Download Fish Up! on Google Play

If you like gentle sorting games with a cute twist, Fish Up! is an easy, free try: a relaxing loop, a growing fish collection, and a chart climb that suggests plenty of other players are enjoying it too — just go in expecting ads along the way.
