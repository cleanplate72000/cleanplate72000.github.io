Dispatch Is Coming to Mobile on December 9 — First Four Episodes Free

AdHoc Studio's hit narrative game Dispatch is coming to mobile. The studio has announced that Dispatch will launch on Android and iOS on December 9, with support for Google Play Games on PC — and the mobile version is being rebuilt for touch and vertical play.

Why this matters: Dispatch has passed 5 million players since its launch, and mobile is its biggest new audience yet. A touch-first rebuild — rather than a straight port — suggests AdHoc is serious about phones, not just ticking a platform box.

Dispatch mobile: what we know

  • Release date: December 9 (Android and iOS)
  • Developer: AdHoc Studio
  • Price model: First four episodes free; full game unlocks for $19.99
  • PC support: Google Play Games on PC
  • Pre-registration: Opening soon on Google Play; pre-orders opening soon on the App Store

What is Dispatch?

Dispatch is a narrative game from AdHoc Studio that has built a large audience since launch, passing 5 million players. The mobile edition keeps the story-driven experience but reworks controls and presentation for touchscreens and vertical play — the way most people actually hold their phones.

What to watch next

Watch for the pre-registration pages going live on Google Play and the App Store, which the studio says will open soon. If you want to try it free, the first four episodes cost nothing — the $19.99 unlock is only for the full game. We will update this story when pre-registration opens and when the December 9 launch arrives.

Source: Mobilegamer.biz new games digest. Details as reported there; prices and dates may change before launch.
