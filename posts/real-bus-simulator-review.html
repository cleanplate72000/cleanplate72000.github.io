Real Bus Simulator Review: City Routes (2026)

Quick verdict — 3.5 / 5. Real Bus Simulator 3D City Bus is a straightforward, free bus driving game with three modes, three buses and a genuinely large player base — but its divided 3.2-star rating and the many player complaints about ads mean you should go in with expectations set: fun behind the wheel, interruptions around it.

Real Bus Simulator 3D City Bus, from developer GamesPivot, has been on Google Play since June 2024 and has now reached about 3.9 million installs — including roughly 470,000 in the last 30 days, around 16,000 a day, according to AppBrain data updated 24 October 2026. Its best chart showing is #4 in Top Free Role Playing in Turkey, with top-ten showings in Denmark, Indonesia and Norway and a #13 spot in India. That is a lot of recent traction for a bus simulator, and the reason it earns a review here this week — even though, as the rating below shows, players do not all agree about it.

The pitch is simple, and the short store description says it in one line: "Drive 3 buses across Career, Free & Offroad modes with exciting missions!" There is no complicated management layer and no story to follow. You pick a bus, pick a mode, and drive — through city streets, on open roads, and over rough offroad tracks, completing missions or simply exploring at your own pace.

Three modes, three buses

The game is built around three modes. In Career Mode, you work through levels and missions, following routes, reaching destinations and improving your driving as each challenge is completed. Every level brings a new driving experience, and each one opens with a cinematic cutscene that introduces the mission before you take the wheel.

Free Mode drops the missions entirely. You drive without strict objectives, explore the environment, practise your driving and enjoy a relaxed session at your own pace — the mode to pick when you just want to drive. Offroad Mode is the change of pace: rough roads, dirt tracks and uneven paths where keeping control of a large bus is the challenge itself, rather than following a city route.

Across all three modes you have three different buses to drive, each with its own look and feel on the road. Three buses is not a huge fleet by simulator standards, but it is enough variety for a free game — and the screenshots show clearly different liveries, from the black coach in the depot to the green-and-red coach on a rural road.

How it drives

The on-screen controls are exactly what a mobile bus simulator needs, and the screenshots show them clearly: a steering wheel on the left, pedals and gear controls on the right, and buttons for the horn, indicators, lights and camera views along the edges. A small map sits in the corner, and the top bar tracks your timer, level, star rating and fuel — one screenshot shows 25 of 50 litres remaining, so fuel is something to watch on longer runs.

That control layout is a genuine plus. Mobile driving games live or die by whether you can steer, signal and change camera without fumbling, and here everything is laid out around the edges of the screen rather than buried in menus. The camera options matter too: players can switch views, including a driver's viewpoint, although — fairly noted in the cons below — at least one player complained that a top-of-screen ad intrudes on that view.

Why it is trending

Real Bus Simulator 3D City Bus is trending on momentum, not on a single viral moment. It is a two-year-old game that is still adding players fast: 470,000 installs in the last 30 days is a big number for a driving simulator, and its chart positions are spread across many countries rather than one market — #4 in Turkey, #6 in Denmark, Indonesia and Norway, #13 in India and #16 in Brazil and Mexico for Top Free Role Playing, per AppBrain's rankings.

Bus simulators are a durable niche on Google Play, and this one covers the three things that niche wants: city routes, free driving, and offroad terrain, all in one free download of about 102 MB. That breadth, plus regular updates — the current version is 13, updated 24 August 2026 — helps explain why it keeps climbing charts long after launch.

Key features

  • Three game modes — Career missions, relaxed Free driving, and challenging Offroad terrain.
  • Three buses to drive — different coaches with their own liveries and feel.
  • Cinematic cutscenes — a short intro scene at the start of every level.
  • City and offroad environments — realistic roads, challenging routes and dirt tracks.
  • Full driving controls — steering wheel, horn, indicators, lights and switchable cameras.
  • Fuel and mission tracking — timer, level, star rating and fuel shown while you drive.
  • Free to download — no upfront price on Google Play.
  • General-audience content — AppBrain lists its content rating as Low Maturity.

Pros and cons

Pros
  • Strong recent traction: about 470,000 installs in the last 30 days, 3.9 million in total
  • Three distinct modes in one free game — missions, free driving and offroad
  • Clear, complete driving controls with camera options, visible in every screenshot
  • City, night and offroad driving give real variety for a free simulator
  • Regularly updated — version 13 arrived in August 2026, two years after launch
Cons
  • Divided rating: only about 3.2 stars on Google Play, from roughly 2,500 ratings
  • Contains ads, and player reviews complain they are frequent — some say ads block the top of the screen
  • Only three buses, so the fleet will feel small for long-term players
  • About 102 MB is a sizeable download for a casual simulator
  • AppBrain lists 15 permissions for the app, worth a glance before installing

Pricing

Real Bus Simulator 3D City Bus is free to download on Google Play, and it contains ads. The listing data checked for this review shows no in-app purchase price range stated, so check any offer you meet inside the game before accepting it. The current version is 13, updated 24 August 2026, and the download is about 102 MB — a Wi-Fi download is the sensible choice.

Download

Download Real Bus Simulator 3D City Bus on Google Play

If you like driving simulators with a bit of everything — city routes, free roaming and rough terrain — Real Bus Simulator 3D City Bus is an easy free try, and its chart climb suggests plenty of other players are enjoying it too — just go in expecting ads along the way.
