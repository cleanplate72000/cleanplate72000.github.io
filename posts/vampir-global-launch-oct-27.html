VAMPIR Global Launch Set for October 27

Netmarble has announced that VAMPIR, its vampire-themed massively multiplayer online role-playing game (MMORPG), will launch in additional countries and regions worldwide on October 27, 2026, at 7:00 p.m. PDT, on PC and mobile with full cross-platform support.

Why this matters: VAMPIR already topped the charts in Korea, and this global launch is the first chance for players in many more regions to play it on the phone or PC they already own, together.

VAMPIR: the key facts

  • Game: VAMPIR, a vampire-themed MMORPG from Netmarble Neo
  • Global launch: October 27, 2026, at 7:00 p.m. PDT
  • Platforms: Mobile (App Store and Google Play) and PC (Epic Games Store), with full cross-platform play
  • Pre-download: Opens October 26, 2026, at 7:00 p.m. PDT, so players can start the moment the game goes live
  • Pre-registration rewards: A Designated Heroic Mount Summon Ticket, 100 million Gold, a full set of Rare Equipment and Immortal Portrait Fragments, plus a welcome coupon code via the official Discord
  • Track record: First launched in Korea in August 2025, where Netmarble says it became the longest-running No. 1 among all new games released in Korea that year; later launches followed in Taiwan, Hong Kong and Macau in March 2026 and Japan in July 2026
  • Setting: A dark vampire world Netmarble describes as a universe ruled by blood, desire and destruction, with a stylised gothic horror look

What to watch next

Watch for the store listings going live for pre-download on October 26, and for how the global servers hold up on launch day. Because of the game's dark vampire theme, check the store listing before downloading for younger players. For more mobile gaming news, see our Apple Arcade November story and the Top 100 apps page.
