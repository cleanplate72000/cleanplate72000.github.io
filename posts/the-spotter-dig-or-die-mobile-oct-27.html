The Spotter: Dig or Die Lands on Mobile Today

The Spotter: Dig or Die comes to mobile today, October 27. Publisher Doghowl Games announced the launch on its news page, with the game arriving on iPhone, Mac, Android and Android PC.

The Spotter: Dig or Die on mobile: the key facts

  • Release date: October 27, 2026, as announced by Doghowl Games on October 27, 2026.
  • Platforms: iPhone, Mac, Android and Android PC, as listed in the announcement.
  • The loop: mine by day and defend by night — daytime mining meets night-time tower defence.
  • By day: descend underground to mine, finding items to recycle, characters, rare artifacts and surprises hidden in the depths.
  • By night: waves of mutants attack your outpost, and how well you mined decides how well you can hold it.
  • The stakes: losing the outpost ends the run, but you can return and try a different strategy.
  • The goal: you play the last spotter at a wrecked gas station, digging the wasteland for parts, fortifying against the mutant waves and rebuilding a receiver to catch one final broadcast.
  • Price: the announcement does not state a price, so none is given here — check the store listing for your region before buying.

Why it matters

The day-night split is what makes The Spotter stand out: every mining decision is really a defence decision, because rarer ores unlock better upgrades and stronger weapons for the outpost. A run that ends in a lost outpost is not wasted either — it teaches you which shafts and upgrades to prioritise next time. For mobile players, it is a complete loop in short sessions: dig, build, survive the night, and try again smarter.
