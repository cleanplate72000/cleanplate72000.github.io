Edits Review: Instagram's Video Editor Is Beating CapCut in 2026

Edits is Instagram's own video editor — and it is quietly winning the fight for creators. Launched in April 2025, it has already passed 130 million installs with a 4.7-star rating from 1.8 million ratings, and it currently ranks #1 in the Top Free Video Players & Editors chart in India and #4 in the US. The headline feature creators love most: 4K exports with no watermark, free.

Our verdict: 4.6/5. If you post Reels, Edits is the most complete free editor right now — watermark-free 4K exports, AI animation, green screen, auto captions, and a live insights dashboard that shows how each video performs. It is less template-heavy than CapCut, but for serious creators that is a fair trade.

Key features that make Edits trending

4K exports, no watermark. This alone makes Edits stand out — most free editors stamp a watermark on your video or charge to remove it. Edits exports clean 4K clips for free.

AI animation and green screen. Animate still images with AI, drop in green-screen backgrounds and layer overlays — tools that used to need a desktop editor.

Auto captions. Generate accurate subtitles automatically and style them for Reels and Shorts, without typing a word.

Live insights dashboard. Edits shows views, reach, watch time and engagement for your videos (shown above) — so you can see what actually works instead of guessing.

Instagram integration. Built by Instagram, Edits is designed around Reels: inspiration feeds, high-quality playback and one-tap sharing to your Instagram account.

Edits vs CapCut: which should you pick?

Pick Edits if you post Reels and want watermark-free 4K exports and real performance insights. Pick CapCut if you live on templates and trending effects — its template library is still bigger. Many creators now use both: CapCut for template ideas, Edits for the final cut. Read our CapCut review to compare, and our 10 best free photo editing apps for stills.

Pros
  • 4K exports with no watermark, completely free
  • 4.7-star rating — among the best-loved editors on Play
  • AI animation, green screen, overlays and auto captions
  • Insights dashboard shows real video performance
Cons
  • Fewer templates and music features than CapCut
  • Deep toolkit has a learning curve for beginners
  • Best suited to Instagram creators; less TikTok-focused

Who should download Edits?

Download it if you make Reels or Shorts and want clean, high-quality exports without paying. Skip it if you only want one-tap templates — CapCut still wins there. Either way, it costs nothing to try, and the 4.7-star rating says most creators stick with it.

Edits FAQ

Is Edits free? Yes — Edits is free on Google Play, including 4K exports without a watermark.

Who makes Edits? Edits is made and published by Instagram.

Does Edits add a watermark? No — Edits exports videos without a watermark, even at 4K.

Is Edits good for Reels? Yes — it is built for Reels, with inspiration feeds, auto captions and an insights dashboard tuned to Instagram performance.
