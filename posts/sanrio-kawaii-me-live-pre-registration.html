Sanrio Rhythm Game Opens Pre-Registration

Sanrio Kawaii Me Live! has opened pre-registration on iOS and Android. It is the first release under the Sanrio Games Brand, and it puts Sanrio's best-known mascots — including Hello Kitty and My Melody — at the centre of a rhythm game.

Key facts

  • Pre-registration: open now on iOS and Android, as reported by Pocket Gamer on October 22, 2026.
  • Launch window: 2027 — no exact date has been announced yet.
  • The game: recruit your favourite Sanrio characters, customise their outfits, and build a stage of your own for them to perform on.
  • Two ways to play: Performance mode lets you simply watch your mascots perform, while Technical mode turns it into a full rhythm game for experienced players.
  • Music: tracks from Universal Music artists such as Soala, Lienel and Polkadot Stingray, plus songs drawn from the franchise's own history.
  • Customisation: stage decorations and costumes let you make each performance your own.

Why it matters

Rhythm games are underused on mobile compared with puzzles and RPGs, so Sanrio is betting that familiar characters and a watch-or-play choice will pull in both longtime fans and newcomers. The two modes matter most: a younger or casual player can enjoy the show without failing a chart, while a seasoned rhythm player still gets a real challenge in Technical mode. As the first game under the new Sanrio Games Brand, it also sets the tone for whatever Sanrio does next in games.

What to watch next

Watch for a firmer 2027 launch date, the full character line-up, and whether pre-registration brings any launch rewards — none have been confirmed in the announcement coverage so far. For another family-friendly mobile launch this month, see our Dr. Seuss Learn & Play launch on iOS.

Source: Pocket Gamer, "Sanrio Kawaii Me Live! opens pre-reg for the new mascot rhythm game" (published October 22, 2026). Details such as the launch date and rewards may change — check the source and the official store pages for the latest information.
