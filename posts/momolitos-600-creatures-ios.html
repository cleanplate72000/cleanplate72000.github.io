Momolitos Brings 600 Creatures to iOS

Solo developer Goca, behind Goca Games, has brought Momolitos to iOS, Nintendo Switch and Steam, a creature-collecting adventure where what you feed your creatures decides what they become.

Why this matters: Instead of evolving creatures by level alone, Momolitos hides its evolution routes behind food choices, so experimenting with ingredients is the core of the game rather than a side activity.

Momolitos: the key facts

  • Game: Momolitos, from solo developer Goca (Goca Games)
  • Platforms: iOS, Nintendo Switch and Steam; no Android version has been mentioned
  • Launch: October 22, 2026, as reported by Pocket Gamer
  • Creatures: 600 creatures to discover through food-based evolution
  • Gameplay: Start with an egg, raise what hatches, explore colourful worlds to find ingredients, solve puzzles and play mini-games, then try new foods to see what evolves
  • Home base: MomoLAB, where players look after their creatures, log discoveries and prepare for the next trip

What to watch next

Watch for player reaction to the hidden evolution routes and whether an Android version is announced. For more mobile gaming news, see our Genshin Impact 7.2 story and the Top 100 apps page.
