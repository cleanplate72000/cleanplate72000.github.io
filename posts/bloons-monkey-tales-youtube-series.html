Bloons Monkey Tales Brings TD 6 to YouTube

Ninja Kiwi is bringing its hit Bloons franchise to animation with Bloons Monkey Tales, a 3D-animated show based on Bloons TD 6 that airs on YouTube, as well as TikTok, Facebook and other platforms.

Why this matters: instead of a new game update, Bloons fans get a weekly show — a free, family-friendly way to follow the same monkeys outside the tower-defence battles.

Bloons Monkey Tales: the key facts

  • Show: Bloons Monkey Tales, a 3D-animated series from Ninja Kiwi, based on Bloons TD 6.
  • Where to watch: YouTube, plus TikTok, Facebook and other platforms.
  • New episodes: set to drop every Saturday.
  • Story: familiar Bloons TD characters come together as a group of monkeys defending their home from the invading Bloons.
  • Shorts too: the episodes are accompanied by a series of shorts offering more glimpses of the monkeys' adventures.
  • Voices: the show features the voice talents of people who have worked on the games, plus cameos from two popular Bloons YouTubers.
  • Run length: Ninja Kiwi has not revealed how long the series will run for — so no end date is announced yet.

Why it matters

Bloons has always been about monkeys holding a line against waves of balloons, so a show built around those same characters is a natural next step — it gives the monkeys personalities and stories instead of just upgrade paths. A Saturday release rhythm also makes it easy to follow week by week, and because it lives on free platforms like YouTube, there is no new game to buy to enjoy it.

What to watch next

Watch for each Saturday's new episode and its accompanying shorts, and whether Ninja Kiwi announces how long the first run will last. For more gaming news, see our Dunk City Dynasty Season 10 story and browse the Top 100 apps page.
