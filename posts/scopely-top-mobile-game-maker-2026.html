Scopely Named No. 1 Mobile Game Maker of 2026

Scopely has been named No. 1 on PocketGamer.biz's 2026 Top 50 Mobile Game Makers list — the annual ranking of the world's leading mobile game companies. It is the second time in the last three years that Scopely has taken the top spot, having previously topped the list in 2024.

Why this matters: The Top 50 list is one of mobile gaming's most-watched industry rankings, judged on revenue, new releases, innovation, business expansion, growth and live operations. Scopely topping it again underlines just how dominant its mix of long-running hits and new games has become.

Scopely's No. 1 ranking: the key facts

  • Ranking: No. 1 on PocketGamer.biz's Top 50 Mobile Game Makers of 2026
  • Previous top spot: Scopely also topped the list in 2024, and has appeared on it for 11 straight years
  • Why it won: PocketGamer.biz cited new hit games, successful acquisitions, innovation, and a central position in industry trends, alongside enduring games and partnerships with creative teams
  • Milestone year: The award lands in Scopely's 15th anniversary year, which began with the announcement that its games had passed $15 billion in lifetime revenue and more than 150 billion hours of playtime
  • Awards season: Scopely was also named a Fast Company Best Workplace for Innovators for the second straight year, and was the most-honoured company at the 2026 Pocket Gamer Mobile Games Awards with 12 honours, including Best Publisher for the fifth time
  • US ranking too: Scopely also landed at No. 1 on PocketGamer.biz's Top 30 US Mobile Game Makers of 2026

What is the Top 50 Mobile Game Makers list?

PocketGamer.biz publishes the Top 50 Mobile Game Makers list every year, ranking the companies shaping mobile gaming. The factors include revenue, the quality of new game releases, innovation, business expansion, growth and live-operations success — so a No. 1 finish reflects a strong year across the board, not just one hit game.

What to watch next

Watch whether Scopely's run of hits continues into 2027, and which studios challenge it at the top of next year's list. For more mobile gaming news, see our Google Play Arena launch story and the Top 100 apps page.
