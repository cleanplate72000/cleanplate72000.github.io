Supercell Now Owns Merge Mansion Maker

Supercell has completed its acquisition of Merge Mansion developer Metacore, which is now fully owned by the Finnish publisher. Metacore announced the news on October 19, saying its game team has officially started as “Supercellians”, while Supercell welcomed the team with the message that the Merge Mansion team “has moved in”.

Why this matters: Merge Mansion now sits inside Supercell's live-games portfolio, with the same team continuing to develop it and a stated aim of returning the game to its chart-topping spot — so players should see continued updates rather than a change of hands to a new team.

Supercell and Metacore: the key facts

  • Deal: Supercell has completed its full acquisition of Metacore, taking 100% ownership
  • Announced: October 19, 2026, in LinkedIn posts from both companies
  • Game: Merge Mansion, the casual puzzle game in which players merge objects to restore a mansion
  • What changes: Merge Mansion joins Supercell's game portfolio; the existing team continues to develop it
  • Financial terms: Not disclosed; the deal had been expected to close at the end of September
  • History: Supercell was already Metacore's largest shareholder and a long-term investor and partner; it announced plans to buy the rest of the studio in May 2026
  • Leadership: Former Small Giant Games chief executive Jose Saarniniemi joined as general manager of Merge Mansion in June 2026
  • Scale: Merge Mansion is estimated to have earned around $490 million since its 2020 launch, according to AppMagic figures cited by the report
  • Restructuring: Metacore has since cut 159 staff at its Helsinki headquarters and closed its offices in Germany and Sweden

What to watch next

Watch for Merge Mansion's next updates under Supercell, which will show whether the new ownership translates into a return up the charts. No immediate change to the player experience was announced alongside the deal. For more Supercell news, see our Squad Busters shutdown story and the Top 100 apps page.
