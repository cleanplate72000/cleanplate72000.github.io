Rainbow Six Mobile Shuts Down Jan 15

Ubisoft has announced it is closing Rainbow Six Mobile, its free-to-play 5v5 tactical shooter for phones. The game will remain available until January 15, 2027, after which it will be closed.

Key facts

  • Closing date: Rainbow Six Mobile stays online until January 15, 2027, then its online services end.
  • One more season first: Ubisoft says the game's next season will still launch as planned and run until the closure.
  • 15 seasons: in its message to players, the game team thanked players for shaping the game through 15 seasons since its soft launch in 2023.
  • Long road to launch: first announced in April 2022, the game had been in development for three years at announcement — and seven years by the time it launched, according to Mobilegamer.biz.
  • Scale, estimated: AppMagic estimates cited by Mobilegamer.biz put lifetime earnings at about $5.3 million in in-app purchase revenue from 13.1 million downloads, including the extended soft launch that began in 2023. These are estimates, not Ubisoft figures.
  • What is still unconfirmed: Ubisoft has said more details about the transition will follow in the coming weeks. What happens to unspent in-game currency and past purchases has not yet been confirmed, so players should wait for that update before spending further.

Why it matters

For mobile shooter fans, this is another reminder of how fragile live-service games can be: a major franchise title, built over seven years by many Ubisoft studios, is closing less than a year after its global launch. It follows a pattern already seen this year on this site — see our Squad Busters shutdown story — where even big-name mobile games can be switched off when they do not find a large enough lasting audience.

What to watch next

Watch for Ubisoft's promised transition update in the coming weeks, which should clarify refunds, unspent currency and exactly when the final season ends. Until then, the only confirmed facts are the January 15, 2027 closure date and that the next season still goes ahead.

Source: Mobilegamer.biz, "Ubisoft is closing Rainbow Six Mobile just seven months after launch", reporting Ubisoft's announcement and quoting the game team's message to players. Details such as transition arrangements may change — check the source and Ubisoft's announcement for the latest information.
