Final Fantasy Tactics Gets Controller Support

Square Enix has updated Final Fantasy Tactics: The War of the Lions on iPhone, iPad and Android, adding full controller support in what RPG Site calls the mobile version's first major update in over five years.

Key facts

  • Controller support: gamepads are now supported on both iOS and Android, as reported by Pocket Gamer on October 21, 2026.
  • Smoother play: the update adds 60fps support, so battles and movement run more smoothly than before.
  • Fast-forward option: a new fast-forward option lets players speed through parts of the game.
  • Battle interface changes: the battle UI has been revised alongside the other improvements.
  • Platforms: iPhone, iPad and Android. On iOS, the iPhone and iPad versions are separate releases, not one universal app.
  • The game itself: a turn-based tactical RPG following Ramza in the kingdom of Ivalice during the Lion War, based on the 1997 original.
  • Not the remake: this update is for The War of the Lions on mobile, not the newer Final Fantasy Tactics: The Ivalice Chronicles remake on consoles and PC.

Why it matters

Tactical battles are long, careful affairs, and touch controls are not always the best fit for them. A proper gamepad changes that: players can settle in for a full battle without fighting the interface, and 60fps play makes the whole game feel more modern. It is also a welcome sign of support for an older premium mobile title, at a time when many publishers have walked away from their older mobile games instead of updating them.

What to watch next

Watch for any further patches to the mobile version, and for whether The Ivalice Chronicles ever makes its own way to mobile — RPG Site notes that as a hope, not an announcement. For another big mobile launch on the horizon, see our Monster Hunter Outlanders story and the Top 100 apps page.

Source: Pocket Gamer, "Final Fantasy Tactics: War of the Lions gets controller support 15 years after release" (published October 21, 2026), corroborated by RPG Site. Details may change — check the source and the official store pages for the latest information.
