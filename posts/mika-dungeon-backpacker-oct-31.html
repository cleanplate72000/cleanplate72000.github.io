Mika: Dungeon Backpacker Hits Mobile Oct 31

Mika: Dungeon Backpacker is due on the App Store and Google Play on October 31, a backpack-building roguelite for iOS and Android in which you play not the hero, but the Dungeon Supporter who packs the heroes' bags.

Why this matters: it turns inventory management into the whole game — every step, every bag slot and every merged weapon is a decision, not an afterthought.

Mika: Dungeon Backpacker: the key facts

  • Release date: October 31, on the App Store and Google Play for iOS and Android.
  • Genre: a backpack-building roguelite with grid-based dungeons.
  • You play Mika: a Dungeon Supporter who packs the bags while a party of four heroes takes the credit.
  • Setting: Stonia, a continent slowly turning into dungeons; Mika's latest job takes her into cathedral ruins where an expedition disappeared a century ago.
  • Exploration: you move one tile at a time, and you do not know what is behind a door until you open it — a chest, a Mimic or a trap.
  • Food is the clock: every step costs a unit of food, so scouring every corner for loot eats into what you have left for the boss on the tenth floor.
  • Bag building: on each floor you pick gear from a shop, squeeze it into limited bag space like a puzzle, and merge matching weapons into stronger ones.
  • Party: four heroes built around Neutral, Order and Chaos alignments to counter what each monster does; once a fight starts it mostly plays itself, with everything in the bag firing on cooldown.
  • Beyond the main run: Blood Arena pits your party and bag against other players', Nightmare Tower wants every hero you own, and an idle reward system ticks away in the background.

Why it matters

Bag-packing games have done well on phones this year, but Mika's twist is that the walk to the shop costs something: charging food for every tile turns exploration into spending, so a detour for one more chest is a choice you pay for at the boss. That single rule makes the packing puzzle matter far beyond the inventory screen.
