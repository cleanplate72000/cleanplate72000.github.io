Supermarket Game Review: Family Fun (2026)

Quick verdict — 3.5 / 5. Supermarket Game, from FM by Bubadu, is a cheerful, family-friendly collection of 10 supermarket mini-games: run the till, sort the cheese, catch a thief and drive the delivery truck. It is free, rated Everyone, and hugely popular — 100,000,000+ installs on Google Play — but frequent ads and a divided player rating keep it from a higher score.

Supermarket Game is the long-running casual game from developer FM by Bubadu (package com.bubadu.supermarket), on Google Play since June 2016. The current version, 1.66, was updated in September 2026, it is about 43 MB, needs Android 7.0 or later, and Google Play rates it Everyone. At the last check it held about 4.0 stars from over 240,000 ratings, and it is trending again right now: AppBrain's trending list for 26 October 2026 puts it at 480,000 installs in the last 7 days, five times the week before, with 1.3 million installs in the last 30 days and a #20 place in the Top Free Casual chart in Indonesia (and #29 in India).

That is a remarkable second wind for a ten-year-old game, and the reason is easy to see in the screenshots: this is not one game but a whole supermarket of small ones, each simple enough for a child to pick up in seconds. If you want a gentle, offline-friendly game for a younger player — or a nostalgic, low-pressure time-filler for yourself — it is worth a look. Just go in with your eyes open about the ads.

All screenshots in this review are the developer's own promotional images from the official Google Play Store listing.

What Supermarket Game does

There is no single goal here, and that is the point. Supermarket Game bundles 10 mini-games, each set in a different corner of the same bright, cartoon supermarket. You move between departments, complete short tasks, and earn bronze, silver and gold medals for your achievements. Nothing is timed harshly, nothing is violent, and there is no chat with strangers — which, together with its COPPA certification (more below), is why it works so well as a children's game.

The star of the show is the cash register. As the screenshots show, you scan items with the barcode reader, work the till, and take the right money — a small, satisfying loop that also sneaks in a bit of counting practice, which several parent reviewers praise. Around it sit nine other departments, each with its own mechanic, so a child who gets bored of one job can simply wander to another aisle.

The 10 mini-games

  • Cash register — scan items, issue invoices and take the correct payment, just like a real cashier.
  • Grocery — a hidden-object game: find the cake, lollipop, chocolate, juice, spaghetti and more from each customer's shopping list.
  • Cheese and salami — sort items from a conveyor belt into the right boxes, as fast as you can.
  • Fruits and vegetables — collect fresh produce and avoid the rotten pieces.
  • Candies — fill passing cups with exactly the right sweets.
  • Weigh-in — bag the produce, put the right quantity on the scale, and watch the numbers — red means wrong.
  • Recycling — sort waste into paper, plastic, glass, battery and organic bins, and learn a little about recycling on the way.
  • Toy catcher — drive the claw machine, hit the red button, and grab a toy.
  • Delivery — drive a delivery truck down a five-lane road through crazy traffic and drop off the packages.
  • Catch a thief — become a supermarket superhero, dodge the obstacles, grab the power-ups and deliver the thief to the police station.

Key features

  • 10 popular mini-games and shopping areas in one free download — variety is the whole selling point.
  • Medal achievements — bronze, silver and gold medals give children a gentle goal beyond just playing.
  • Bright, friendly presentation — colourful cartoon graphics, simple controls and cheerful sound effects, built for young players.
  • Light on your phone — at about 43 MB it is far smaller than most modern games, and it runs on Android 7.0 and later.
  • COPPA-certified for child privacy — certified compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act by PRIVO, an FTC-approved safe harbour, and committed to the Play Families policy.
  • A proven, still-growing audience — 100,000,000+ installs, 1.3 million in the last 30 days, and a top-30 casual chart place in Indonesia and India.

Pros and cons

Pros:

  • Ten genuinely different mini-games in one app, so it stays fresh longer than a single-mechanic game
  • Free, rated Everyone, COPPA-certified, no chat and no user-generated content — a safer pick for children
  • Small 43 MB download that runs on older Android phones and tablets
  • Medals and simple tasks give young players goals without pressure or fail states
  • Enormous, still-growing player base: 100,000,000+ installs and 480,000 in the last week alone

Cons:

  • Contains ads, including third-party ads that can redirect to other sites or apps — the most common complaint in recent reviews
  • A divided rating: behind the 4.0-star average sit tens of thousands of 1-star ratings, and some players report too little guidance on how each mini-game works
  • Some in-game items and features require real-money in-app purchases
  • Simple, decade-old graphics and shallow mechanics — adults looking for a real store-management simulation will find this too light
  • The developer discloses that it shares app activity, app info and device identifiers with third parties, so check the privacy policy if that matters to you

Pricing and safety notes

Supermarket Game is free to download on Google Play. The listing states that it contains ads and offers in-app purchases, with some items and features requiring real money, so set up purchase approval in Google Play before handing the phone to a child. Data is encrypted in transit and you can request that your data be deleted, according to the Play listing's data-safety section.

The honest summary is this: the game itself is gentle, colourful and safe for children, and the COPPA badge is a real reassurance. The catches are commercial, not content-based — ads that interrupt play and can link out to other apps, and purchases behind some items. With purchase approval switched on, those are manageable, and the game underneath is a cheerful little supermarket that has kept children entertained for a decade.

If your child likes Bubadu's style, our Bubbu & Mimmi World review covers the same developer's virtual-pet game — pet care, salons and mini-games, with the same COPPA badge and the same need for purchase approval first.

Final verdict

Supermarket Game earns its second wind. Ten mini-games, a tiny download, an Everyone rating and a COPPA badge make it one of the easier yeses among trending games for younger players, and 480,000 new installs in a single week show plenty of families agree. The ads are a genuine annoyance, and the divided rating means you should try it free first rather than expecting a flawless experience. But for a free, light, child-friendly game with real variety, it is a solid download — from the official Play Store link below, never from a third-party APK site.
