Ginobili, Markkanen Join Dunk City Dynasty

Dunk City Dynasty has kicked off Season 10 Part 1 on iOS and Android, adding NBA stars Manu Ginóbili and Lauri Markkanen to its roster, NetEase's NBA-licensed 3v3 streetball game.

Why this matters: a new season headlined by a four-time NBA champion gives existing players a fresh reason to return — and the Halloween events running alongside it hand out free rewards too.

Dunk City Dynasty Season 10: the key facts

  • Season: Season 10 Part 1, live now on iOS and Android.
  • New players: Manu Ginóbili leads the season, joined by Finnish All-Star Lauri Markkanen.
  • Ginóbili: played for the San Antonio Spurs from 2002 to 2018 and won four championships there; the game calls him the "Argentine Soul".
  • Markkanen: brings a forward's size with a shooter's touch, so he fills a different role from Ginóbili.
  • Game: NetEase's NBA-licensed 3v3 streetball game, which adds new names most seasons — Season 9 opened with Anthony Edwards and Mikal Bridges.
  • Halloween Trip: runs from October 21 to November 10, 2026, with quests around an event map for free rewards.
  • Halloween outfits: the Triumph Reaper outfit lives in the new Costume Supply Crate, while the Pixel Pumpkin & Full Moon set is tied to the new Treasure Hunt Event.
  • Also running: Season Journey, Party Hustle, the Party Dual Pass and Gifts of Gratitude carry on alongside the new season.

Why it matters

Ginóbili made his name coming off the bench as one of the league's best-known sixth men, famous for drives and passes nobody saw coming — so putting him at the front of a season is a deliberate nod to long-time basketball fans. Markkanen's shooting gives the season a second, very different option rather than a like-for-like repeat. And with Halloween Trip offering free rewards until November 10, there is something to play for even before touching the new outfits.

What to watch next

Watch for the next part of Season 10 and whether more NBA names join the roster. For more gaming news, see our Legends of Amberland II mobile story and browse the Top 100 apps page.
