Supercell Hit by Cyberattack, Players Safe

Supercell has confirmed it was hit by a cyberattack that accessed an internal platform used to host company meetings and presentations. The Clash of Clans developer announced the incident on October 21, 2026, saying it was targeted by multiple cyberattacks last week — and that, based on its investigations so far, no player data was affected.

Why this matters: If you play Clash of Clans, Clash Royale, Brawl Stars or Hay Day, Supercell says your account, purchases and progress are not affected. The real risk is follow-up phishing: the stolen material could be used to impersonate Supercell staff, so treat any unexpected message claiming to be from Supercell with extra caution.

Supercell cyberattack: the key facts

  • What happened: An unauthorised party accessed a platform Supercell used to host meetings and presentations, and copied recorded videos from it
  • Announced: October 21, 2026, after multiple cyberattacks against the company the previous week
  • Player data: Supercell believes no player data was affected, based on its investigations so far
  • What was accessed: Videos featuring current and former employees, guests and partners from internal events, which may include their names, images and voices; the names and email addresses of the platform's users were also accessed
  • Who is most affected: Anyone who spoke or presented at a recorded Supercell event is, in the company's words, “very likely” affected
  • Response: Supercell blocked further access, took the service offline, and began investigating with the platform and its security experts
  • Authorities notified: A police report has been filed, a notification submitted under the EU Cyber Resilience Act, and the Data Protection Ombudsman notified
  • Scam warning: Supercell warns the material could be used for targeted phishing and impersonation — be cautious with unexpected messages, calls or video calls claiming to come from Supercell, especially if they ask for money, passwords, access or files
  • Contact: People who have worked with Supercell can email incidents-info@supercell.com for more information

What to watch next

The investigation is still ongoing, so Supercell says it cannot share further details yet. Watch for its follow-up statement, which should confirm whether the “no player data affected” finding holds once the investigation closes. In the meantime, the practical step for players is simple: never hand over a password, code or payment in response to an unsolicited message, and verify anything suspicious through a channel you already trust.
