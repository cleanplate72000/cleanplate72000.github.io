On Raft Review: Bermuda Survival (2026)

Quick verdict — 4.5 / 5. On Raft is October 2026's surprise adventure hit: a Bermuda Triangle survival story with real momentum — 160,000 installs in its first weeks, a 4.8-star rating, and a number 2 spot among America's top new free adventure games. You play Riley Ashford, stranded after a helicopter crash, gathering resources, crafting weapons and building a raft to island-hop your way home. The trade-offs are just as real: reviewers complain loudly about frequent ads, and the game is a hefty 281 MB download. But if you want a story-driven survival adventure that is free and family-safe, this is one of the strongest new finds of the month.

On Raft, from developer 24 HIT Riga SIA, is a survival adventure game with a proper story. Riley Ashford and her brother Jake are treasure hunters exploring the Bermuda Triangle with their pilot, Marcus Reed, when a powerful magnetic storm strikes their helicopter. Jake disappears in the crash, Riley and Marcus are separated, and Riley wakes up alone on a strange island with no way back. That setup — a missing brother, a vanished pilot, and a secret that has kept these islands isolated for generations — is what lifts On Raft above the usual resource-grinding survival games.

On its official Google Play listing it holds 4.8 stars from nearly 7,000 ratings, with Play showing 100,000+ installs; independent tracker AppBrain puts the total higher, at about 160,000 installs, all of them in the last 30 days — roughly 10,000 installs a day — and ranks it number 2 in Top New Free Adventure in the United States, number 3 in Canada and France, and number 4 in Germany. It has an Everyone content rating, is about 281 MB, and its version 1.0.0 was last updated on 23 October 2026. It has been on Google Play only since late September 2026, so this is a genuinely new breakout, not a slow burner.

The key art sells the fantasy in one image: Riley, alone on a raft, holding off a shark with a spear. The game itself is calmer than this moment — mostly gathering, crafting and exploring — but the danger is never far away.

Key features

  • A real story: search for Jake and Marcus while uncovering traces of an ancient tribe and the mysterious Long Fire behind the islands' isolation.
  • Explore mysterious islands: break objects, gather valuable resources, and unlock new materials as you push deeper into the Triangle.
  • Upgrade your tools: stronger tools destroy tougher obstacles, opening up new resources and new areas.
  • Craft powerful weapons: build and improve weapons to face the dangerous enemies lurking across the islands.
  • Build and upgrade your raft: your raft is your way between islands — improve it to reach further, and defend it when hostile rafts and enemy crews attack.
  • Combat on land and sea: defeat enemies in island fights and fend off hostile crews when you try to reach new islands.
  • Auto travel: reviewers single out the auto-travel feature for praise — the game keeps moving without constant babysitting.

Survival is the day-to-day loop: explore the island, break objects, gather resources, and put everything you find to use. The crashed helicopter wreck is a reminder of how Riley got here.

Who is it for?

This is a game for players who like survival with a destination. If you enjoy gathering and crafting but want a story pulling you forward — a brother to find, a mystery to solve — On Raft gives you that structure, and its early reviewers praise exactly that: one October 2026 reviewer calls out the great storyline and awesome graphics, and another loves the dialogue and the auto travel. It pairs naturally with other action-led new finds, like our Tank Busters review, if you want something faster between survival sessions. If, on the other hand, you hate adverts, be warned: several Play reviewers complain about forced ads, with one saying they are the main reason to avoid the game, and another reporting that destroying resources takes too long because upgrades are gated behind story progress. One reviewer also reported their phone getting extremely hot during play — worth knowing if your handset already runs warm. The honest picture is a very good young game wrapped in a very aggressive free-to-play monetisation layer.

Building your raft is the second half of the game: upgrade it piece by piece, and it carries you to the next island — and the next piece of the story.

Pros
  • A genuine story — a missing brother, an ancient tribe, and a Bermuda Triangle mystery
  • Very strong launch: 4.8 stars, 160,000 installs in weeks, number 2 new free Adventure in the US
  • Everyone rating, free to download, and also available on iPhone
  • Satisfying loop: explore, gather, upgrade tools, craft weapons, build your raft
  • Reviewers praise the storyline, graphics, dialogue and auto travel
Cons
  • Frequent ads are the top complaint in Play reviews
  • Large download at about 281 MB — check your storage first
  • Upgrades are gated behind story progress, so you cannot rush ahead
  • One reviewer reported serious phone overheating during play
  • Very young game (version 1.0.0) — expect rough edges and balance changes

Pricing

On Raft is free to download on Google Play, and it contains ads — the listing is upfront about that, and reviewers confirm the ads are frequent. In-app purchases run from $0.99 to $99.99 per item, including ad-free packages mentioned by reviewers for players who want to pay to skip the interruptions. The main cost of playing is patience: the ad breaks, the 281 MB download, and the story-gated upgrade pace.

Download On Raft on Google Play

The bottom line: judged on what it actually is — a story-driven survival adventure with a memorable setup, a satisfying gather-and-craft loop, and breakout momentum most new games never get — On Raft earns its October 2026 buzz. Its rating is strong, its growth is fast, and its Everyone rating makes it an easy recommendation. Just go in with eyes open about the ads and the 281 MB size, and you will get one of the best new adventure games on Play right now.
