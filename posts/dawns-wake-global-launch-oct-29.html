Dawn's Wake Global Launch Set for Oct 29

Dawn's Wake, the multidimensional fantasy RPG from Neptune and developer Han-Squirrel Studio, launches worldwide on October 29, 2026, following an earlier closed beta test in the Philippines and pre-registration in selected markets.

Why this matters: it arrives on a busy launch day — the same day as Monster Hunter Outlanders, according to the announcement — and mixes tactical, choice-driven storytelling with idle-friendly progression, so it suits both careful planners and shorter play sessions.

Dawn's Wake: the key facts

  • Release date: October 29, 2026, worldwide.
  • Publisher: Neptune; developer: Han-Squirrel Studio.
  • Platforms: mobile, with a PC version also listed on Steam.
  • Pre-registration: offered through the App Store and Google Play in selected markets ahead of launch.
  • The story: you play a king stripped of his memories and sealed away by malevolent forces, travelling across multiple realms to break the cursed seals, uncover hidden conspiracies and reclaim his throne.
  • Choices matter: your decisions influence how the adventure unfolds, and heroes from different lands join you, each with their own history and connection to your journey.
  • Strategic combat: battles reward studying enemy movements and the battlefield — a single well-chosen move can decide the outcome, rather than brute force alone.
  • Easier progression: automated monster hunting gathers resources with less manual grinding, and an offline reward system banks resources for when you return.
  • Beyond battle: hunting, fishing, cooking, blacksmithing and mount training offer other ways to spend time in the game's world.

Why it matters

Many mobile RPGs ask you to grind through the same battles to progress. Dawn's Wake pairs a genuinely tactical combat system — where positioning and timing count — with automated hunting and offline rewards, so progress continues even when you step away. Add a story that reacts to your choices, and it is a launch worth watching for RPG fans who want depth without a daily grind.

What to watch next

Watch for the October 29 launch on the App Store, Google Play and Steam, and whether the global version matches the pre-registration build in content and rewards. Dawn's Wake is a fantasy game with combat, so younger players should check the store age rating first.
