Roco Kingdom Quack Quack Beta Starts Oct 27

Roco Kingdom, the open-world creature-collecting adventure from Morefun Studios, opens its global Quack Quack beta test on 27 October 2026 on PC, Android and iOS. It is the franchise's first worldwide test: the series has been running in China since July 2010, and this global version is heading for a full launch on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, PC and mobile in 2027.

Roco Kingdom Quack Quack beta: the key facts

  • Beta start date: 27 October 2026, on PC, Android and iOS mobile devices.
  • Who can play: players check their beta status on the official site using a Level Infinite Pass; access is rolling out in waves, so players not approved yet may still be approved later, and pre-registration for “Lucky Drops” test access stays open while the beta runs.
  • The world: the colourful Continent of Carosia, home to more than 400 creatures called Jinies to catch, train and battle.
  • How it plays: open-world creature catching, playable solo, with player-versus-environment and player-versus-player options, plus cosy touches such as home decoration and farming.
  • No gacha: there is no gacha system in the game, though season passes for cosmetics are planned.
  • Beta rules: this build is in English and Japanese, has no in-game purchases, and save data created in the test will be wiped at the end.
  • Cross-play: players can use the same account on PC and mobile to try cross-play between the two versions.
  • Full launch: expected in 2027 on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, PC and mobile.

Why it matters

Creature collectors live or die on how good it feels to find the next catch, and Roco Kingdom arrives with a huge roster — more than 400 Jinies — plus a no-gacha promise that sets it apart from many mobile collectors. A cross-play beta on both PC and phones also means players can test whether the game works as a proper open-world adventure rather than a phone-only spin-off, before the full console and PC launch in 2027.

What to watch next

Watch for beta access rolling out to more players, early impressions of the catching and battling loop, and any word on when the full 2027 launch lands. For another game landing this week, see our Hyphen mobile launch story and browse the Top 100 apps page.
