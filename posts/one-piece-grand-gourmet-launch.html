ONE PIECE: Grand Gourmet Launches on Mobile and PC

Bandai Namco and Kairosoft — the studio behind Game Dev Story — have released ONE PIECE: Grand Gourmet, a restaurant management game set in the world of One Piece. It is out now on iOS, Android, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2 and PC via Steam.

Why this matters: Instead of another One Piece battler, this is a cosy management sim: you run a floating restaurant with Sanji, cook dishes from the series and serve more than 400 characters. It is one of the most unexpected big-name mobile launches of the year.

ONE PIECE: Grand Gourmet: key facts

  • Out now: October 23, 2026 on iOS, Android, PC via Steam, Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2
  • Developer: Kairosoft; publisher: Bandai Namco Entertainment
  • The setting: the Baratie Number Two, a floating restaurant run with Luffy and the Straw Hat Crew
  • Gameplay: gather ingredients, cook dishes with Sanji, build set menus and decorate the restaurant
  • Characters: more than 400 One Piece characters appear as staff and customers, in pixel-art style
  • Customisation: over 200 decorative items, from Whole Cake Island to Egghead themes

What to watch next

Early Steam players have praised the pixel art and the sheer number of dishes and characters, while flagging a fiddly interface — the developers have already shipped a first patch addressing some of those complaints. Source: DayOne, "Diner Management 'ONE PIECE: Grand Gourmet' Out Now" (published October 23, 2026), corroborated by TwistedVoxel and ixbt.games. Details as reported there; dates and availability may change.
