Photo Video Maker with Music Review (2026)

Quick verdict — 3.0 / 5. Photo Video Maker with Music, from developer Think It Tech, packs a genuinely wide video toolkit into one free app — slideshows, pro trimming, audio tools and a large new set of AI templates — and its surge to 640,000 installs in the last 7 days shows plenty of people are trying it. But its 3.2-star rating is divided for a reason: recent reviewers complain most about heavy advertising, and at roughly 109 MB it is a large download. Worth trying for a birthday slideshow; go in expecting ads.

Photo Video Maker with Music (package com.photovideomaker.videomakerwithmusic — note that several unrelated apps share this generic name on Google Play, so check the developer name Think It Tech before installing) has been on Google Play since June 2022 and has passed 10 million installs in Play's install bracket, with AppBrain estimating about 14 million total installs. It is trending now because its growth has suddenly accelerated: AppBrain's trending list for 26 October 2026 puts it at 640,000 installs in the last 7 days, five times the week before, with 1.3 million installs in the last 30 days, and it currently ranks #1 in Top Free Video Players & Editors in India and #9 in the United States. Its rating stands at 3.2 stars from about 6,449 ratings on the live Play listing, it was updated to version 1.1.34 on 21 October 2026, Google Play rates its content Everyone, and it is free to download and contains ads. AppBrain lists the download size at about 108.9 MB and Android 7.0+ as the minimum version.

That combination — a very recent update, a sharp weekly jump, and a top chart position — is exactly why it earns a review this week. The October update is also the story of the app right now: it adds an AI image generator, an AI video generator with trending presets, new video templates and brand-new collage templates. In other words, an established slideshow maker is reinventing itself as an AI template app, and the store screenshots lead with that new identity rather than with the classic slideshow tools.

Slideshows first, pro tools second

At its core, the app does what its name promises. You pick photos or clips from your gallery, arrange them into a slideshow or short movie, add music, text, stickers and filters, and export a finished video to share. The developer lists the workflow in four steps: select photos or videos, add music, effects, text and stickers, edit with trim, merge or speed tools, then save in high quality and share. For a birthday, a wedding, a travel recap or a family slideshow, that is the whole job, and reviewers who like the app describe exactly that use: one five-star reviewer from October 2026 writes about making a family photo slideshow with soft background music that made their parents happy, while another says they finished a first project in minutes.

Beyond the basics, the toolkit is wider than most single-purpose slideshow apps. The pro tools include crop and trim, merging clips, slow motion and fast motion, reverse video, and video compression that claims to shrink file size without losing HD quality. The audio side goes further still: add music or voiceovers, trim audio, extract audio from a video, and convert video to MP3. Add a photo collage maker, a video-to-GIF maker and a watermark tool, and this is really several small editors bundled into one app — which helps explain both its appeal and its 109 MB size.

The new AI templates are the headline

The store screenshots show where the app is heading. The AI Dance Maker turns a single still photo into a dancing clip, the template library promises more than a thousand AI styles, and the AI Champion Photo feature restyles portraits into sports-themed posters. For anyone who wants a viral-style video without learning an editor, that is the pitch: pick a photo, pick a trending preset, get a finished clip. It is also the part of the app to judge most carefully. AI presets are fun, but their results vary with the photo, and the app's own screenshots are polished promotional examples rather than typical output. Try the free templates before assuming every preset will look that good.

The rest of the feature list is practical rather than flashy. Filters and effects cover cinematic looks and vintage tones, frames and overlays dress a video for an occasion, and text, stickers and emojis let you personalise a slideshow without opening a second app. Nothing here is unusual on its own; the selling point is having all of it in one place, free, without needing editing experience. The developer's own summary — easy enough for beginners, powerful enough for pros — is a fair description of the ambition, even if the rating suggests the execution does not satisfy everyone.

Why the rating is only 3.2 stars

The honest caveat is the advertising. The Play listing confirms the app contains ads, and the most recent one-star reviews are blunt about them: too many ads before you even get started, ads interrupting a task so often that you have to start over, and disappointment that the app does not match what its advertising promised. A rating histogram with roughly 3,100 five-star ratings against about 2,500 one-star ratings tells the same story — this is a divided app, loved by people whose slideshow worked first time and disliked by people who hit the ad wall. If you are patient with ads, or you only need one quick slideshow, that trade may be fine. If ads frustrate you, a cleaner editor is worth the search, even at a cost.

Two smaller caveats are worth knowing before you install. First, at about 109 MB with 97 libraries listed by AppBrain, this is a heavy app for a casual tool, so it is best downloaded on Wi-Fi with some free storage. Second, because the name is generic, double-check you are installing Think It Tech's version — package com.photovideomaker.videomakerwithmusic — and not one of the many lookalike slideshow apps with the same name.

Pros
  • Trending hard: 640,000 installs in the last 7 days, 5 times the week before, and #1 Top Free Video Players & Editors in India
  • Wide free toolkit: slideshows, trim, merge, speed controls, reverse video, compression, audio extraction and video-to-MP3
  • New AI templates, AI image and video generators and collage templates added in the 21 October 2026 update
  • Rated Everyone, free to download, works on Android 7.0 and up
  • 14 million total installs and a 10 million-plus Play install bracket show a long-lived, actively updated app
Cons
  • Divided 3.2-star rating, with recent one-star reviews complaining most about heavy, interrupting ads
  • Large download at about 109 MB for a casual slideshow tool
  • AI template results vary and store screenshots show polished promotional examples, not typical output
  • Generic name shared by many lookalike apps — easy to install the wrong one by mistake
  • Some reviewers say the app does not live up to what its own advertising promises

Pricing

Photo Video Maker with Music is free to download on Google Play, and its listing confirms it contains ads. The store page checked for this review shows no in-app purchase price range, so the free download is the honest headline — but the advertising is the real cost to weigh. If you need one slideshow for a birthday or a family occasion, try it, export your video, and judge it on that single project before keeping it. If the ads get in your way, delete it and try a different editor rather than fighting through them.

Download

Download Photo Video Maker with Music on Google Play

If you want a free, all-in-one slideshow and video maker and you can tolerate ads, Photo Video Maker with Music is worth one project: a broad toolkit, a genuinely trending AI template library, and a top chart position to back it up. If you want a cleaner, more polished editor, also read our CapCut review before you decide.
