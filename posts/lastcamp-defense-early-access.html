LastCamp: Defense Hits Mobile Early Access

LastCamp: Defense, a cute roguelike tower defence starring a marshmallow skeleton, is now playable in early access in select regions on Google Play, as highlighted by Pocket Gamer on October 26, 2026.

Why this matters: it is a tower defence with a clear, charming hook — one skeleton who never sleeps and never dies is the only one left to keep humanity's flame burning.

LastCamp: Defense: the key facts

  • Status: early access in select regions on Google Play; no full global launch date has been announced in the source.
  • Platform: Android via Google Play in those early-access regions; the source mentions no iOS version.
  • Hero: a marshmallow skeleton who never sleeps and never dies — even with his head chopped off — making him the only one qualified to keep the flame going.
  • Gameplay: build towers with coins earned from defeating endless waves of foes, then merge towers to upgrade them.
  • Progression: after surviving each wave, upgrade gear, unlock Traits and collect more heroes — including one called Chick Champion, literally a champion of chicks.
  • Boss rewards: taking down a boss or two brings randomised boons.
  • Ads: Pocket Gamer's writer says they had not encountered any ads in the early-access version yet.
  • Price: the source states no price, so none is given here.

Why it matters

Tower defence games live or die on whether one more wave feels worth playing, and LastCamp: Defense adds a roguelike loop on top of that — new gear, Traits and heroes after every wave, plus tower merging and boss boons. The Halloween-season timing helps too: an adorable skeleton defending humanity's last flame is an easy story to root for, even if the world around him is ending.

What to watch next

Watch for a wider rollout beyond the current select regions, and for whether the no-ads experience in early access survives a full launch. For more gaming news, see our Bills Must Be Paid mobile story and browse the Top 100 apps page.
