AI PDF Reader Editor Review: Summaries (2026)

Quick verdict — 3.0 / 5. AI PDF Reader & Editor, from developer BrightCode Dev, packs a genuinely useful idea into one free app: read a PDF, get an AI summary of it, ask it questions, translate it, annotate it, scan paper into it, and merge or split it — without switching apps. The catch is the crowd's verdict so far: about 3.4 stars from roughly 840 ratings, with reviews split sharply between five-star praise and one-star complaints. Try it for everyday documents, but do not hand it your passport scan or your most sensitive contract on day one.

AI PDF Reader & Editor (package com.brightcode.pdfreader) has been on Google Play since September 2026 and is currently at version 1.0.1.4, updated 12 October 2026. AppBrain's trending list for 26 October 2026 puts it at about 91,000 installs in the last 7 days — eight times the week before — and its total stands at about 520,000 installs, all of them in the last 30 days, a strikingly fast start for a two-month-old app. It has also reached #156 in the Top Free Tools chart in South Korea and #163 in Russia. Google Play rates its content Everyone, the download is a fairly large 89.63 MB, and the Play listing confirms a price of 0 USD — free to download.

That combination — a brand-new app, a sharp weekly jump, and an everyday problem — is exactly why it earns a review this week. Most people already have a PDF reader on their phone, but few readers also summarise a 40-page report, answer a question about it, and translate it. If those AI extras work, one app replaces three. The question this review has to answer honestly is whether they work well enough to trust, given that divided rating.

The headline feature is the AI summary. Point the app at a long PDF and it returns the key points, so a report, a textbook chapter or a contract can be skimmed in a minute rather than read end to end. The developer's own example shows a style guide boiled down to outfit tips — a fair demo of the idea, though a fashion article is an easier test than a legal document, where a missed clause matters. Treat summaries as a starting point: read the original before you act on anything important, especially figures, dates and obligations.

Chat with PDF is the natural partner to the summary. Instead of searching page by page, you ask a question based on the document's content and the app finds the relevant detail — useful for manuals, research papers and long reports. Translation keeps the document's layout, according to the listing, and works across supported languages, which matters for anyone reading foreign manuals or study material. The example in the listing translates a Korean document into English while keeping its formatting, which is the right test for this feature.

Beyond the AI tools, this is a full PDF toolkit. The reader offers smooth zoom and scrolling, horizontal, vertical and page-by-page viewing, in-document search, jump-to-page, rotation and a dark mode, and it opens PDFs from Files, Gmail, Drive and other apps. The editor adds highlighting, underlining, strikethrough, drawing, handwriting, comments and markup, with edits saved directly to the file. Scanning turns paper into PDFs with automatic edge detection, cropping and multi-page capture, OCR extracts text from scanned pages and images, and the organiser merges, splits, extracts, reorders, rotates and deletes pages, adds page numbers and crops margins. Signing, password protection and redaction round it out, plus converters between images, PDFs and web pages, and printing.

Key features

  • AI PDF summary — turn long PDFs into clear key points for reports, books, contracts and study notes.
  • Chat with PDF — ask questions about a document and find details without searching page by page.
  • PDF translation — translate documents into supported languages while keeping the original layout.
  • Fast PDF reader — smooth zoom and scrolling, search, jump to any page, rotation and dark mode.
  • Editor and annotation — highlight, underline, strikethrough, draw, handwrite and comment, saved to the file.
  • Scan to PDF and OCR — scan documents with edge detection, then extract text from scanned pages and images.
  • Merge, split and organise — combine files, split them, extract pages, reorder, rotate, delete and add page numbers.
  • Sign and protect — add signatures, sign contracts, add or remove password protection, and redact sensitive text.
  • Converters and file manager — image to PDF, PDF to image, web page to PDF, printing, recent files, favourites and a recycle bin.
  • Everyone rating — a family-safe utility with no age-gated content.

Pros and cons

Pros
  • Trending fast: about 91,000 installs in 7 days, eight times the week before, per AppBrain's 26 October 2026 list
  • One app for reading, summarising, chatting with, translating, editing, scanning, signing and converting PDFs
  • AI summary and chat-with-PDF genuinely save time on long reports, manuals and study notes
  • Translation keeps the document's layout, which most quick translators do not
  • Free to download, Everyone rating, and updated recently (12 October 2026)
Cons
  • Divided rating: about 3.4 stars from roughly 840 ratings, with AppBrain's histogram showing about half five-star and about three in ten one-star
  • One early reviewer called it a scam attempt — unproven, but a warning to test it on unimportant files first
  • A hefty 89.63 MB download and 24 permissions, per AppBrain — check what it asks for before granting access
  • The listing itself advertises scanning ID cards and passports: think twice before scanning identity documents into any new app
  • AI summaries can miss or simplify detail, so never act on a summary of a contract without reading the original

Pricing

AI PDF Reader & Editor is free to download on Google Play, with the listing confirming a price of 0 USD. The listing data checked for this review did not confirm an in-app purchase price range, so judge any paid offer inside the app on its own merits before accepting it — especially anything that unlocks the AI features, which are the most likely thing to sit behind a paywall. For a free utility, the honest test is simple: use the reader, the summary and the translator on ordinary documents for a week, and only then decide whether anything inside it is worth paying for.

Download

Download AI PDF Reader & Editor on Google Play

If you read long PDFs every day and want AI summaries, translation and editing in one place, AI PDF Reader & Editor is worth a careful try — free, fast-growing and genuinely ambitious, but with a rating that says to test it before you trust it.
