Twist Colors Review: Cube Smash Fun (2026)

Quick verdict — 4.5 / 5. Twist Colors: Cube Smash is a bright, relaxing voxel puzzle: fire colour-matched blocks at a 3D sculpture and watch it crumble piece by piece. It is easy to pick up, genuinely satisfying, and free — just expect a chunky download, ads, and the odd level that leans on boosters.

Twist Colors: Cube Smash, from developer Nova Games Technology Limited, is one of October 2026's fast climbers on Google Play. It holds 4.9 stars from more than 7,400 ratings, has passed 100,000 installs, and is rated Everyone — and on AppBrain's trending free Casual chart for 25 October 2026 it picked up around 25,000 installs in seven days, three times the week before. For a game built around smashing colourful cubes, that is a fast start, and the reason it earns a review here this week.

The idea is immediately readable. Each level shows you a 3D sculpture built from tiny coloured cubes — a rainbow hot-air balloon with pink flowers, a chunky red squirrel, a green dinosaur, a pair of pink heart-patterned boots — and below it a tray of numbered colour blocks. You fire the matching colour at the sculpture to chip its pieces away, clearing section after section until nothing is left. There is no timer pressure in the levels shown, and the developer pitches it as a relaxing puzzle: rotate, observe, plan your shots, and chase the biggest combos you can.

How it plays

The loop is simple but not mindless. The tray at the bottom holds blocks in different colours, each stamped with a number, and the sculpture above is made of matching colours. Fire the right colour at the right part and that chunk breaks apart; fire carelessly and you burn through your blocks without clearing the shape. The screenshots show the same structure at very different stages — level 8's squirrel, level 75's boots, level 105's balloon, level 131's dinosaur — so the variety comes from the sculptures themselves, not from new rules every few levels. Some trays also show locked slots and booster buttons, which is where the strategy creeps in: on harder sculptures, the order you clear colours in decides whether you finish cleanly or stall with the wrong colours left.

That "which colour first" choice is the whole puzzle. The early levels are gentle, letting you enjoy the simple pleasure of a voxel shape collapsing. Later ones pack many more colours into one sculpture, so you have to read the shape before you start firing. It is a calm, tactile kind of puzzling — closer to a satisfying clear-out than to a frantic arcade game — and the chunky voxel art keeps every level looking cheerful even when it gets tricky.

Key features

  • Voxel sculpture levels: every level is a different 3D shape to smash — animals, objects, balloons and more.
  • Colour-matching shooting: pick the matching colour block from the tray and fire it at the sculpture to break pieces off.
  • Hundreds of levels: the store listing promises hundreds of carefully designed puzzles, with difficulty rising as you climb.
  • Combos and chain reactions: smart, well-planned shots trigger satisfying chain clears and higher scores.
  • No time pressure: play at your own pace, with smooth animations and bright, colourful visuals.
  • Boosters for stuck levels: undo-style tools and extra blocks sit below the tray for the levels that fight back.

Pros and cons

Pros: instantly understandable; genuinely relaxing with no timer; charming voxel sculptures that change every level; strong player rating (4.9 stars); free to play; suitable for all ages.

Cons: a large download at around 376 MB; contains ads; at least one reviewer reports a mid-game level that felt impossible without boosters, so later levels may lean on tool top-ups; the Play data-safety section says the app may collect location and device IDs, data is not encrypted, and data cannot be deleted — worth knowing before installing.

Pricing

Twist Colors: Cube Smash is free to download and play on Google Play, with ads. The store listing does not show an in-app purchase price range, and no subscription is advertised — the free game plus ads is the deal as listed. If you enjoy puzzle games and do not mind ads, there is nothing to pay to get started.

Who should download it

This is a good pick if you like calm, visual puzzle games — the sort of game you open for ten minutes and put down feeling pleasantly unfrazzled. Fans of colour-sorting and block-smashing games will feel at home immediately, and the Everyone rating makes it an easy recommendation for younger players too, with the usual caveat about ads. If you dislike large downloads or games that eventually nudge you toward boosters, the 376 MB size and the reported mid-game difficulty spike are worth weighing first.

If you like this kind of satisfying clear-out puzzler, you may also enjoy our BlockFlow review, another colourful sorting game with a strong player rating.

Download Twist Colors: Cube Smash on Google Play

Bottom line: a cheerful, easy-to-learn voxel smasher with a big, happy player base behind it. Download it for the relaxing sculpture clears, stay for the combos — just keep an eye on the storage space and the ads.
