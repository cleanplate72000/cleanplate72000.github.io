Fun Soccer Goal Roll Review: Curl It (2026)

Quick verdict — 4.0 / 5. Fun Soccer Goal Roll turns football into a drawing puzzle: sketch a path, bend it round the defenders, and watch a cartoon striker finish it. It is at its best in short, satisfying bursts, and its recent chart climb is real. It is an honest 4.0, not a 5, because it is a very large download for such a simple idea, and its player ratings are thinner and more mixed than its install count suggests — but if you like a quick game you can play one-handed, it delivers.

Fun Soccer Goal Roll, from developer nsxxkitten, is a Q-version cartoon football game with one clear idea, stated right on its Google Play page: swipe your finger to draw the trajectory of the ball, control the passing and shooting angles, avoid the opponent's defence, and score brilliant goals. It has been on Google Play since January 2026, and its recent momentum is striking — 1,000,000+ installs in total, with around 120,000 of those in the last 30 days at the last AppBrain snapshot (20 October 2026), and a place in the Top 100 free casual games, including #86 in Norway. For a game built on a single swipe, that is real traction — and the reason it earns a review here this week.

On ratings, the honest picture is mixed rather than glowing. AppBrain's 20 October 2026 snapshot put it at 4.15 stars from 260 ratings — a positive average, but a small rating base for a game with a million installs, so a handful of new reviews can move it. Recent Play reviews show the same split: some players call it simply fun and good, while at least one recent reviewer dismissed it outright. Treat the score as promising but thin, not as a settled verdict, and judge it by the gameplay below.

How it plays

The loop is easy to learn in seconds. Your striker has the ball, defenders stand between you and the goal, and your finger is the playmaker. Draw a line and the ball follows it: a straight dash for a clean breakaway, a wide curl round a defender, or a short layoff to open up space. The screenshots show exactly why this works — the white guide line arcs across the pitch, bends round the back line, and finishes in the top corner, so you can see the plan before you commit to it.

That visibility is the game's real skill. Plenty of mobile football games ask you to time a tap and hope; here, you plan the route. A lazy straight line runs straight into a defender, while a patient curl that starts wide and bends late beats the same defence cleanly. It rewards thinking one pass ahead, and it makes a good goal feel earned rather than lucky — you drew that shape, and the shape is what scored.

Levels, shots and skins

Fun Soccer Goal Roll is built for single-player sessions rather than long matches. The Play listing promises numerous levels, fancy shots and quirky player movements, and the screenshots back that up: one shot glows with a power-up effect, another bends in a full loop before dropping into the net, and a later level adds moving drone obstacles and marked blocks in front of the goal that force you to thread the ball through a gap rather than just curling round a man.

Between levels, progression comes from unlocking different skins of star players and trying new shot styles, all set to what the listing calls passionate background music. The cartoon look — small, round, brightly kitted players on a vivid green pitch — keeps the whole thing light and family-friendly, and the single-player structure means there is no pressure to keep up with other players online. You can play one level while waiting for a bus, or work through a run of levels in an evening, and either way the game fits the gap.

Key features

  • Draw-the-path controls — swipe to set passes and shots, then watch the ball follow your line
  • Curling and bending shots — bend the ball round defenders and into the corners
  • Numerous single-player levels — escalating stages, from clean breakaways to tight, obstacle-filled pitches
  • Star player skins — unlock different looks for your cartoon players as you progress
  • Fancy shots and quirky celebrations — powered-up strikes and playful player animations
  • Cartoon, family-friendly style — bright Q-version players, colourful pitches and simple controls

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Drawing your own shot path is genuinely satisfying and different from tap-to-shoot football games
  • Simple to learn — one finger, one line — with real depth in curl, angle and timing
  • Strong recent traction: 1,000,000+ installs, around 120,000 in the last 30 days
  • Family-friendly cartoon style and single-player levels, with no online pressure
  • Quick sessions — one level at a time, ideal for short breaks

Cons

  • Large download at around 303 MB — a lot of storage for a simple drawing game
  • Thin rating base: only around 260 ratings behind a 4.15-star average, with mixed recent reviews
  • Medium Maturity rating — check it suits younger players in your household before installing
  • Play's data-safety listing says collected data cannot be deleted, and it collects device IDs
  • The core idea may wear thin over long sessions — it shines brightest in short bursts

The honest caveats

The first caveat is size. At around 303 MB, Fun Soccer Goal Roll is a heavy download for a game whose whole mechanic is drawing a line on a pitch, so check your storage first and download over Wi-Fi if your data plan is tight. The second is the rating base: a million installs with only a few hundred ratings means the average is not yet a reliable guide, and recent reviewers disagree about it, so go in expecting a fun toy rather than a guaranteed classic.

The third caveat is privacy, taken straight from the Play listing's data-safety section: the developer declares no data shared with third parties, but it may collect device or other IDs, data is encrypted in transit, and — unusually — data cannot be deleted on request. That is worth knowing before you install, even though the game itself is a harmless cartoon football puzzler. The game was last updated on 15 October 2026, with bug fixes and interface optimisation, so it is actively maintained rather than abandoned.

Pricing

Fun Soccer Goal Roll is free to download on Google Play. The listing checked for this review shows a price of 0 USD; treat any paid offer you meet inside the app on its own merits and check it before accepting. It carries a Medium Maturity content rating, and the download is about 303 MB — sizeable, so Wi-Fi is the sensible choice. It is a casual game for football fans and puzzle players alike, not a full football simulation, and it is best judged on that basis.

Download

If you like a sports game where the cleverest line beats the hardest shot, Fun Soccer Goal Roll is worth a try: the drawn-path mechanic is fresh, the curling goals feel earned, and its chart climb shows it is finding an audience fast. Just go in knowing the download is large and the ratings are still thin.
