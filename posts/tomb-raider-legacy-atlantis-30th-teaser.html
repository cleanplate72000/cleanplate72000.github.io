New Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis Teaser

Publisher Amazon Games and developers Crystal Dynamics and Flying Wild Hog have released a new 30th anniversary teaser trailer for Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, celebrating three decades of Lara Croft's adventures.

Why this matters: Legacy of Atlantis reimagines Lara Croft's original adventure, so this anniversary teaser is the latest checkpoint on the road to a 2027 release that both long-time fans and newcomers have been watching.

Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis teaser: the key facts

  • Game: Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, a reimagining of Lara Croft's original adventure.
  • Publisher: Amazon Games.
  • Developers: Crystal Dynamics and Flying Wild Hog.
  • What is new: a new teaser trailer released to mark Tomb Raider's 30th anniversary, reported by Gematsu on October 25, 2026.
  • Release date: February 12, 2027.
  • Platforms: PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC via Steam.
  • What the teaser is not: it does not announce a new release date or new platforms — the February 12, 2027 date and platform list stand as previously announced.

Why it matters

Anniversary trailers are usually about nostalgia, but this one also serves as a progress marker: with the launch now under four months away, each new look at Legacy of Atlantis helps players judge how the original adventure is being rebuilt for modern platforms. A 30th anniversary is a rare moment for a series to speak to two audiences at once — returning players who know the original by heart, and newcomers meeting Lara Croft for the first time.

What to watch next
