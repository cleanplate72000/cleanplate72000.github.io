Home Clean Review: ASMR Makeover (2026)

Quick verdict — 4.0 / 5. Home Clean: Makeover ASMR is one of the most satisfying cleaning games on Play right now: pressure-washing a filthy pool, spraying down a grimy fridge and vacuuming a stained sofa are all oddly calming, and watching a wreck of a room turn spotless never gets old. It loses a star because the ads are genuinely heavy — several players report an ad before the game even loads and more during every level — and because the 449 MB download is chunky for a casual game.

Home Clean: Makeover ASMR, from developer BLACKGEMS GLOBAL JOINT STOCK COMPANY, is a relaxing cleaning and home makeover game built on a simple, satisfying loop: take a filthy space, scrub it, spray it, vacuum it and polish it until it shines, then move on to the next room. The official Play description promises deep cleaning of every space — scrubbing stains, wiping surfaces, vacuuming dust and washing away dirt — plus a story-driven journey through locations including the kitchen, the living room and the garden, all wrapped in soothing ASMR sound effects designed to make every action feel calming.

Why review it now? Because it is climbing fast. AppBrain's tracking puts Home Clean at about 210,000 installs in the last 7 days and roughly 950,000 installs in the last 30 days — about 32,000 new players a day — taking it to roughly 6.2 million installs in total since it launched in April 2026. On the live Play listing it holds 4.55 stars from about 9,000 ratings, with a 5,000,000+ install bracket, and it has reached number 31 in India's Top Free Simulation chart and the top 200 in Turkey and Brazil. A cleaning game that new pulling in a million installs a month is exactly the kind of quiet breakout worth a closer look.

What you actually do

Each level hands you a mess and a set of tools. You pressure-wash algae off a pool, spray cleaner over a fridge caked in grime, run a vacuum over a stained blue sofa, and scrub a kitchen sink that looks like it has not been touched in years. The controls are tap-and-drag simple: pick the right tool, work over the dirty patch, and watch the grime visibly lift. There is no timer breathing down your neck in the levels shown, which is part of the appeal — this is a game for unwinding, not for chasing a high score.

As you progress, new areas unlock. The Play description lists the kitchen, the living room and the garden among the locations, and the makeover side of the game means you are not just cleaning — you are restoring rooms to something worth living in. The before-and-after payoff is the whole point: a sink split down the middle, filthy green water on top and gleaming steel below, sells the fantasy better than any score screen.

Key features

  • Plenty of cleaning challenges: pools, fridges, sofas, sinks and whole rooms, each with its own mess and tools.
  • ASMR audio: spraying, scrubbing and vacuuming sounds designed to be calming rather than noisy.
  • Cleaning plus makeover: it is not just scrubbing — rooms are restored and transformed as you finish them.
  • New areas as you progress: kitchen, living room, garden and more unlock over time.
  • Simple controls: tap-and-drag tools, no complicated menus, easy to pick up for a few minutes at a time.

Pros and cons

Pros
  • Genuinely satisfying cleaning loop with visible before-and-after results
  • Calming ASMR sounds and no-rush pacing
  • Lots of variety — pools, fridges, sofas, sinks and full rooms
  • Strong traction: 4.55 stars and about a million installs a month
  • Rated Everyone, so it is fine for older children too
Cons
  • Ads are heavy — players report ads before the game loads and several during each level
  • 449 MB download is large for a casual game
  • Some levels feature cartoon bugs and heavy grime that may put off younger players
  • In-app purchases are offered but prices are not shown on the Play listing checked
  • Collects location, app info and device ID data, per the Play data-safety section

Pricing and data

Home Clean is free to download and play, with ads and in-app purchases. The Play listing checked does not display the in-app purchase prices, so check the in-game shop before spending — and note that several players say there is a paid option to remove ads, which is worth knowing given how often ads appear. On data, the Play listing says the app may collect location, app info and performance data, and device or other IDs, and that data is encrypted in transit. If you are privacy-conscious, that is worth a glance before installing.

One honest warning from the player reviews: the ads are the game's biggest complaint by a wide margin. One highly-rated review says an ad pops up before the game even loads, another says there are two ads during every level, and a third calls the game unplayable without the paid ad-removal option. Treat those as player reports rather than verified facts, but the pattern is consistent enough that you should expect frequent ad breaks. If ads ruin a relaxing game for you — and in a relaxation game they rather defeat the purpose — this is the thing to weigh before downloading.

Who should download it?

If you like the quiet satisfaction of cleaning games — the same crowd that enjoys Tidy Puzzle — Home Clean is an easy recommendation: the cleaning feels good, the makeovers are rewarding, and the ASMR angle is real rather than a label slapped on the store page. If you hate ads, play in short bursts, or wait for a sale on the ad-removal option. And if your phone is short on storage, remember the 449 MB download before you tap install.

Download Home Clean: Makeover ASMR on Google Play

Bottom line: a satisfying, good-looking cleaning game with a real breakout behind it — just go in expecting ads, and you will enjoy the scrubbing a lot more.
