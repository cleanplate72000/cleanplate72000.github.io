Guitar Tuner & Metronome Review (2026)

Quick verdict — 4.0 / 5. Guitar Tuner & Metronome packs three practice tools into one free app — a chromatic tuner, an adjustable metronome and a chord library — and its chart climb is genuinely fast. It is Everyone-rated and easy to pick up, but expect ads, and remember its rating base is still small and moving.

Guitar Tuner & Metronome, from developer Hydra Global Ltd., arrived on Google Play in August 2026 and has already reached about 130,000 installs — roughly 120,000 of them in the last 30 days, averaging around 3,900 installs a day according to AppBrain data updated 24 October 2026. It is ranked #2 in Top New Free Music & Audio in Canada, Italy and Australia, #3 in the United Kingdom and France, and #6 in the United States. For a plain practice utility with no big brand behind it, that is a strikingly fast start — and the reason it earns a review here this week.

The pitch is simple: instead of juggling a separate tuner app, a metronome app and a chord book, you open one app before you practise. The store listing calls it "Guitar Tuner: Chromatic & Metronome" and promises precision tuning, rhythm practice and chord learning for beginners and experienced musicians alike, across acoustic and electric guitar, bass, ukulele and other string instruments.

How the tuner works

Open the tuner, play a string, and the app listens through your phone's microphone and shows the note, how sharp or flat it is, and whether you are in tune. The listing promises fast, accurate tuning with real-time note and pitch detection, an easy-to-use interface and support for standard guitar tuning. The screenshots go further: the tuner screen shows a standard 6-string layout (E, A, D, G, B, E), an Auto mode, and claims of 24 instruments and 100+ tunings.

Customisation is a real selling point here. One screenshot shows a Custom screen with a 440.0 Hz reference pitch, saved tunings including Open G, Drop D (bass) and Open A, and individual string notes you can add or replace. That matters: a folk player who lives in open tunings and a rock player who drops to D do not want to fight the same fixed preset, and this app at least shows it understands that.

Metronome and chord library

The metronome offers an adjustable BPM, tap tempo and multiple rhythm patterns, according to the listing — the three controls that actually matter when you are trying to hold a steady tempo rather than just hear a click. Tap tempo is the useful one for beginners: tap along to a song and the metronome matches your speed, instead of making you guess a number.

The chord side covers major, minor, 7th and barre chords and more, with clear chord diagrams and finger-placement guides, plus a wider chord library for looking up shapes quickly. The tools menu in the screenshots presents this as two separate doors — "Learning chords" for working through chords, and "Chords library" for reference — which is a sensible split for a practice session: learn first, look up later.

Why it is trending

Guitar Tuner & Metronome is trending because it does one obvious job well and bundles the two tools every guitarist reaches for next. Tuners are a crowded category on Google Play, so standing out takes either a famous name or a fast-growing chart run — and this app has the second. About 120,000 of its 130,000 installs arrived in the last month, and AppBrain tags it "New, Growing fast, Loved by users" with a Top 10 chart showing.

It also helps that the app asks for nothing up front: it is free, it is rated Everyone, and its core promise — tune, keep time, learn chords — needs no account or explanation. A beginner picking up a guitar for the first time can be tuning within a minute of installing, which is exactly the kind of low-friction start that turns into word of mouth.

Key features

  • Chromatic guitar tuner — fast, accurate tuning with real-time note and pitch detection via the microphone.
  • Standard and custom tunings — standard guitar tuning plus custom presets such as Open G, Drop D and Open A, with a 440 Hz reference.
  • 24 instruments and 100+ tunings — claimed in the app's own screenshots, covering guitar, bass, ukulele and other string instruments.
  • Adjustable metronome — adjustable BPM, tap tempo and multiple rhythm patterns for steady practice.
  • Chord learning — major, minor, 7th, barre and more, with clear diagrams and finger-placement guides.
  • Chord library — a reference library for finding chord shapes quickly mid-practice.
  • Everyone rating — Google Play rates the app's content as suitable for everyone.
  • Free to download — no up-front price, with ads supporting the free version.

Pros and cons

Pros
  • Very fast riser: about 130,000 installs, 120,000 in the last 30 days, #2 Top New Free Music & Audio in several countries
  • Three genuinely useful tools in one app — tuner, metronome and chord library
  • Custom tunings and a 440 Hz reference go beyond a bare-bones tuner
  • Free, Everyone-rated, and quick to start — no account needed to tune
  • Clear, beginner-friendly layout with diagrams and an Auto tuning mode
Cons
  • Contains ads, which will interrupt practice sessions in the free version
  • Rating base is still small and moving — an AppBrain snapshot on 24 October showed 5.0 stars, while the live Play page now shows about 4.3 stars from over 650 ratings, so treat early scores cautiously
  • The tuner needs microphone access, as any tuner must — worth knowing before installing
  • AppBrain lists 21 permissions, so check them before installing
  • Screenshots are promotional (the in-app branding reads "PerfectTuner"), so real-world tuning accuracy may vary by phone and room noise

Pricing

Guitar Tuner & Metronome is free to download on Google Play, and it contains ads. The listing data checked for this review shows it as free with ads, with no in-app purchase price range stated in that data, so check any offer you meet inside the app before accepting it. The current version is 1.0.9, updated 19 October 2026, and the download is about 50 MB — modest for a practice app, so a mobile download is fine.

Download

Download Guitar Tuner & Metronome on Google Play

If you play guitar, bass or ukulele and want one free app for tuning, timing and chords, Guitar Tuner & Metronome is an easy try: the chart climb suggests plenty of other players are finding it useful too — just go in expecting ads and a still-settling rating.
