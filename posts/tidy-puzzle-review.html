Tidy Puzzle Review: ASMR Tidy Fun (2026)

Quick verdict — 3.8 / 5. Tidy Puzzle: Perfect Satisfy is a genuinely calming little game: tap, drag and slide a messy room, laundry pile or make-up palette back into perfect order, with soft ASMR sounds as you go. The tidying itself is satisfying and the art is cute without being overstimulating. It is held back by heavy advertising, a huge 352 MB download, and a middling 4.0-star average — so enjoy it as a free, relaxed time-filler, not as a must-have.

Tidy Puzzle: Perfect Satisfy, from developer FALCON GAME, is a relaxing puzzle game built around one simple pleasure: turning disorder into perfection. Each level hands you a mess — a cluttered room, a laundry basket, a smudged make-up palette, a half-cooked meal — and asks you to put it right by tapping, dragging and sliding objects into their proper places. There is no timer breathing down your neck and no fail state to dread; the game wants you to unwind, not to stress.

The numbers show a game climbing fast. Independent tracker AppBrain lists it on its trending puzzle page for late October 2026, with about 210,000 installs in 7 days — roughly three times the week before — and about 530,000 installs in the last 30 days, around 18,000 a day. In total AppBrain counts about 6.6 million installs, while Google Play itself shows a 5,000,000+ installs bracket. The live Play listing shows about 4.0 stars from over 9,100 ratings, and Play rates it Everyone. The current version is 1.5.6, updated 6 October 2026, and the download is about 352 MB — large for a casual puzzle game, so use Wi-Fi. It has been on Play since April 2025.

The pitch in one image: a cosy, freshly tidied room and a cheerful painter. That calm, cute look is the whole game in miniature.

How it actually plays

The Play description splits the game into three kinds of level. Cleaning mini-games ask you to interact with objects to clean them: tidy up a pool or a room, or fix a face make-up palette, with a realistic-simulation feel. Cooking levels have you preparing dishes from around the world — pizza, sushi, kimbap and bubble tea are all named — with calming ASMR sounds and visuals. And brain puzzles sit alongside both, so the game still asks you to think a little while you relax.

The controls are deliberately simple: tap, drag and slide. That simplicity is the point. You are not learning combos or chasing a high score; you are placing the right thing in the right spot and enjoying the small sound and visual payoff when it clicks into place. One reviewer on Play praised exactly this, saying the art style, music and touch feedback all fit together, and that the game is cute, cosy and colourful without being overstimulating visually or through its audio. New levels are promised weekly, so the supply of messes to fix keeps growing.

A laundry level: dresses on the rail, a washing machine below, and shelves of supplies to sort. Every object has an obvious home, which is what makes it relaxing.

Why it is trending

Tidy Puzzle is trending because it sits squarely in the ASMR tidying trend: the same satisfaction people get from watching a messy room become clean, turned into something they can do themselves in short bursts. Its rule needs no explanation — everyone knows what a tidy room looks like — yet each level still gives a small sense of completion. That combination travels well in screenshots and short clips, which helps explain the sharp October rise: three times the previous week's installs, and top-100 free puzzle rankings in several European countries, including France, Germany, the Netherlands and Poland.

It also helps that the game is genuinely varied. A game that only ever asked you to tidy rooms would get old; mixing in cooking levels and brain puzzles gives it more staying power than a single-mechanic tidy-up toy. The weekly level updates support that too, even if the core loop stays gentle by design. Players looking for a hard challenge will not find one here — AppBrain itself notes the casual relaxation focus may appeal less to players seeking deep or highly challenging puzzle modes — but that is a trade-off, not an accident.

A make-up palette level: a heart-shaped palette being brushed clean, with tools laid out below. The satisfying part is watching the mess disappear stroke by stroke.

Key features

  • Cleaning mini-games: tidy rooms, pools and make-up palettes by interacting with objects
  • Cooking levels: prepare dishes such as pizza, sushi, kimbap and bubble tea
  • Relaxing brain puzzles alongside the tidying and cooking
  • Satisfying ASMR sounds as items click into their perfect places
  • Cozy, lively graphics in a cute, colourful style
  • No time pressure — a casual game to unwind at your own pace
  • New levels added weekly

A cooking level: steak, asparagus and fries ready to plate, with a pan on the stove. The cooking stages are the same tidy-up loop with a tastier payoff.

Pros and cons

Pros
  • Instantly relaxing loop: tap, drag and slide mess into order
  • Three kinds of level — tidying, cooking and brain puzzles — so it does not get stale quickly
  • Cute, cosy art and ASMR sounds that reviewers praise for not being overstimulating
  • Free to download, with an Everyone content rating
  • Weekly new levels, and no data shared or collected according to the Play listing's Data safety section
Cons
  • Heavy ads are the top complaint: several reviewers report two ad videos after every level, some unskippable, and say they spent more time watching ads than playing
  • One reviewer reports an ad for malware interrupting a level, and another says the game is unplayable without Wi-Fi — treat both as single-player reports, not verified findings
  • Middling rating: about 4.0 stars, well below the best relaxing puzzlers
  • Very large download at about 352 MB for a casual game — use Wi-Fi
  • The Play listing notes data is not encrypted and cannot be deleted, despite no data being collected or shared

Pricing

Tidy Puzzle: Perfect Satisfy is free to download on Google Play, and it contains ads. The Play listing also signals in-app purchases, and one reviewer reports paying about $9 AUD to remove most of the intrusive advertisements, calling that reasonable compared with some other games — but that is one player's report, so check exactly what any paid offer includes before you buy. Given how often reviewers complain about the ads, the free version is best tried first: if the ad frequency bothers you after a few levels, you will know before spending anything. The current version is 1.5.6, updated 6 October 2026, and the download is about 352 MB.

Download

Download Tidy Puzzle: Perfect Satisfy on Google Play

If you like a game that calms you down rather than winds you up, Tidy Puzzle is an easy, free try: a fast-climbing chart game with a genuinely satisfying core loop, as long as you can tolerate its ads.
