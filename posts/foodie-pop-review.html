Foodie Pop! Review: Tasty Puzzle (2026)

Quick verdict — 3.5 / 5. Foodie Pop! is a cheerful, easy-to-grasp cooking puzzle: read each pet customer's order, pick the right ingredients from the grid, and watch the dish cook itself. The early levels are genuinely relaxing — but ads, a chunky download and some brutally hard later levels keep it from a higher score.

Foodie Pop!, from developer ABI GAMES PTE. LTD., launched on Google Play in October 2026 and has already passed 280,000 installs — every one of them in the last 30 days, at roughly 21,000 installs a day. That is a fast start for a two-week-old game, and it has reached #1 Top New Free Puzzle in the United States and the United Kingdom, with #2 showings in Canada, Germany, France and Japan. On Google Play it is rated Everyone, it is free, and it contains ads. Its rating is divided — around 3.9 stars in the latest AppBrain snapshot (1,215 ratings) and lower in the live Play snapshot — which, as the reviews show, comes down to one thing: how much you mind the ads and the difficulty spikes.

The pitch is simple, and it is the game's best idea. You are not tapping frantically to chop, stir and plate against a timer. Instead, you check what each cute customer orders, choose the right ingredients from the puzzle grid, and watch them cook automatically in ovens, blenders, grills and more — then serve the finished dish and keep the customer smiling. Serve customers fast and in order, the developer warns, or they get angry and leave. It is match-and-serve puzzle play with a cooking skin, and in the early levels it clicks immediately.

How it plays

Each level gives you a set of customer orders at the top of the screen — a burger here, fries and a drink there — and a grid of ingredients below. You pick the ingredients each order needs, the kitchen appliances do the cooking, and the finished dish goes out. The loop adds pressure gently: more customers pile up, orders get longer, and you have to think ahead about which ingredients to pick first so nothing waits too long. There is no timer breathing down your neck in the early game — one reviewer specifically praises the lack of a timer — and the 2x speed mode lets you play at your own pace when you want things faster.

Between levels, the restaurant side of the game opens up. You earn coins and stars to expand and customise your restaurant, upgrade your kitchen to serve orders faster, and unlock new ingredients, recipes and kitchen areas as you progress through days of increasing challenge. The dishes themselves are the usual comfort-food cast — donuts, cupcakes, burgers, fries and drinks — served to a whole cast of lovable pet characters, from the capybara in the key art to a police-eagle customer and a pair of cat chefs. The developer also promises relaxing ASMR-style cooking sounds, and the screenshots back up the cosy look: bright kitchens, chunky ingredient tiles and very cute customers.

Why it is trending

Foodie Pop! is trending because it is instantly readable. One screenshot tells you the whole game — hungry pet, order ticket, ingredient grid — and the "pick the ingredients, the cooking happens itself" twist removes the fiddly time-management stress that puts many players off cooking games. Players praise exactly that: one five-star reviewer says each level is different, some are challenging, the graphics are smooth, the characters are cute, and the gameplay actually matches the advertisement. Another calls it a beautiful cooking game and jokes that playing it feels like eating enough food for one day.

It also helps that the game is generous early on. The first levels teach the loop in minutes, the goals are always visible at the top of the screen, and a quick level fits a coffee break. At about 222 MB (version 1.9, updated 22 October 2026), it is a chunky download for a puzzle game — worth knowing before you tap install on mobile data — but the polish in the screenshots explains why so many players have tried it in its first two weeks.

Key features

  • Pick-and-cook puzzle play — choose the right ingredients from the grid; ovens, blenders and grills cook automatically.
  • Cute pet customers — a whole cast of animal customers with orders you can read at a glance.
  • Serve fast, in order — keep customers happy before they get angry and leave.
  • A huge menu — donuts, cupcakes, burgers, fries, drinks and more to cook and serve.
  • Restaurant growth — earn coins and stars to expand and customise your own restaurant.
  • Kitchen upgrades — upgrade appliances and unlock new ingredients, recipes and kitchen areas.
  • Day-by-day progression — progress through days with increasing challenges that keep gameplay fresh.
  • 2x speed mode — play faster when you want to, or take it slow with no timer pressure early on.
  • Everyone rating — Google Play rates the app's content as suitable for everyone.

Pros and cons

Pros
  • Instantly understandable loop — read the order, pick the ingredients, serve the dish
  • Genuinely cosy: cute pet customers, bright kitchens and relaxing cooking sounds
  • Fast start with real traction — 280,000 installs in its first two weeks, #1 new Puzzle in the US and UK
  • No frantic timer in the early game, plus a 2x speed mode for faster play
  • Restaurant growth gives the puzzle a longer-term goal beyond clearing levels
  • Everyone content rating, and gameplay that matches its advertising, per reviewers
Cons
  • Contains ads — one reviewer reports an ad after every level, another reports a banner blocking abilities around level 10
  • Hard and super-hard levels can feel impossible even with power-ups, and random orders mean you cannot learn a level for next time
  • Gem pressure: reviewers say later levels push you towards gems and purchases
  • About 222 MB — a chunky download for a puzzle game
  • Still a young game with a divided rating, so the average may move as more players arrive
  • Restaurant upgrades can feel slow, with players waiting for the next area to open

Pricing

Foodie Pop! is free to download on Google Play, and it contains ads. The Play listing checked for this review shows a $0 price with no in-app purchase price range displayed, so treat any paid offer inside the app on its own merits: reviewers mention gem purchases and one reports an $8 ad-removal offer, but those are player reports, not listing facts. The current version is 1.9, updated 22 October 2026, and the download is about 222 MB — best grabbed on Wi-Fi if you are trying it on a whim.

Download

Download Foodie Pop! on Google Play

If you like a cooking game without the time-management panic, Foodie Pop! is an easy try: a fresh pick-the-ingredients twist, a genuinely cute cast, and a loop — read, pick, serve — that stays satisfying well past the first few restaurants. Just go in knowing the ads and the hard levels are real.
