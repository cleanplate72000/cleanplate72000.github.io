Chicken vs Aliens Soft-Launches on Android

Naphora's Chicken vs Aliens has gone from game jam to soft launch in just six weeks, and the quirky action game is now available on Google Play, with an App Store release to follow.

Why this matters: A six-week journey from game jam to a playable soft launch is unusually fast, and it shows how a small studio can test a fun idea with real players before committing to a full worldwide release.

Chicken vs Aliens: the key facts

  • Game: Chicken vs Aliens, from developer Naphora
  • Status: Soft launch now available on Google Play; App Store release to follow
  • Premise: Players take control of a chicken and battle waves of aliens
  • Progression: As the chicken levels up, players select new upgrade cards to build a bird with real firepower
  • Abilities: Unlockable attacks include Burning Shoot, Spread Shoot and Explosive Shoot
  • Backstory: The game went from game jam to soft launch in just six weeks
  • Studio: Naphora, whose managing director Charles Bussy has spoken about rebuilding the studio and going mobile-first

What to watch next

Watch for the App Store release date, and for whether the soft launch expands to more regions before a full global launch. Because this is a soft launch, features and availability may change as Naphora gathers player feedback. For more mobile gaming news, see our VAMPIR global launch story and the Top 100 apps page.
