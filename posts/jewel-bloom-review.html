Jewel Bloom Review: Gem Coloring Calm (2026)

Quick verdict — 4.5 / 5. Jewel Bloom: Gem Coloring is a genuinely calming colouring puzzle: sort sparkling jewels into place, watch a pixel artwork appear, and play at your own pace with no timers and no Wi-Fi needed. It is free and beautifully simple — just expect ads, a $9.99 in-app purchase item, and a still-small rating base behind that eye-catching 4.9-star average.

Jewel Bloom: Gem Coloring Games, from developer Ashita Games, launched on Google Play on 1 October 2026 and has already passed 100,000+ installs on the store. On Google Play it holds 4.9 stars from about 1,630 ratings, and AppBrain's tracked total is around 220,000 installs, with roughly 190,000 of those in recent weeks — the kind of fast, early growth that puts a new puzzle game on the trending lists. It appears in the top twenty new free puzzle charts in several European countries, including the Czech Republic, Austria and the Netherlands. For a colouring game barely a month old, that is a striking start — and the reason it earns a review here this week.

The idea is inspired by fuse bead art and DIY crafts, and it shows. Each puzzle starts as a scattered board of coloured jewels. You move misplaced gems into empty slots, sort matching colours together, and complete each colour region until the hidden picture reveals itself. Matching jewels move together in batches, so you never have to place every gem one by one — the developer's own pitch is simple: sort colourful jewels, reveal gem art, and relax.

How it plays

The loop is calm by design. A half-finished picture sits at the top of the screen, with a tray of coloured gems below it. Your job is to work out which colours belong where, clear space for the right colours, and place them in batches. As each region fills, the picture sharpens — a starburst here, a sunset there, a flamingo by a palm tree — until the whole artwork is complete. There are no countdowns and no sudden game overs, so a level never punishes you for thinking slowly.

Two small helpers keep the game friendly rather than fiddly. First, you can zoom in on large, detailed artworks to focus on one part of the picture at a time. Second, glowing slots point to a possible next move whenever you need a little guidance, so you are rarely left staring at a board with no idea what to do next. The combination — big pictures, clear colours, gentle hints — makes Jewel Bloom one of the easiest puzzle games on the store to pick up, including for players who do not usually play puzzle games at all.

Why it is trending

Jewel Bloom is trending because it reads instantly and relaxes instantly. Colour-sorting games are everywhere, but a game where sorting produces a finished piece of art — a picture you can actually admire when you are done — gives every level a payoff that a plain sorted board does not. The pictures themselves help too: the store screenshots show kawaii characters, cute animals, landscapes, everyday objects and even iconic artworks, all rendered as sparkling pixel gems. A new picture is always a small surprise.

It also fits real life well. The game is playable offline, so it works on a train, in a waiting room or anywhere without Wi-Fi, and a single picture makes a satisfying short session while a longer session can run through several designs. Version 1.8.0, updated 16 October 2026, runs on Android 7.0 and up, so it should work on most phones in use today. The Play listing does not show a download size, so check the size on the store page before downloading if storage is tight.

Key features

  • Jewel sorting puzzles — move misplaced gems into empty slots and organise each board colour by colour.
  • Batch moves — matching jewels move together, so pictures complete faster without bead-by-bead work.
  • Pixel art reveals — finish a colour region and watch the hidden picture appear, from animals to landscapes to iconic artworks.
  • Zoom for detail — zoom in on large artworks and focus comfortably on every part of a picture.
  • Gentle hints — glowing slots suggest a possible next move when you get stuck.
  • No timers, no pressure — no countdowns and no sudden game overs; play entirely at your own pace.
  • Offline play — enjoy jewel colouring puzzles anywhere, with no Wi-Fi needed.
  • Everyone rating — Google Play rates the app's content as suitable for everyone.

Pricing

Jewel Bloom: Gem Coloring Games is free to download on Google Play, and it contains ads. The listing also shows in-app purchases, at $9.99 per item in the store's About details — no price range is shown, so treat any paid offer inside the app on its own merits and check it before accepting. The current version is 1.8.0, updated 16 October 2026, and it requires Android 7.0 or later.
