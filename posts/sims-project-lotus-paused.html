EA Pauses Mobile Sims Project Lotus

Electronic Arts has paused development on its mobile multiplayer Sims game, Project Lotus, and shifted its focus to a different Sims project, Project Evergreen. The change was announced on October 21, 2026, in EA's "Building EA's Next Chapter" update, alongside the closure of BioWare covered in our EA BioWare shutdown story.

Key facts

  • What paused: Project Lotus, the mobile-focused multiplayer Sims title previously known as Project Rene, in development in some form since at least late 2022.
  • What it was: a free-to-play multiplayer Sims game focused on mobile — not a direct follow-up to The Sims 4, despite early fan hopes.
  • What replaces the focus: Project Evergreen, which EA says it is prioritising so the team has more time to reach the project's full potential and vision.
  • EA's wording: "We're going to prioritize our efforts on 'Project Evergreen' for The Sims ... and to help make this possible, we're pausing development on 'Project Lotus.'"
  • Not a cancellation: EA described Lotus as paused, not cancelled, and gave no release window or platform plan for either project.
  • What Evergreen is — unconfirmed: EA has not said what Evergreen is. Sims Community reporting describes it as more like The Sims 4: 2 than a numbered Sims 5, so treat any "Sims 5" talk as rumour, not fact.

Why it matters

For mobile players, this is a clear change of direction: the Sims game built for phones has been put on hold, while the franchise's next big step is being developed elsewhere. For long-time Sims 4 players, it is also a sign that EA is not walking away from the series — it is concentrating its Sims team on one project instead of splitting it across two.

What to watch next

Watch for EA or Maxis to say anything concrete about Project Evergreen — its scope, platforms and timing are all still unannounced. Until then, the only confirmed facts are the pause of Project Lotus and the prioritising of Evergreen; anything about a fall 2027 launch or a Steam Deck version remains unconfirmed reporting, not an EA announcement.

Source: Dot Esports, "The Sims pauses Project Lotus in favor of a rumored mainline title" (published October 21, 2026), reporting EA's announcement and quoting it directly. Details such as Evergreen's scope and timing may change — check the source and EA's announcement for the latest information.
