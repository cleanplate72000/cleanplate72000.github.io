Home's 14th Anniversary Update Hits Mobile

Benjamin Rivers' pixel-art mystery Home is still going strong fourteen years after its 2012 PC launch. A new 14th Anniversary update is out now on iOS and Android, and for the first time the game is also available on the Epic Games Store — free on mobile until October 29.

Why this matters: Home is a short mystery you can finish in an evening, and this update makes it the best version yet on phones — with a developer commentary mode, smoother 60 FPS play and better controller support. The free Epic Games Store window makes it an easy pick-up before October 29.

Home anniversary update: what is new

  • Postmortem Mode: a developer commentary that changes depending on the choices you make, brought over from the Switch version
  • Behind-the-scenes gallery added alongside the commentary
  • Better controller support and 60 FPS on mobile
  • Free on the Epic Games Store for mobile from October 22 to October 29
  • Platforms: iOS and Android (the game first reached iPad a year after its 2012 PC launch)

What is Home?

Home is a pixel-art mystery: you wake up in a dark room during a storm, inside a house that is not yours, and piece together what happened from what you find. There are no enemies to fight and no inventory to manage — the story shifts in small ways depending on how you read the clues, so the truth is largely whatever you decide it was.

What to watch next

The free Epic Games Store offer runs until October 29, so that is the deadline if you want the game for nothing — you will need the Epic Games Store app set up on your phone first.
