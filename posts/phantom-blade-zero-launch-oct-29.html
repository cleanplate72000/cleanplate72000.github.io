Phantom Blade Zero Out Oct 29, Preload Live

Phantom Blade Zero, S-Game's wuxia action game featuring martial arts star Donnie Yen, launches on 29 October 2026 for PC and PlayStation 5, and preloads are already live. IGN's release-times guide confirms the date and the staggered unlock, so players in the Americas get in on the evening of 28 October while most of the rest of the world follows on 29 October.

Phantom Blade Zero: the key facts

  • Launch date and platforms: 29 October 2026, on PC and PS5.
  • Unlock times: access depends on where you live — 7pm PDT on 28 October in Los Angeles and 10pm EDT on 28 October in New York, 2am BST on 29 October in London, and 10am CST on 29 October in Beijing.
  • Preload: preloading began on 26 October (7pm PDT in Los Angeles, 10pm EDT in New York, and 2am BST on 27 October in London), so the game can be ready before launch day.
  • IGN's verdict: IGN scored it 9/10, calling it "an unbelievably smooth action game filled with memorable boss fights, compelling combat, and limitless swagger."
  • Early review snapshot: a GameBeacon roundup dated 26 October put it at 88 on Metacritic from 31 critic reviews (30 positive, 1 mixed) — an early snapshot that may move as more reviews land.
  • Install size: a hefty 145GB, and an SSD is required on PC.
  • PC specs: minimum specs call for 16GB of RAM and a GeForce GTX 1660 6GB or Radeon RX 5500 XT 8GB (1080p/30fps with upscaling); recommended specs list a GeForce RTX 3060 Ti or Radeon RX 6700 XT 12GB (1440p/60fps with upscaling).

Why it matters

Phantom Blade Zero has been one of the autumn's most-watched action games, and the first verdicts back up the hype around its combat: reviewers keep praising the boss fights and the smooth, stylish fighting, even where they are cooler on the story. With preloads live and a 145GB download, the practical news for players is to start the download early — especially on PC, where the SSD requirement and the recommended specs mean older machines should check the requirements before launch day.
