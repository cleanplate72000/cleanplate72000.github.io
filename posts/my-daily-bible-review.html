My Daily Bible Review: KJV Every Day (2026)

Quick verdict — 4.0 / 5. My Daily Bible - KJV Version does exactly what its name promises: a simple, free King James Bible app built around one verse, one prayer and one small habit a day. It is genuinely easy to use and rated for Everyone — just expect adverts to interrupt the reading, which is the main complaint in its lower-rated reviews.

My Daily Bible - KJV Version, from developer KBeauty, is one of the fastest-climbing Books & Reference apps on Google Play this month. It holds 4.6 stars from about 600 ratings, sits in the 100,000+ installs bracket on Play, and — remarkably for an app that only appeared on Play in September 2026 — all roughly 110,000 of those installs have arrived in the last 30 days, at about 3,800 installs a day. It is currently ranked #8 Top Free Books & Reference in Canada, #10 in Brazil, #34 in the United States and #24 in the United Kingdom. That is a sharp, worldwide climb for a small, single-purpose app, and the reason it earns a review here this week.

The idea is deliberately small. My Daily Bible is not trying to be a full Bible study suite with commentaries, maps and concordances. It is built for a daily routine: open the app in the morning, read the day's verse, say the day's prayer, and close it again. The developer's own tagline sums it up: “Daily Bible verses, prayers, devotionals, and spiritual inspiration.” For readers who want Scripture in their day without a complicated app getting in the way, that restraint is the appeal.

What you actually get

The app's core features, as listed by the developer, are straightforward:

  • Daily Bible verses — an inspiring King James Version verse to guide and encourage your day.
  • Daily prayers — simple prayers for peace, strength, gratitude, hope and faith.
  • Devotional inspiration — short Bible-based messages for reflection and spiritual growth.
  • Favourites — save the verses and prayers you want to return to, so they are easy to find again.
  • Audio Bible — listen to Scripture read aloud, useful when your eyes are busy or you prefer to listen.
  • Bible quiz and reading plan — short quizzes, such as a five-question round, that test what you have read and keep the habit engaging.
  • Simple, clean design — a peaceful reading experience with an easy-to-use interface.

The reading view itself is clean and practical. A store screenshot shows Genesis chapter 3 in the traditional King James wording, with a toolbar offering Note, Bookmark, Share and Copy, plus coloured highlight dots for marking passages. That is exactly the small set of tools a daily reader needs: read, mark, save, share — nothing more to learn.

Why it is trending

My Daily Bible is trending because it fits a morning routine. A single verse and a short prayer take only a few minutes, the app opens straight into that day's content, and the habit is easy to keep — which is why reviewers describe it as the first thing they do in the morning, even when travelling. One five-star reviewer writes that they enjoy reading it every day, “especially in the morning”, while another says it “gets me through each and every day”. At about 31 MB (version 1.0.2, updated 22 October 2026), it is also a light download, and its Everyone content rating means it is suitable for the whole family.

The audio option matters too. A store screenshot shows the Audio Bible playing Genesis chapter 1 with playback controls and a 1.0x speed setting, so a chapter can be listened to while cooking, commuting or getting ready. For a habit app, that flexibility is the difference between a streak that survives a busy day and one that does not.

Prayer, anytime

The prayer section is just as simple. A store screenshot shows a Morning Prayer drawn from 2 Corinthians, with the full prayer text on screen and a single “Amen” button to finish. There is no social feed, no comments section and no extra layer to wade through — you read the prayer, tap Amen, and you are done. That quiet simplicity is clearly part of why the app's ratings are high: it respects the few minutes it asks for.

Pros and cons

Pros
  • Strong 4.6 stars from about 600 ratings, with all 110,000 installs arriving in the last 30 days
  • Simple daily routine: one verse, one prayer and a short devotional, easy to keep up
  • King James Version text with Note, Bookmark, Share, Copy and highlight tools
  • Audio Bible for listening to chapters hands-free, plus quizzes and a reading plan
  • Rated Everyone, free to download, and only about 31 MB
  • Favourites let you save the verses and prayers you want to revisit
Cons
  • Contains adverts, and several one-star reviewers say ads interrupt reading too often — the app's main weakness
  • Some reviewers report quizzes and prompts getting in the way of simply reading
  • A few reviewers note the audio uses AI-style voices rather than human narration
  • Very new (September 2026), so long-term support and depth are still unproven
  • Focused on daily verses and prayers only — not a full study Bible with commentaries or maps

Pricing

My Daily Bible - KJV Version is free to download on Google Play, and its listing data shows that it contains adverts. The listing checked for this review shows no in-app purchase price range, so the free download is the honest headline — but the adverts are the real cost to weigh. If you read for a few minutes a day, an occasional advert is a small price; if you read for longer stretches, the interruptions that some reviewers complain about may grate. Try it for a week of mornings before deciding whether it earns a permanent place on your home screen.

Download

Download My Daily Bible on Google Play

If you want a quiet, simple way to bring a King James verse and a prayer into each morning, My Daily Bible is an easy recommendation: a 4.6-star average, a clean reading view, audio for busy mornings, and a habit that takes minutes rather than an hour.
