Paint Camo Review: Hide & Seek 3D (2026)

Quick verdict — 4.5 / 5. Paint Camo: Hide & Seek 3D turns the schoolyard classic into a paint-matching stealth game: spray yourself the exact colour of the wall, strike a pose, and freeze while the hunter walks past. It is free, Everyone-rated, and has raced to about 780,000 installs in just 43 days — but be ready for ads between rounds, the one complaint that keeps showing up in player reviews.

Paint Camo: Hide & Seek 3D, from developer Unicorn Studio Official, has one of the most instantly understandable pitches on Google Play: "Paint your body to hide! Blend in and outsmart hunters in hide & seek." There is no complicated tutorial to survive and no sprawling open world to learn. Each round gives you a room, a paint gun, and a hunter with sharp eyes — your job is to disappear into the scenery before the search begins. It is the kind of game that is fun within thirty seconds of opening it, which goes a long way towards explaining its chart climb.

The numbers are striking for a game barely six weeks old. AppBrain's data for 24 October 2026 puts Paint Camo at about 780,000 installs, every one of them in the last 30 days, averaging roughly 26,000 installs a day. The same day's trending Casual chart credits it with 460,000 installs in seven days — twice the week before — and it ranks #9 in Top Free Casual in Russia, #15 in India and #151 in the United States. The rating is strong too: 4.6 stars from 820 ratings on AppBrain's 24 October snapshot, with Google Play showing a similar 4.6-star score. The app is about 264 MB at version 1.0.8, updated 22 October 2026, and it carries an Everyone content rating.

So how does a round actually work? As the hider, you pick a spot, then use the brush tools to spray your body with paint that matches whatever is behind you — bricks, wood panelling, wallpaper, or a museum wall. Matching the colour is only half the job, and this is where Paint Camo is cleverer than a simple colour-matching toy: you also have to choose a pose that blends into the props around you. Tuck yourself beside a barrel, a candle stand or a piece of furniture, hold the pose, and stay perfectly still while the hunter scans the room. One wrong shade, one stray elbow, and you are spotted.

Then the roles flip, and the game shows its other half. As the hunter, you walk the same rooms looking for the detail that does not belong: a patch of paint slightly off-shade, a shadow in the wrong place, a "statue" that is breathing just a little too obviously. The Play listing describes it as shooting down sneaky hiders, but in practice this is cartoon paint-blaster play against blocky characters — there is nothing graphic here, and the Everyone rating reflects that. Winning as the hunter is a genuine observation test, not a reflex test, which makes it a good fit for players who like spotting the difference rather than fast thumbs.

Key features

  • Paint-to-hide stealth. Spray your character with paint matched to bricks, wood, wallpaper and other surfaces, then freeze — the core loop is simple, visual and immediately satisfying.
  • Pose-based camouflage. Colour alone will not save you: you must pick a pose that fits the props around you, from barrels to furniture, to truly disappear.
  • Two roles in every match. Play as the hider trying to survive the round, then switch to the hunter and spot every disguised opponent before time runs out.
  • Multiple paint maps. Detailed stages with different patterns, textures and lighting keep the hiding spots fresh — a trick that works in one room will not work in the next.
  • Unlockable customisation. Completing levels unlocks new paint colours, brushes, poses and character skins, so regular players get a growing wardrobe of disguises.
  • Everyone-rated and free. Free to download on Google Play with an Everyone content rating, and the whole hide-and-seek loop is playable without paying.

Pros and cons

What works: the central idea is genuinely original in a crowded casual market — most hide-and-seek games are about running, while this one is about standing still and thinking. The two-role structure doubles the game: hiders learn to think like hunters and vice versa, so matches stay interesting far longer than a single-mechanic game would. The presentation helps too: bright, chunky 3D rooms and expressive blocky characters make every near-miss funny rather than frustrating. And the growth figures are not hype — 780,000 installs in 43 days with a 4.6-star rating is a real breakout, not a paid spike.

What to watch: first, ads. Player reviews praise the gameplay and then complain about the advertising in the same breath — one October reviewer calls it their favourite game "but... the adds", and another one-star review complains about back-to-back ads between rounds. If you play in short bursts, expect interruptions. Second, the rating base is still small — 820 ratings against 780,000 installs — so the 4.6-star score could shift as more players weigh in. Third, at about 264 MB it is a hefty download for a casual game, so check storage before installing. Finally, a privacy note from the Play listing's data-safety section: the app may share location and personal info with third parties, though data is encrypted in transit and you can request deletion — worth knowing, standard for free ad-supported games.

Pricing

Paint Camo: Hide & Seek 3D is free to download on Google Play, and the Play listing fetched for this review showed a price of $0. The listing does not disclose an in-app purchase price range in the data we could verify, so we are not quoting one here — check the Play page's current details before spending anything in-game. The core hide-and-seek loop, both roles and the map rotation are playable without paying; ads are the main cost of the free version.

If you like light, satisfying casual games with a clever twist, our Stress Relief review covers another October 2026 breakout built around simple, calming mechanics. Paint Camo is the livelier of the two: less of a wind-down toy, more of a quick-fire party game — and the moment a hunter walks straight past your perfectly painted hiding spot is genuinely delightful.

Download Paint Camo on Google Play

The bottom line: Paint Camo: Hide & Seek 3D earns its trending spot with a simple idea executed well — paint, pose, freeze, and hope the hunter looks the other way. The ads between rounds are a real annoyance, the rating base is still young, and the download is chunky. But as a free, Everyone-rated game that turns standing still into the tensest, funniest minute on your phone, it is one of the easiest recommendations in this week's trending charts: download it, find your favourite hiding spot, and try not to giggle when the hunter walks by.
