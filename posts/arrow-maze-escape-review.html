Arrow Maze Escape Review: Tap Order (2026)

Quick verdict — 4.0 / 5. Arrow Maze Escape, from RV AppStudios, is a clean, calm arrow puzzle that does one thing well: it makes you think before you tap. It is free, rated Everyone, and its 4.5-star player rating is backed by real momentum — 100,000+ installs on Google Play and 92,000 of them in the last 30 days alone.

Arrow Maze Escape - Arrow Game is the puzzle hit from developer RV AppStudios (package com.rvappstudios.arrow.maze.escape.puzzle.arrow.game), on Google Play since July 2026. Its latest version, 1.0.8, was updated on 12 October 2026 and adds a Halloween theme. At the last AppBrain check on 24 October 2026 it held a 4.53-star rating from 800 ratings, with 520 of those at five stars and none at one star, and it reached #4 in the Top New Free Puzzle chart in India. That is a strong start for a game with no famous licence behind it — and, from the way it plays, it is easy to see why people are sticking with it.

All screenshots in this review are the developer's own promotional images from the official Google Play Store listing.

What Arrow Maze Escape does

The premise could hardly be simpler, and that is the point. Each level gives you a crowded board full of arrows, all pointing in different directions. You tap the arrows in the right order to slide each one out of the maze. An arrow moves only when its path is clear, so a careless tap can block the board — and tapping a blocked arrow costs you a life. Clear every arrow and the board is solved.

That one rule creates the whole game. Early boards are gentle: a few arrows, obvious exits, and a quick win. Later boards, like the ones in the screenshots below, are dense mazes where one arrow sits directly in another's way, and the only solution is to work out the exact sequence before you start tapping. It is a logic puzzle first and a reflex game never, which makes it a good fit for short breaks: a level takes a minute or two, there is no timer breathing down your neck, and you can put the phone down mid-board without losing anything.

Key features

  • Hundreds of handcrafted levels — the listing promises hundreds of puzzles with unique layouts and gradually increasing difficulty, and the screenshots alone show levels numbered into the hundreds.
  • Daily challenges — fresh challenges each day give you a reason to come back after you have worked through the main levels.
  • Hints when you get stuck — a hint button highlights your next safe move, so a hard board does not become a dead end.
  • Clean, minimal design — plain boards, bright arrows, no visual clutter, and smooth tap controls that keep your attention on the paths, not the menus.
  • A seasonal twist — the October 2026 update adds a Halloween theme, so the same puzzles get a spooky new look for the season.
  • Suitable for all ages — Google Play rates it Everyone, and the developer describes it as suitable for players of all ages.

Pros and cons

Pros:

  • Instantly understandable — one rule, no tutorial needed
  • Genuinely clever later levels that reward planning over speed
  • Calm, untimed play that suits short sessions
  • Hints and daily challenges keep it from going stale
  • Strong player rating (4.5 stars) with a fast-growing install base

Cons:

  • Contains ads, as listed on Google Play — expect interruptions between levels
  • At about 88 MB it is large for a simple puzzle game, so check your storage first
  • The lives system can frustrate: tapping a blocked arrow costs a life, which punishes experimentation
  • The arrow-escape genre is crowded in 2026, so if you already play a similar game, this one may feel familiar rather than fresh

The listing does list in-app purchases, priced from $0.99 to $74.99 per item, so read any offer screens inside the game before tapping. It is rated Everyone, with In-Game Purchases listed as an interactive element, it is about 88 MB, and it comes from RV AppStudios — worth stating plainly, because several different games share similar “arrow maze” names on the store, and this review covers only the RV AppStudios game linked below.

Final verdict

Arrow Maze Escape is not reinventing the puzzle genre, and it does not need to. It takes a simple, satisfying idea — tap the arrows in the right order, clear the board — and executes it cleanly, with hundreds of levels, daily challenges, and a calm pace that respects your time. The ads and the 88 MB size are real downsides, and the lives system will annoy players who like to experiment freely. But a 4.5-star rating from 800 players, 100,000+ installs, and a #4 new-puzzle chart position in India say this is a trending game that actually delivers on its promise.
