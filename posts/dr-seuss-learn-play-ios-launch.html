Dr. Seuss Learn & Play Out Now on iOS

The official Dr. Seuss learning app is out now: Dr. Seuss: Learn & Play has launched on iPhone and iPad, bringing more than 100 learning games inspired by 22 Dr. Seuss books to children aged 2 to 8.

Why this matters: It is a rare big-name children's app built around reading first — no ads, no behavioural tracking and no grind mechanics — with a weekly progress report for parents.

Dr. Seuss: Learn & Play: the key facts

  • App: Dr. Seuss: Learn & Play, from Be The Monster, made under licence from Dr. Seuss Enterprises
  • Platforms: iPhone and iPad only, as reported — no Android version has been announced
  • Content: More than 100 learning games inspired by 22 Dr. Seuss books, for children aged 2 to 8
  • Learning areas: Reading, counting, music, art and problem-solving, woven into the books' stories
  • Child-friendly design: No ads, no behavioural tracking, no coins or streaks, and one price covers up to 4 children
  • Price (US): $7.99 a month or $64.99 a year, after a 7-day free trial — check the App Store listing for pricing in your region
  • Parents: A weekly report shows what is taking hold, such as letters recognised and words starting to be read alone

What to watch next

Watch for an Android version, new books added to the 22 at launch, and how parents rate the subscription once the first trials end. For more family-friendly mobile news, see our Apple Arcade November story and the Top 100 apps page.
