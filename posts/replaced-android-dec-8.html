Cyberpunk Hit REPLACED Hits Android Dec 8

Atari and Sad Cat Studios have announced that cyberpunk action platformer REPLACED is coming to Android on December 8, 2026. The Android version is being ported by Poland-based studio The Knights of U and will arrive as part of the Google Play Games program.

Why this matters: REPLACED was one of this year's most striking indie releases on PC and Xbox, and its Android arrival means phone players can now join in — with a single purchase covering mobile, tablet and PC through Google Play Games.

REPLACED on Android: the key facts

  • Game: REPLACED, from Sad Cat Studios, published by Atari
  • Android release date: December 8, 2026
  • Port studio: The Knights of U, based in Poland
  • Google Play Games: One purchase gives access across Android phones, tablets and PC, with progress syncing between devices
  • Pre-registration: Open now on Google Play
  • Original launch: PC and Xbox Series X|S in April 2026, now played by more than 900,000 players across all platforms
  • The game: A 2.5D cyberpunk action platformer — you play R.E.A.C.H., an AI trapped in a human body, exploring an alternate 1980s America reshaped by nuclear disaster
  • Style: Cinematic pixel-art visuals blending 3D assets with pixel art, plus melee and ranged combat and heavy emphasis on exploration
  • iOS version: No iOS release has been announced — this announcement is for Android only
  • Controls: Full touch-control and gamepad details for the Android version are still to be confirmed

What to watch next

Watch for the Google Play listing to confirm pricing and control options ahead of December 8, and for any word on an iOS version. REPLACED is an action-heavy cyberpunk title, so younger players should check the store age rating first.
