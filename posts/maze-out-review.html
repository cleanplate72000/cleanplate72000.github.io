Maze Out! - Tap Puzzle Review: Arrow Escapes Done Right (2026)

Quick verdict — 4.5 / 5. Maze Out! takes one of the simplest puzzle ideas on mobile — tap the arrows in the right order and clear the board — and turns it into a genuinely tense little race against the clock. It is free, it works offline, and its 4.7-star rating from early players is well earned.

Maze Out! - Tap Puzzle is the breakout puzzle hit from Grand Games A.Ş., launched on Google Play in October 2026. In its first days it passed 50,000 installs, reached #6 in the Top New Free Puzzle chart in Indonesia and #24 in the US puzzle chart, and holds a 4.7-star rating from over 1,300 reviews. That is a fast start for a game with no famous licence behind it — and, having looked at how it plays, it is easy to see why people are sticking with it.

All screenshots in this review are the developer's own promotional images from the official Google Play Store listing.

What Maze Out! does

The premise could hardly be simpler. Each level gives you a crowded board full of arrows, all pointing in different directions. You tap the arrows in the right order to extract each one from the board. Free one arrow and you open a path for the next; tap in the wrong order and you block yourself in. The twist is the clock: you have plenty of moves, but limited time, so every level becomes a small exercise in thinking fast as well as thinking clearly.

That combination — unlimited moves, limited time — is what separates Maze Out! from the many slow, untimed tap-away puzzles on the store. You are never punished for experimenting, but you are always under just enough pressure that a level feels like a race rather than a chore. Early boards are gentle introductions; later ones throw in linked arrows, corners, pipes and other obstacles that force you to plan several moves ahead instead of just tapping whatever looks free.

Key features that matter

  • Tap-away arrow puzzles with real planning — you must free arrows in the right order, not just tap at random. One blocked arrow can stall the whole board.
  • Race against the clock — plenty of moves but limited time per level, which keeps short sessions exciting and stops the game feeling flat.
  • Unique obstacles as you progress — linked arrows, corners, pipes and more change how each board behaves, so later levels do not feel like reskins of the first.
  • Offline play — no Wi-Fi needed, which makes it a genuinely useful game for commutes, flights and patchy signal.
  • Clean, calm presentation — simple designs, soft colours and smooth animations, built for comfortable longer sessions rather than sensory overload.
  • Satisfying flow — clear a path, clear the board, clear your mind: the developer's own summary is accurate, and the loop is easy to fall into.

Who it is for

Maze Out! is an easy recommendation for anyone who likes logic puzzles, brain-training games or the tap-away genre, and for parents looking for a game rated Everyone with no realistic violence — just arrows, mazes and a timer. It is also a strong pick for short-session players: a level takes a minute or two, the game works offline, and there is no story or daily-login pressure demanding your attention. If you prefer slow, untimed puzzling, the clock may grate; the game is at its best when you enjoy a little pressure.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Simple to learn, genuinely tricky to master — order matters, not just speed
  • Timed levels keep short sessions exciting
  • Works fully offline, anywhere
  • Everyone rating and family-friendly presentation
  • 4.7 stars from 1,300+ early ratings — players clearly like it
  • Obstacles like linked arrows and pipes keep later levels fresh

Cons

  • Contains ads, as with most free puzzle games
  • The timer will frustrate players who prefer untimed puzzles
  • At around 145 MB it is chunkier than the simplest puzzle apps
  • Still new, so long-term content depth is unproven

Pricing

Free to download and play on Google Play, with ads. There is no upfront cost and no subscription required to play through the puzzles. As always, check the official store listing for the current details before downloading.

  • Classic maze and logic puzzles — if the timer is the problem, look for an untimed tap-away or maze game instead; the genre is crowded, but few match Maze Out!'s polish at this size.

Final verdict

Maze Out! is the rare trending game that is exactly what it promises: a clean, clever, well-made arrow puzzle with just enough time pressure to make every cleared board feel earned. It is free, it works offline, and its early ratings suggest it is not a flash in the pan.
