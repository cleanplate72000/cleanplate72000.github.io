UnGodly Launches Worldwide on November 11

Azra Games has set November 11, 2026 as the worldwide launch date for UnGodly, its free-to-play dark fantasy action RPG for mobile. The game arrives on the App Store and Google Play, with the Galaxy Store and ONE Store following in December.

Why this matters: UnGodly is the debut game from a studio founded by Mark Otero, the creator of Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes, and it is pitching itself as a console-depth RPG built for phones, with four-hero teams and chained elemental attacks rather than auto-play combat.

UnGodly: the key facts

  • Game: UnGodly, a free-to-play dark fantasy action RPG with character collection and team-building, from Azra Games
  • Launch: November 11, 2026, worldwide on the App Store and Google Play; Galaxy Store and ONE Store follow in December
  • Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Vision Pro, Android and Google Play Games for PC
  • Combat: Command a team of four heroes, each tied to an ancient element, swapping between them mid-fight to chain abilities into bigger elemental attacks
  • World and story: Lead the Primals, heroes from divine bloodlines, against a force called The Devouring, through open-world PvE campaigns and a branching story
  • Roster: Includes Conan the Barbarian, following a crossover announced in July 2026; the story opens with mercenary Vander rescuing a young woman named Damea
  • Day-one competition: Leaderboards and Challenges are live from launch, turning the PvE campaign into a competitive mode
  • Pre-registration: Open now on the App Store and Google Play ahead of launch

What to watch next

Watch whether the launch build lives up to the studio's "progression earned, not bought" promise once players reach the character-collection shop, and whether the Galaxy Store and ONE Store versions arrive on time in December. The game is a darker, combat-heavy fantasy title, so younger players should check the store age rating first. For another dark fantasy launch this month, see our VAMPIR global launch post.
