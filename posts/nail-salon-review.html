Nail Salon Review: Relaxing Nail Art (2026)

Quick verdict — 4.0 / 5. Nail Salon: Nail Art Studio is a calm, genuinely creative little salon game: pick a nail, paint it, glitter it, jewel it, and admire the result. It is at its best as a five-minute creative break, not as a deep game — the loop is simple, it contains ads, and its rating still rests on a few hundred reviews. If you like low-pressure beauty games, it is an easy, free download.

Nail Salon: Nail Art Studio, from developer Uchiha Global, is a casual manicure game that has quietly climbed the free charts since its launch in September 2026. On its official Google Play listing it holds 4.5 stars from 378 ratings, with 100,000+ installs and an Everyone content rating, and AppBrain's October 2026 data puts its total installs at around 180,000 — all of them in the last 30 days, at roughly 5,900 installs a day. It has reached the top 100 free casual charts in several countries, including number 44 in the Czech Republic, number 47 in Slovakia, number 55 in Portugal and number 95 in India. It is free to download, contains ads, is about 80 MB in size, and was last updated on 21 September 2026 (version 1.0.2).

The idea is refreshingly simple. There is no timer pressuring you, no fail state to worry about, and no complicated salon-management system to learn. You open the studio, choose a nail, and build a manicure step by step: a base polish colour, a glitter or sparkling finish, a pattern, and a finishing jewel. You can create matching sets across the whole hand, or deliberately mix different looks on different nails. That freedom is the whole appeal — every finished hand is a small piece of personal design, and there is always another colour combination to try.

The design gallery shows the range on offer: glossy colours, glitter finishes, gems and patterned sets, all in one cheerful salon view.

The Play Store description breaks the experience into four parts. First, create your own nail art: start with a clean nail and build your style step by step, mixing colours, textures and decorative details. Second, explore colours and glitter: try bright polish, soft shades and sparkling finishes, using different salon tools to paint and customise each nail. Third, add patterns and jewels: layer floral patterns, glitter effects and colourful designs, then finish with heart-shaped gems and other decorative pieces. Fourth, style each nail individually, so a matching set and a mixed set are both possible. The how-to-play summary is just as short: select a nail, choose a polish colour or sparkling finish, apply patterns and decorative effects, add a jewel, and admire the finished nail art.

Painting is the heart of the game: a brush, a glitter polish and a single nail, with no timer and no pressure.

Key features

  • Step-by-step manicures: polish, glitter, pattern and jewel, one nail at a time.
  • Lots of colours and finishes: bright polish, soft shades and sparkling glitter effects to mix and match.
  • Patterns and decorations: floral patterns, glitter effects and heart-shaped gems for the finishing touch.
  • Individual nail styling: design matching sets or a different look on every nail.
  • Relaxing salon setting: simple touch controls and a cheerful beauty-salon atmosphere, with no fail state.
  • Free to play: free download on Google Play, with ads supporting the game.

Preparation matters too: filing and shaping the nail comes before the polish goes on.

Who is it for?

This is a game for anyone who enjoys creative, low-stress play: beauty and fashion fans, younger players (it carries an Everyone rating), and anyone who wants a relaxing five-minute break rather than a competitive challenge. It also sits nicely alongside other creative hobby apps — if you like making things with your hands, our SewPal review covers a beginner sewing companion in the same gentle, creative spirit. What it is not is a deep progression game: there is no salon to upgrade, no story campaign, and no long-term goal beyond the next design. If you need unlocks and levels to stay interested, the simple loop may wear thin after a few sessions.

Pros
  • Calm, pressure-free creative play with no fail state
  • Wide mix of colours, glitter, patterns and gems
  • Style each nail individually or build matching sets
  • Everyone rating, free to download
  • Fast-growing: around 180,000 installs in its first month
Cons
  • Contains ads, which interrupt the relaxing flow
  • Simple loop with little long-term progression
  • Still a small rating base (378 ratings), so the 4.5-star score may move

Pricing

Nail Salon: Nail Art Studio is free to download on Google Play (listed at 0 USD), and the Play listing confirms it contains ads. No subscription or paid edition is mentioned on the official listing, so the cost of playing is simply putting up with the occasional advert. At around 80 MB, it is also a reasonably light download that will not eat much storage.

Download Nail Salon on Google Play

The bottom line: Nail Salon: Nail Art Studio does one thing well — it lets you design pretty nails without stress. The tools are simple, the results look good, and the price is free. Expect ads and a short, gentle loop rather than a deep game, and you will enjoy it for exactly what it is: a relaxing little salon in your pocket, and one of the fastest-growing casual games of October 2026.
