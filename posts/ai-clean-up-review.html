AI Clean Up Storage Cleaner Review (2026)

Quick verdict — 3.5 / 5. AI Clean Up: Storage Cleaner does the core job well: it finds duplicate photos, large videos, similar shots and forgotten screenshots, and lets you review them before anything is deleted. That is genuinely useful, and it explains the app's fast rise. But the score is held back by a repeated complaint in user reviews — intrusive pop-ups and notifications that some users found hard to silence — and by a Play Data safety note saying your data cannot be deleted. Use it as a tidy-up tool, with notifications off, not as a set-and-forget cleaner.

AI Clean Up: Storage Cleaner, from developer Markcon Apps, is one of the fastest risers in Google Play's Productivity charts this month. According to AppBrain data updated on 25 October 2026, the app has reached about 1.4 million installs in total, with around 860,000 of those in the last 30 days — roughly 29,000 new installs a day. On the live Google Play listing it holds about 4.4 stars from over 6,000 ratings, and it ranks in the top 40 free Productivity apps in India and in the top 100 for Productivity in the United States. For an app that only launched on Google Play in July 2026, that is a very fast climb — and the reason it earns a review here this week.

The pitch is simple, and it is one almost every phone owner understands: storage fills up quietly, mostly with photos and videos you never meant to keep twice. AI Clean Up promises to find that waste for you. Its Play description puts it plainly: find duplicate photos, large files and unwanted media to clean up phone storage. The app then groups what it finds so you can keep the best copy and remove the rest, rather than hunting through your gallery one photo at a time.

How it works

Open the app and it scans your media and storage, then presents the results in categories. The promotional images show the main ones: Similar Photos, Videos, Screenshots, Delete Duplicates, Blurred Photos and Large Videos, each with the space it is taking up. That breakdown matters, because it tells you where the real waste is. On many phones the answer is not a pile of small files at all — it is a handful of large videos, or hundreds of near-identical burst shots from one afternoon.

The storage screen makes that point visually. It shows a single “Space to clean” total, broken down by Photos, Music and Videos, with a prominent Clean now button underneath. It is the kind of summary that helps you decide in seconds whether a tidy-up is worth doing, instead of guessing from the storage bar in your phone's settings.

Duplicates, done carefully

The duplicate finder is the feature most people will use first. The Play description says the Smart AI Cleaner recognises redundant media and organises it in a single place for a quick review, and that you have control over what remains or what is deleted. The screenshots back that up: the Duplicates screen groups matching photos together, marks one as “Best”, and lets you deselect individual shots before a bulk delete.

That review step is the most important part of the whole app, and it is worth slowing down for. A duplicate finder can only guess which copy is best — the sharpest, best-framed version is not always the one its algorithm picks. Check the group, keep the copy you actually want, and only then delete the rest. The same caution applies to similar photos: they are not always true duplicates, and a slightly different expression or framing may be the one you would rather keep.

Key features

Smart AI Cleaner. Scans for duplicate photos, identical images, screenshots and videos, and groups redundant media in one place for review.

Large file finder. Surfaces large photos and videos so you can see, in megabytes and gigabytes, what is actually eating your storage.

Similar photo finder. Groups near-identical shots together, so burst photos and repeated takes can be trimmed down to the best few.

Screenshot clean-up. Finds screenshots you may have forgotten after they served their purpose, and lets you review them as a batch.

Photo and video compressor. Shrinks large photos and videos to a smaller file size while keeping them usable, for files you want to keep but do not need at full size.

Compression is the quiet winner

Deletion gets the attention, but compression may be the more useful feature for many people. The screenshots show a video being compressed from an original 200 MB down to 50 MB. If you have videos you want to keep — a child's first steps, a holiday clip, a family gathering — deleting them is not the answer, and compression offers a middle path: keep the memory, give back three-quarters of the space.

Two cautions still apply. First, compression is lossy by nature: a smaller file is not identical to the original, so do not compress your only copy of something irreplaceable without keeping a backup elsewhere first. Second, the 200 MB to 50 MB example is a promotional illustration, not a promise for every video — your own savings will depend on the clip, its length and its quality.

Pros and cons

Pros: genuinely useful core tools — duplicates, similar photos, large files and screenshots in one place; review-before-delete design, with a Best marker to guide you; a clear storage breakdown so you can see where space is going; compression for files you want to keep; free to start; rated Everyone on Google Play; and real traction, with about 860,000 installs in the last 30 days.

Cons: the most repeated complaint in user reviews is intrusive pop-ups and notifications — several 1-star reviewers describe the app as disturbing, say it keeps popping up on screen, and say they struggled to silence it, with one reviewer calling it malware in frustration (that is a user's word, not a verified finding). The Play Data safety section also says the app may share location and app activity data with third parties, and that data cannot be deleted. Finally, like most cleaners, it cannot work miracles: if your storage is full of files you want to keep, no cleaner will free much space without you deleting or backing something up.

How to use it safely

If you try AI Clean Up, set it up on your terms. First, turn off its notifications in your phone's settings if you do not want reminders — that directly addresses the top complaint. Second, only grant the permissions it actually needs for the scan you want, and review every deletion group before confirming. Third, back up photos and videos you care about before a big clean-up, and use compression rather than deletion for anything irreplaceable. If the notifications still bother you after switching them off, uninstalling from your phone's Settings app is the clean break — and that experience, not the star rating alone, is the real test of any cleaner.

Pricing

AI Clean Up: Storage Cleaner is free to download on Google Play and contains ads, according to AppBrain's listing data. The Play listing checked for this review did not state an in-app purchase price range, so treat any paid options as optional extras and check the listing for current prices before paying. The download is about 81.68 MB, the current version is 1.0.28 (updated 22 October 2026), and the app has been on Google Play since July 2026. It is rated Everyone.

Download

Download AI Clean Up: Storage Cleaner on Google Play

If your phone is warning you about storage and your gallery is full of duplicates and forgotten screenshots, AI Clean Up is worth a careful try: the tools are real, the review step is sensible, and 860,000 installs in a month show a lot of people are reaching for exactly this kind of help. Just go in with notifications off, review before you delete, and back up what matters first.
