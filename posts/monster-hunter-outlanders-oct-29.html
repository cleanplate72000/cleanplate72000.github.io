Monster Hunter Outlanders Lands Oct 29

Capcom's mobile spin-off Monster Hunter Outlanders launches on iOS and Android on October 29, 2026. Developed by TiMi Studio Group under licence from Capcom, it is a free-to-play open-world survival hunting game made specifically for phones — not a port of a console game.

Why this matters: Monster Hunter is one of Capcom's biggest series, and this is its first full made-for-mobile hunting game, with classic monster hunting, crafting and exploration rebuilt for touchscreens.

Monster Hunter Outlanders: the key facts

  • Game: Monster Hunter Outlanders, developed by TiMi Studio Group under licence from Capcom
  • Launch date: October 29, 2026, on iOS and Android; a separate mainland China version follows in 2027
  • Price: Free to play, with in-app purchases; Adventurers are obtained through gacha-style pulls
  • World: Aesoland, an open world of biomes including forests, swamps and deserts, with a glider, climbing, swimming, a grappling hook, and a building system for launchers, ziplines and gliding facilities
  • Weapons: Seven weapon types listed at launch — Great Sword, Long Sword, Dual Blades, Hammer, Lance, Heavy Bowgun and Bow — plus the Charge Blade, confirmed by producer Dong Huang
  • Co-op: Real-time co-op for up to four players, with guilds and fixed teams; every adventurer is joined by a buddy, including the series' familiar Palico
  • Combat: Two control schemes, one for series veterans and one for mobile-first players, and no auto-combat
  • Pre-registration: Open on the App Store and Google Play, and PocketGamer.biz reports it has passed 5 million pre-registrations
  • Pre-launch device guidance: Android 12, 8 GB of RAM and a Snapdragon 888 processor or equivalent; iPhone 12 or newer running iOS 17 — beta-era guidance, so final store requirements may differ

What to watch next

Exact server opening times for each region have not yet been announced, so watch for those before launch day — and check which publisher's version applies in your region, since Garena publishes the game in parts of Southeast Asia while Level Infinite publishes the global version.
