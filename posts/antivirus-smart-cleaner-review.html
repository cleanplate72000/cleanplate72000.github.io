Antivirus Smart Cleaner Review (2026)

Quick verdict — 4.0 / 5. Antivirus Smart Cleaner packs a virus scanner, junk cleaner, large-file finder and photo tools into one free, Everyone-rated app — and its chart run is remarkable, with hundreds of thousands of downloads in its first weeks. The cleaning tools are genuinely useful, but the virus scanner deserves honest expectations, and some reviewers complain about repeated warnings and ads. Worth trying for the cleanup tools; do not treat it as a replacement for Play Protect.

Antivirus Smart Cleaner, from developer TOP TRADE, is one of the fastest-rising free tools on Google Play right now. On the Play Store it holds 4.3 stars from about 1,500 reviews, sits in the 500,000+ downloads bracket, and is rated Everyone. Independent tracking by AppBrain puts it even higher — around 680,000 installs, all of them in the last 30 days, averaging roughly 56,000 installs a day — with top-free Tools rankings including #11 in Mexico, #20 in Belgium, #28 in Indonesia and #36 in Brazil, plus #59 in the United States. For an app that only appeared on Google Play in September 2026 (current version 1.2.0.0, updated 29 September 2026), that is a breakout by any measure, and it is why it earns a review here.

The pitch is simple: one app to scan for threats, clear out junk and take back storage space. It is free to download, contains ads, and at about 31 MB it is a small install — far smaller than the junk it promises to clear. There is no desktop-style antivirus magic here, though, and this review keeps that distinction in view: on Android, a cleaner app can scan your apps and files and flag suspicious items, but it cannot reach into the system the way a desktop security suite can. Used with that expectation — a handy extra check, not a guarantee — the app makes a lot more sense.

How it works

The home screen is a single purple panel with a big Scan now button at its centre, surrounded by shortcuts to the main tools: Cleanup, Big file cleaning, WhatsApp Cleaner and Similar photos. Tap Scan now and the app checks your device for threats and junk; tap a shortcut and you go straight to that job. It is a layout built for people who want the result, not a settings maze — everything important is one or two taps away.

The junk cleaner is the workhorse. It sweeps residual and leftover files that pile up from everyday use and clears them in one tap, and the promo screenshots show a single clean freeing around 280 MB. That figure will vary wildly from phone to phone — a phone that has never been cleaned will free far more than one cleaned weekly — but the point stands: this is the tool most owners will actually use most often, and it is the easiest to judge. If your storage warning has been nagging you for weeks, this is the first button to press.

Key features

  • Virus scanner — scans the device for viruses and potential threats, with a one-tap scan from the home screen.
  • Junk cleaner — finds and removes useless residual files to free up phone storage in one tap.
  • Large file cleaner — previews bulky files you no longer need so you can remove them and reclaim space quickly.
  • Duplicate photos finder — locates similar or duplicate photos so you can cut redundant storage use.
  • Photo compression — shrinks picture file sizes without losing too much visual quality, according to the developer.
  • Photo privacy cleanup — erases hidden location information from photos before you share them.
  • Photo, video and audio manager — browse, preview and delete unwanted media files from one place, with sorting by type.
  • App manager — check all installed apps and uninstall the ones you rarely use to save device space.
  • WhatsApp cleaner — a dedicated shortcut, shown on the home screen, for clearing WhatsApp clutter.
  • Everyone rating — Google Play rates the app's content as suitable for everyone.

Why it is trending

Antivirus Smart Cleaner is trending because it answers the two storage complaints almost every Android owner has: “my phone is full” and “is my phone safe?” Bundling a scanner with the cleaners means one download covers both worries, and the 31 MB size means the cure is smaller than the disease. The numbers tell the story: from a September 2026 launch to roughly 680,000 tracked installs in a single month, with a 4.3-star average across 1,500 reviews, is the kind of growth that only happens when an app is solving a problem people actually feel every day.

The photo tools help too. Duplicate photos and oversized videos are usually the real storage hogs, and a large-file list that shows each file's size before you delete anything is far more useful than a blind “clean everything” button. Sorting media by type, compressing photos and stripping location data before sharing are small features individually, but together they make the app feel like a proper storage toolkit rather than a single-button gimmick.

Pros and cons

Pros
  • Remarkable breakout: ~680,000 installs in its first month, 4.3 stars from 1,500 Play reviews
  • One free app covers scanning, junk cleaning, large files, duplicate photos and media management
  • Small ~31 MB download, Everyone content rating, and a genuinely simple one-tap layout
  • Large-file list shows sizes before you delete, so cleanup stays under your control
  • Photo privacy cleanup strips hidden location data — a thoughtful, practical extra
Cons
  • Honest limit: an Android cleaner cannot remove system-level malware like desktop antivirus can — treat the scanner as an extra check, not a guarantee
  • Some Play reviewers report repeated warnings they found disturbing, and ads pushing other apps; the developer replied in October 2026 apologising and promising improvements
  • Contains ads, and the store page gives no in-app purchase details
  • Play's data-safety note says the app may collect app activity, app info and device IDs, and that collected data cannot be deleted
  • Like every cleaner, results vary: a well-kept phone will free far less than the headline figures suggest

Pricing

Antivirus Smart Cleaner is free to download on Google Play and contains ads. The current version is 1.2.0.0, updated 29 September 2026, the download is about 31 MB, and it is rated Everyone. Keep Play Protect switched on alongside it — Google's built-in protection and a cleaner app are complements, not substitutes, and no single app should be your only line of defence.

Download

Download Antivirus Smart Cleaner on Google Play

If your phone is crying out for space, Antivirus Smart Cleaner is an easy, free first try: a 4.3-star debut, a chart run across Mexico, Belgium, Indonesia and Brazil, and a toolkit that covers junk, large files and duplicate photos in one place. Just go in with clear eyes about the scanner and the ads, and check each file before you delete it.
