Yumtopia Review: Cozy Merge Cooking (2026)

Quick verdict — 4.5 / 5. Yumtopia: Merge & Cook is the cosiest breakout puzzle game of October 2026: a warm merge-and-cook loop, a family restaurant to rebuild, a cat called Momo, and — unusually for a free mobile game — no ads listed on its Play Store page. The trade-offs are real too: it is a hefty 230 MB download, its early tutorial hand-holding annoys some players, and its energy system will eventually slow you down. But for a relaxing, family-safe puzzle game, this is one of the best new finds of the month.

Yumtopia: Merge & Cook, from developer Playchamp Games, is a merge puzzle game with a story. Bella returns to her hometown to save her grandmother's restaurant legacy from a greedy industrial food corporation, helped by her chubby orange cat, Momo. On its official Google Play listing it holds 4.5 stars from 1,210 ratings, with Play showing 100,000+ installs; independent tracker AppBrain puts the total higher, at about 170,000 installs, including 110,000 in the last 30 days — roughly 3,600 installs a day — and ranks it number 4 in Top New Free Puzzle in the Netherlands and number 5 in India. It has an Everyone content rating, is about 230 MB, and its Play listing was last updated on 21 October 2026. It has been on Google Play since July 2026.

The core loop is the classic merge formula, given a cooking twist. You drag and combine simple ingredients on a board to discover more than 500 foods and tools, from secret tomato sauces to full dishes, then use them to complete customer orders. Each completed order moves Bella's story forward and earns the resources to rebuild and decorate the old family restaurant, corner by corner. Later, the journey goes global: the Play description promises trips to the USA, Mexico, France, Italy and beyond, each with its own themed ingredients, characters and restaurant designs.

The merge board is the heart of the game: combine ingredients, watch them upgrade, and feed the results into orders — with Momo the cat watching over your shoulder.

Key features

  • Merge and cook: drag and combine ingredients to discover 500+ foods and tools, from sauces to full global dishes.
  • Complete orders: serve customers, earn rewards, and push Bella's story forward one order at a time.
  • Restore and design: rebuild the old family restaurant and decorate it your way, then unlock themed restaurants around the world.
  • Travel the world: leave Bella's hometown for the USA, Mexico, France, Italy and beyond, each with new ingredients and characters.
  • A cozy story: a heartwarming tale about family, a childhood sweetheart, and the secrets behind grandmother's recipes.
  • Momo the cat: pet and feed Bella's loyal, chubby orange cat, who stays by your side throughout the journey.
  • No ads listed: Google Play does not list this game as containing ads — a genuine rarity among free puzzle games.

Orders are where merging pays off: Bella serves the dish, the customer is happy, and the restaurant rebuild moves one step closer.

Who is it for?

This is a game for players who like their puzzles relaxing rather than frantic. If you enjoy a merge board you can dip into for ten minutes, a story that is gentle rather than dramatic, and the satisfaction of watching a run-down restaurant slowly come back to life, Yumtopia fits that mood perfectly — and it pairs naturally with other calm puzzle games, like our Maze Out! review, if you want something more logic-driven between cooking sessions. If, on the other hand, you hate being told what to do, be warned: several Play reviewers complain about the pointing-finger tutorial that guides every early move, with one August 2026 reviewer saying it felt like playing the game for someone else. The developer replied that the guide disappears after level 15, and that it can be switched off in settings — though one reviewer said turning hints off did not stop it — so patience through the first levels is part of the deal.

Designing is the second half of the reward: the restaurant, its garden and its pool all take shape as you complete orders and travel.

Pros
  • Warm, cozy merge-and-cook gameplay with 500+ foods and tools to discover
  • Everyone rating, free to download, and no ads listed on Google Play
  • Real momentum: 110,000 installs in the last 30 days, top 5 new free Puzzle in the Netherlands and India
  • Reviewers praise the fair energy system compared with other merge games
  • A genuine story and a memorable cat, not just a bare puzzle board
Cons
  • Large download at about 230 MB — check your storage first
  • The early tutorial hand-holding frustrates some players until around level 15
  • Energy-based progression will eventually slow longer sessions
  • One reviewer reported a level 15 order not counting completed items — progress bugs can happen in young games
  • Play's data-safety section says the app may collect personal and financial information and that data is not encrypted, so treat account details with care

Pricing

Yumtopia: Merge & Cook is free to download on Google Play, and — notably — Google Play does not list it as containing ads, so there are no advert breaks between merges. Progression runs on an energy system, which reviewers describe as fairer than in many rival merge games, with one October 2026 reviewer specifically praising how the energy is earned. The official listing does not highlight a subscription, so the main cost of playing is patience: energy, and the 230 MB of storage the game needs on your phone.

Download Yumtopia: Merge & Cook on Google Play

The bottom line: judged on what it actually is — a cozy, family-safe merge puzzle with a real story, a restaurant worth rebuilding, and no ads getting in the way — Yumtopia: Merge & Cook earns its October 2026 breakout. Its rating is strong, its growth is fast, and its no-ads listing makes it an easy recommendation. Just clear 230 MB first, tolerate the tutorial through level 15, and you will get one of the warmest little puzzle games on Play right now.
