Playdigious Brings Subnautica to iPhone Duo

Playdigious has confirmed which of its mobile games will work with Apple's folding iPhone Duo, answering an early question for owners of the new dual-screen device.

Why this matters: the games are not just carried over — Playdigious says each one gets a revamped, optimised interface built for the Duo's dual-screen design, so nothing important should be lost on the fold.

Playdigious iPhone Duo games: the key facts

  • Confirmed games: Subnautica, Don't Starve Together: Pocket Edition, 9 Kings, Planet of Lana and Evoland 2, as reported by Pocket Gamer on October 26, 2026.
  • What changes: each confirmed game gets a revamped and optimised interface specifically for the iPhone Duo's dual-screen, folding design.
  • Who announced it: Playdigious, the publisher behind many of these mobile ports, is among the first publishers to confirm Duo compatibility for its games.
  • The device: the iPhone Duo is Apple's folding phone with a dual-screen design; Pocket Gamer puts its price at $2,000.
  • Why owners care: with a device at that price, knowing existing games still work — and are tuned for the new screen — matters before buying.
  • What this is not: this is a compatibility announcement for existing Playdigious games, not a new game launch or a new Duo-exclusive title.

Why it matters

New hardware only works if the games people already own come with it. By confirming Subnautica and Don't Starve Together alongside Planet of Lana, 9 Kings and Evoland 2 — and by reworking their interfaces for two screens — Playdigious gives Duo buyers a ready-made library on day one, rather than a wait for ports to catch up.

What to watch next

Watch for other publishers to follow with their own Duo compatibility lists, and for how the optimised interfaces actually feel in play. For more gaming news, see our The Captain Pocket Edition story and browse the Top 100 apps page.
