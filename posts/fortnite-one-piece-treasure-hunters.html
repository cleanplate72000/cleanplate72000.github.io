One Piece Joins Fortnite's Treasure Hunters

Epic Games has confirmed that One Piece is coming to Fortnite next season, ending weeks of fan speculation. The reveal came in a teaser on 24 October 2026, showing the Going Merry, the Straw Hat Pirates' first ship, emerging from the fog.

Why this matters: One Piece has been one of the most requested Fortnite collaborations, and this is the first time it has been officially confirmed for the game — as part of a treasure-hunting mini-season rather than a one-off shop drop.

Fortnite Treasure Hunters: the key facts

  • What: Fortnite's next mini-season, Treasure Hunters, with a One Piece collaboration confirmed
  • When: Treasure Hunters begins Sunday, 1 November 2026, after downtime, as Chapter 7 Season 4 comes to an end
  • How long: The mini-season should last about a month before a brand-new chapter begins
  • Theme: An anime-pirate mash-up built around treasure hunting
  • The teaser: The Going Merry emerges from the fog, confirming the collaboration is with the One Piece anime rather than the Netflix live-action series
  • Other crews teased: An in-game news feed image showed pirate crew logos including Peely, A$AP Rocky, Hatsune Miku, Kasane Teto and Lil Uzi Vert
  • Not yet detailed: The report does not list specific One Piece characters, skins or items, or whether they arrive at launch or later in the season

What to watch next

Watch for Epic's next announcements on exactly which One Piece characters and items are included, and how the treasure-hunting season plays out once it goes live on 1 November. For more on One Piece on mobile, see our ONE PIECE: Grand Gourmet launch story, and browse the Top 100 apps page.

Source: Polygon's report, "One Piece Is Officially Coming to Fortnite" (published 24 October 2026). Details as reported there; availability and details may change.
