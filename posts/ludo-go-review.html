Ludo Go! Review: Fresh Twist on Ludo (2026)

Quick verdict — 4.5 / 5. Ludo Go! is the rare board-game revival that respects the original: classic ludo rules, a fresh board on every level, and special tiles and piece abilities that keep each race tense. Just expect ads to pick up as you climb the levels, and a chunky 148 MB download for a board game.

Ludo Go! - Dice Board Games, from developer Easybrain, arrived on Google Play in July 2026 and has shot straight to the top of the board-game charts. It holds 4.9 stars from more than 14,000 ratings on Play, has passed 500,000+ installs, and AppBrain tracks around 430,000 of those installs in the last 30 days alone — roughly 14,000 installs a day. It is currently number 1 in Top New Free Board games in Canada, Germany, France, Japan, Australia, Brazil, Spain, the United Kingdom and Italy. For a game based on a board most people already know by heart, that is a remarkable launch.

The appeal is easy to explain: everybody already knows ludo, so there is nothing to learn, but Ludo Go! never lets a match feel like the same board twice. Every level brings a new layout, new bonus tiles and new ways to ruin your opponent's day. As the developer puts it: “Roll the dice and become the king of Ludo!”

How it actually plays

The rules are the ludo you grew up with. Roll the dice and move your pieces around the board; roll a 6 to leave the starting area and enter the race; land on an opponent's tile and their piece goes straight back to start; bring all your pieces home before your opponent to win the level. Choosing which piece to move is where the thinking lives — a safe advance, or a risky dash that might get you knocked back?

Where Ludo Go! earns its twist is in what it adds on top. Bonus tiles scattered around the board can move a piece forward, send it back, teleport it or block a route, and later levels add tricks players have spotted in reviews — slides like in Sorry, an assassin piece, and magnets that pull your nearest piece toward you. On top of that, pieces can unlock unique abilities, including extra life, armour and speed, and a Joker ability that lets a pawn move any number of spaces up to your roll. Each level is a new layout, so you cannot coast on one memorised route.

Why it is trending

Ludo Go! is trending because it takes a game whole families already play and gives it a reason to come back daily. The level structure is the key: instead of one endless board, you get a short, winnable race with a new layout every time, which suits quick sessions on the bus or the sofa. Easybrain is also a known quantity in casual games, and the polish shows — bright boards, chunky dice, and turns that resolve fast without fiddly menus.

The numbers back the buzz. A 4.9-star average across 14,000+ ratings is unusually high for a free game with ads, and the rating histogram is almost entirely five stars. Players in reviews compare it fondly to the Sorry board game they grew up with, and several long-time players say they like that they can play solo, without needing another person online, and still get a proper contest out of every level.

Key features

  • Classic ludo rules — roll, move, knock opponents back to start, and race all your pieces home first.
  • A new board every level — fresh layouts, bonus mechanics and special tiles appear as you progress, so no two races play the same.
  • Bonus tiles — tiles that move pieces forward, send them back, teleport them, block routes or trigger special effects.
  • Unique piece abilities — unlock extra life, armour, speed and the Joker, which can move any number of spaces up to your roll.
  • Fast, tactical turns — simple rules from the first roll, but every dice throw forces a real choice about which piece to move.
  • Parchis-style variety — classic ludo blended with parcheesi-style routes and traditions for a familiar but fresh feel.
  • Everyone rating — Google Play rates the game's content as suitable for everyone, so it works as a family game.
  • Solo-friendly play — level-based races you can enjoy on your own, without waiting for other players.

Pros and cons

Pros
  • Instantly familiar — anyone who knows ludo can start winning races within a minute
  • Outstanding reception: 4.9 stars from 14,000+ ratings and 430,000 installs in the last 30 days
  • New board layouts every level keep a classic game from going stale
  • Bonus tiles and piece abilities add real tactics without complicating the rules
  • Everyone content rating and solo-friendly levels make it a genuine family game
Cons
  • Ads ramp up as you progress — reviewers say ads are rare early on but start interrupting play from around level 5 to 7
  • A few long-time players report dice luck feeling skewed toward the opponent, and occasional mid-game crashes
  • At about 148 MB it is a hefty download for a board game — check your storage before installing
  • The listing pitches level-based races rather than live online matches, so don't expect real-time multiplayer against friends
  • Contains ads, with in-app purchases listed at $10.99 per item

Pricing

Ludo Go! - Dice Board Games is free to download on Google Play and contains ads, with in-app purchases listed at $10.99 per item. The current version is 1.4.0, updated 14 October 2026, and the download is about 148 MB. The ads are the main trade-off: reviewers say the first levels are nearly ad-free, then ads become more frequent — worth knowing before you settle in for a long session.

Download

Download Ludo Go! on Google Play

Go in expecting ads in the later levels and a biggish download, and you will find one of the friendliest board games on Play right now.
