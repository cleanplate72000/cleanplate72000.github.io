Juicy Journey Review: Merge & Cook (2026)

Quick verdict — 4.0 / 5. Juicy Journey: Merge & Cook is a cosy, no-ads merge game with a real story hook: Sera returns home with her daughter to rebuild the family restaurant on Kawa Island, and every merged dish brings a broken island back to life. It is free, it carries an Everyone content rating, Google Play lists 10,000+ installs, and AppBrain counts about 34,000 installs — all of them in the last 30 days. The catch is size and youth: at roughly 478 MB it is a hefty download for a casual game, and with only a few hundred ratings so far, the glowing average is promising rather than proven.

Juicy Journey: Merge & Cook, from developer FunTaste, arrived on Google Play in September 2026 and was updated to version 0.35.0 on 16 October 2026, with Halloween event updates in the latest changes. Its Play listing shows around 4.7 stars (about 180 ratings at checking time; AppBrain's crawl showed 4.6 from 141), and its rating histogram leans heavily five-star — 120 of the ratings AppBrain counted were five stars. That is a strong start for a game barely a month old, and the growth backs it up: AppBrain records roughly 4,000 installs a day over the last 30 days, all of its 34,000 total.

The story is what separates Juicy Journey from a plain merge board. After a painful betrayal, Sera comes home with her daughter to rebuild the family restaurant and start over — but years ago, a mysterious incident brought the island's famous food festival to an abrupt end. As Kawa Island comes back to life, long-buried secrets begin to surface. It is a warm, character-driven setup, and the cast — Sera, her neighbours, and a very expressive otter — gives the merging a reason beyond the next order.

The core loop will be familiar to merge fans, and it is satisfying: drag identical ingredients together to make better ones, fill customer orders, and unlock new ingredients as you go. Fruits, seafood, desserts and spices all feed into dishes, and the cooking tools — juicers, grills, ovens and steamers — turn simple ingredients into recipes inspired by cuisines from around the world. Earnings go back into the island: renovate restaurants, unlock beautifully themed areas, and turn a quiet island into a lively destination.

Two things deserve praise that many free games cannot claim. First, the listing carries no ads — AppBrain records no ads for this game, which is rare among free casual games and makes long, relaxed sessions genuinely relaxing. Second, the presentation is polished for a new release: bright island art, readable boards, and a Halloween event already running in its first month, which suggests the developer is actively supporting it rather than launching and vanishing.

Key features:

  • Merge hundreds of colourful ingredients — fruits, seafood, desserts, spices and more
  • Cook dishes from around the world with juicers, grills, ovens and steamers
  • Restore Kawa Island: renovate restaurants and unlock themed areas
  • A character-driven story — Sera's homecoming, the island's secrets, and the lost food festival
  • Halloween event updates in the latest version (0.35.0, 16 October 2026)
  • No ads, according to the Play listing data

Pros: warm story and characters; satisfying merge-and-cook loop; no ads; polished art; actively updated with seasonal events; free to start.

Cons: a hefty ~478 MB download; a small rating base, so the high average could still move; some early players report the game sticking on loading at 15%, tight energy limits with too many generators, and a repetitive loop; at least one player found the story slower to start than the pre-release promise suggested. If your phone is short on storage, or you dislike energy-gated play, both points are worth weighing before the download.

Pricing: Juicy Journey is free on Google Play. The listing material we checked shows a price of free and does not disclose in-app purchase prices, so treat any in-game shop as unverified until you open the current Play page — check the "in-app purchases" line there before spending anything. The core game — merging, cooking and restoring the island — is playable without paying.

If you like merge cooking games, our Yumtopia review covers another cosy merge-and-cook title with a restaurant of its own — Juicy Journey is the story-led, no-ads alternative, while Yumtopia leans harder on restaurant design.

Download Juicy Journey: Merge & Cook on Google Play

The bottom line: Juicy Journey earns its trending spot the honest way: a likeable story, a satisfying merge loop, and no ads interrupting either. The 478 MB size and the small, young rating base — plus a few early loading and energy complaints — keep it at 4.0 rather than higher, but as a free, Everyone-rated game that is clearly still growing, it is an easy recommendation for merge fans with room on their phone. Just check the Play page's current purchase details first, and give Kawa Island a chance to win you over.
