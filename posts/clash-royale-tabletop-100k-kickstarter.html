Clash Royale Tabletop Passes $100k Goal

Clash Royale: The Grand Arena, the tabletop version of Supercell's hit mobile strategy game, has passed $100,000 on Kickstarter. Supercell and Maestro Media launched the campaign on October 20, 2026, and it reached its original $25,000 funding goal in just one hour, raising $82,000 in that first hour alone.

Key facts

  • What it is: a tabletop game for two to four players, based on the mobile game Clash Royale.
  • Funding: $25,000 goal reached in one hour; $82,000 raised in that hour; over $100,000 raised at the time of PocketGamer.biz's report on October 22, 2026.
  • How it plays: each player picks a Champion and builds an eight-card deck around them. The Champion's evolution card stays with the player throughout the game.
  • The battle: players command forces, deploy cards, launch attacks and counter opponents across two lanes, managing their Elixir stocks as they fight to destroy Towers and collect Crowns. The player with the most Crowns wins.
  • Between battles: players can upgrade or add characters to their decks.
  • Designers: David Gordon, Ryan Rodriguez and TAM.
  • Stretch goals: further backing could unlock upgraded acrylic standees and other surprises.
  • Precedent: the game follows Maestro Media's Clash of Clans: The Epic Raid, which was backed on Kickstarter in 2025.

Why it matters

A four-times-funded campaign in its first days shows how far a mobile game can travel beyond the phone screen: Clash Royale's deck-building and Elixir management translate naturally to a table, and fans clearly want a version they can play face to face. It is also another sign of Supercell treating its biggest brands as worlds to play in more than one way, not just as apps. The honest caveat is that a Kickstarter total is backing, not a release date — no launch window for the finished tabletop game was given in the report.

What to watch next

Watch for the campaign's stretch-goal unlocks and, later, a production and delivery timeline for backers. For another Supercell story from this month, see our Squad Busters shutdown report.

Source: PocketGamer.biz, "Clash Royale tabletop game surpasses $100,000 on Kickstarter after reaching target in one hour" (published October 22, 2026), reporting Supercell and Maestro Media's Kickstarter campaign. Funding totals and stretch goals may change as the campaign continues — check the source and the official campaign page for the latest information.
