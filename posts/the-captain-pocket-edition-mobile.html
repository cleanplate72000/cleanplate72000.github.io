The Captain Pocket Edition Lands on Mobile

The Captain - Pocket Edition is out now on iOS and Android, bringing Sysiac Games' pixel-art sci-fi adventure to phones after its PC debut in 2021 and a Switch release a year later.

Why this matters: it is a full, choice-driven adventure with no in-app purchases — one £6.99 payment buys the whole journey home, not a drip-feed of extras.

The Captain - Pocket Edition: the key facts

  • Out now: on the App Store and Google Play for iOS and Android, as reported by Pocket Gamer on October 26, 2026.
  • Price: £6.99, with no in-app purchases.
  • You play Captain Thomas Welmu: a Spacefleet science officer stranded on the far side of the galaxy, carrying the one thing that can stop the forces heading for Earth.
  • The journey: make your way home, picking up crew and falling out with people along the way — and deciding which civilisations make it.
  • Genre: a pixel-art point-and-click comedy adventure, influenced by Star Trek: The Next Generation, Space Quest and Monkey Island, though some choices get fairly heavy.
  • Choices matter: each planet has its own dilemma with more than one way it can play out, so a first trip home will not be the only one.
  • Space battles: between stops there are battles, but you can slip past enemies and still reach the end — a very science-officer way to win.
  • Maker and publisher: developed by Sysiac Games (Peter Hedin and Benny Eriksson) and published by Tomorrow Corporation, of Little Inferno and Human Resource Machine fame.
  • History: Indie Fund backed the game back in 2017; it reached PC in 2021 and Switch a year later before this mobile edition.

Why it matters

Mobile ports of story games often arrive sliced into episodes or propped up by in-app purchases. The Captain - Pocket Edition does neither: a single price, no extras to buy, and a story built around decisions rather than reflexes. For players who like their adventures thoughtful, that combination is rarer than it should be.

What to watch next
