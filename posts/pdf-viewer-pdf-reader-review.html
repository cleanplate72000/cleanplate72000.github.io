PDF Viewer Review: All-in-One PDFs (2026)

Quick verdict — 3.8 / 5. PDF Viewer - PDF Reader is a genuinely useful all-in-one PDF toolkit: read, highlight, scan and convert documents without juggling four separate apps. It is free and growing fast — just expect ads, a mixed bag of user reviews, and a permissions footprint worth checking before you hand it your files.

PDF Viewer - PDF Reader, from developer BitCraft Tools, arrived on Google Play in July 2026 and has already passed 500,000+ installs — with around 530,000 installs tracked in total, about 230,000 of them in the last 30 days, averaging roughly 7,600 installs a day. On Google Play it holds 4.0 stars from about 680 ratings, and it has climbed into the top 100 free tools apps, reaching #43 Top Free Tools in Poland with top-200 showings in Australia, Norway, Austria, South Korea and Switzerland. For a document app barely three months old, that is a fast start — and the reason it earns a review here this week.

The pitch is simple: one app for everyday PDF tasks. The developer's own summary says it best — “View, annotate, scan, convert, and organize PDF documents with practical tools.” If your phone is where work files, study notes and scanned receipts all end up, that single-app promise is the whole appeal.

What it actually does

Start with reading, which is the core of the app. You can open PDF documents on your device, scroll continuously or move page by page, zoom in for fine print, jump straight to a selected page, search text inside a document, and read in full-screen mode. There is a choice of light and dark themes, and the store screenshots show a dark-mode toggle right in the reader — a small touch that matters a lot for late-night reading.

Then comes annotation. While reading, you can highlight important text, add notes and comments, and draw or write directly on PDF pages. That turns the app from a passive reader into a study and review tool: mark up a contract, annotate lecture notes, or flag the paragraph you need to come back to.

Scanning and converting

The scanner is the feature most likely to win you over. Point your phone camera at a printed document — a receipt, an invoice, a form, a page of notes — and the app detects the document edges automatically, lets you crop the pages, and saves the result as a PDF. The store screenshots show the scan view with Auto mode plus ID card and multi-page document options, which is exactly the setup you want for scanning both sides of a card or a multi-page form.

Conversion rounds it out. You can create PDF files from images, convert Word and Excel files to PDF, and convert a PDF back to an image or a long image for sharing. Add the organiser — sort files by name, size or recently opened, rename or delete documents, browse your saved PDFs — plus utilities to merge several PDFs into one, split a document apart, and share files by email, messaging or cloud storage, and you have a small document office in your pocket.

Why it is trending

PDF Viewer is trending because it bundles the five document jobs people actually do on a phone — read, annotate, scan, convert and organise — into one free download, instead of asking you to install a reader, a scanner and a converter separately. At roughly 7,600 installs a day over the last 30 days, it is growing the way only a genuinely practical utility does. The timing helps too: version 1.1.8-Bitcraft, released 20 October 2026, is fresh, and the app is still small enough at about 49 MB to try on a whim.

That said, the 4.0-star average tells a more mixed story than the download numbers. The rating histogram shows plenty of five-star and four-star reviews alongside a solid block of one-star reviews, and recent reviewers complain about heavy advertising, repeated update prompts, and one confused user who expected the app to play a video they were sent — which, to be fair, is not what a PDF reader does. Go in expecting a capable free tool with ads, not a polished premium product, and you will not be disappointed.

Key features

  • Flexible PDF reading — continuous scroll or page-by-page, zoom, jump to a page, in-document text search and full-screen mode.
  • Light and dark themes — a dark-mode toggle right in the reader for comfortable night reading.
  • Annotation tools — highlight text, add notes and comments, and draw or write on PDF pages.
  • Document scanner — automatic edge detection, page cropping, and saving scans as PDF, including ID card and multi-page modes.
  • Conversion tools — images to PDF, Word and Excel to PDF, and PDF to image or long image.
  • File organisation — sort by name, size or recently opened, rename, delete and browse saved PDFs.
  • PDF utilities — merge multiple PDFs, split documents, and share via email, messaging or cloud storage.
  • Everyone rating — Google Play rates the app's content as suitable for everyone.

Pros and cons

Pros
  • Five document jobs in one free app — reader, annotator, scanner, converter and organiser
  • Fast-growing and genuinely useful: 500,000+ installs, about 230,000 in the last 30 days
  • Scanner with automatic edge detection plus ID card and multi-page modes is a real standout
  • Dark mode, text search and jump-to-page make long documents easy to work through
  • Reasonable 49 MB download for everything it does, and an Everyone content rating
Cons
  • Contains ads, and several recent reviewers call the advertising heavy
  • Mixed reviews: a solid 4.0 average hides a noticeable block of one-star ratings
  • Recent users report nagging, looping update messages
  • Play's data safety section says the app may share files and docs with third parties and collect location and app activity — check the permissions before handing it sensitive files
  • It is a document tool, not a media player — do not expect it to play videos sent to you

Pricing

PDF Viewer - PDF Reader is free to download on Google Play, and it contains ads. The listing data checked for this review shows it as free with ads and no in-app purchase price range, so treat any paid offer you meet inside the app on its own merits and check it before accepting. The current version is 1.1.8-Bitcraft, updated 20 October 2026, and the download is about 49 MB — good value if the scanner and annotation tools click for you.

Download

Download PDF Viewer on Google Play

If you want one free app to read, mark up, scan and convert PDFs, PDF Viewer - PDF Reader is an easy one to try: a 4.0-star average, half a million installs, and a scanner that genuinely earns its place on your home screen. Just keep the ads and the data-sharing disclosure in mind, and check permissions before loading it with sensitive documents.
