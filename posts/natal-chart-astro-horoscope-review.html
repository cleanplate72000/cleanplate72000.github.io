Natal Chart Review: Birth Charts, Ads (2026)

Quick verdict — 3.5 / 5. Natal Chart: Astro Horoscope packs a genuinely deep astrology toolkit — detailed birth charts, synastry, transits and moon-phase tracking — into a free app that is growing fast. But frequent ads and premium-locked detail, reported by many players, drag the experience down, and beginners may find the sheer depth of chart language hard to navigate at first.

Natal Chart: Astro Horoscope, from developer DN Solutions, has been on Google Play since May 2026 and is having a remarkable autumn: AppBrain data updated 24 October 2026 puts it at about 3.8 million installs in total, with roughly 960,000 of those in the last 30 days — around 32,000 new installs a day. It is ranked in the top 100 free Lifestyle apps, including a top-50 showing in India. Its rating, however, tells a more divided story: about 3.9 stars from around 3,500 ratings on its Play listing. That split — huge growth, middling score — is exactly why this app deserves an honest review rather than a simple recommendation.

The core idea is straightforward. Enter your date, time and place of birth, and the app builds your natal chart: the positions of the planets at the moment you were born, laid out as a circular chart with houses, signs and aspects. From there it offers daily horoscopes personalised to that chart, compatibility readings for two people, and trackers for the Moon and the current planets. It is aimed at both curious beginners and seasoned astrology readers, and the feature list is far longer than a typical daily-horoscope app.

How it works

Everything starts with your birth data. The app asks for a name, date and time of birth and a birthplace — the example screens show a New York birthplace — and then calculates your chart. Your Sun sign, Moon sign and Rising sign are shown up front, with the full wheel underneath: house cusps, planetary positions, aspects between planets and planetary dignities, for readers who want the technical detail rather than just a one-line horoscope.

Daily content is then tied to that chart instead of to your Sun sign alone. The app also tracks transits — where the planets are right now compared with your birth chart — plus solar returns for your birthday year and lunar returns for each month. A live-sky view shows current planet positions and the current Moon phase, and a Moon sign calculator is built in. In other words, this is a chart-first app: the horoscope is a by-product of the chart, not the whole product.

Key features

  • Birth chart creator. Full natal charts from exact birth time and place, with house cusps, aspects and planetary dignities.
  • Sun, Moon and Rising signs. The three headline placements are shown clearly, so beginners can start there before digging into the full wheel.
  • Compatibility and synastry. Compare two charts for a compatibility score, use synastry to see how two charts interact, or build a composite chart for the relationship itself.
  • Transits, solar and lunar returns. Track current planetary movements against your chart, plus yearly solar-return and monthly lunar-return timing.
  • Live sky and Moon phase. Current planet positions in real time, Moon-phase calendars and a Moon sign calculator.
  • Personalised daily horoscopes. Daily zodiac readings framed around your own chart rather than a generic sign forecast.
  • Numerology and AI tools. Numerology readings alongside the astrology, plus AI-assisted chart and horoscope tools for extra interpretation.

Pros and cons

Pros: a much deeper toolkit than most free horoscope apps; birth charts, synastry, transits and Moon tracking in one place; a clean, attractive dark design; free to start; genuinely popular, with nearly a million installs in the last month alone.

Cons: players repeatedly report very frequent ads interrupting the app, with some reviews describing ads every few moments; several reviewers say fuller detail requires a premium subscription, so the free version can feel like a preview; the chart vocabulary — cusps, aspects, dignities, returns — can overwhelm beginners, and at least one reviewer found the app hard to use at first. And a fair warning that applies to every astrology app: the planetary positions are astronomical data, but the interpretations are not scientific evidence — enjoy the readings as reflection and entertainment, not as predictions to make serious decisions by.

Pricing and details

Natal Chart: Astro Horoscope is free to download on Android, rated Everyone, and about 61 MB — a modest download. It was last updated on 12 October 2026 (version 1.0.31 in AppBrain's latest data). It contains ads, and players report that a premium subscription unlocks fuller detail; the Play listing itself is free, so check the in-app subscription screen before paying for anything. If you like practical lifestyle companions as well as cosmic ones, also read our Sewpal sewing pattern and guide review, another free lifestyle app with a much calmer, ad-light feel.

Download

Download Natal Chart: Astro Horoscope on Google Play

If you already follow astrology and want your actual birth chart — not just a daily Sun-sign paragraph — Natal Chart: Astro Horoscope is one of the most complete free options on Play right now, and its growth shows plenty of readers agree. Just go in with eyes open: expect ads, expect some detail behind a subscription, and treat the forecasts as food for thought rather than fact. For casual star-gazers, a simpler horoscope app may be less work; for chart readers, this one earns its place on the home screen.
