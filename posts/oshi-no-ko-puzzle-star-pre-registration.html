Oshi no Ko Puzzle Star Pre-Registration Opens

NHN has opened global pre-registration for OSHI NO KO Puzzle Star, the first official puzzle game based on the popular TV anime OSHI NO KO. Players on iOS and Android can now sign up ahead of its global launch.

Key facts

  • What it is: OSHI NO KO Puzzle Star, a match-3 puzzle game from NHN, announced on October 20, 2026.
  • Platforms: Google Play and the Apple App Store, for mobile phones and tablets.
  • How it plays: connect and clear blocks of the same type, with different puzzle skills and presentations for each character.
  • Anime extras: a Memory Board lets players revisit key episodes from the original anime, alongside outfit coordination based on original costumes and game-original illustrations.
  • Languages: Korean, Japanese, English, and Simplified and Traditional Chinese, with the same title confirmed for all regions.
  • Launch rewards: all players will receive a B-Komachi Ai outfit and decoration items after launch, plus stage-clearing items and enough Diamonds for 10 gacha pulls.
  • Launch date: NHN has not announced a launch date yet — pre-registration is open now, with details on the game's official website.

Why it matters

Anime puzzle games live or die on whether they feel like the show, not just look like it. Puzzle Star leans into that with character-specific skills, episode Memory Boards and original outfits, rather than a generic match-3 reskin. For fans already following the anime — whose third season aired in early 2026, with a fourth season confirmed — pre-registering now also locks in the launch outfit and bonus items at no extra cost.

What to watch next

Watch for NHN to confirm the global launch date and whether pre-registration milestones unlock further rewards. For another pre-registration story, see our Sanrio Kawaii Me Live! pre-registration story and the Top 100 apps page.

Source: NHN via Business Wire, "NHN Begins Global Pre-registration for New Puzzle Game '[OSHI NO KO]Puzzle Star'" (published October 20, 2026), corroborated by Inven Global. Details may change — check the source and the official game website for the latest information.
