Scramble Knights Online Early Access Live

Scramble Knights Online launches in Early Access today, October 26, 2026, for Xbox Series and PC via Steam and the Microsoft Store. Developer Funktronic Labs announced the date for its massively multiplayer online game-lite action RPG, which is also arriving as a Game Preview title on Xbox Game Pass.

Key facts

  • Platforms: Xbox Series and PC via Steam and the Microsoft Store; also listed for Xbox Game Pass (Game Preview) on Series X|S, PC and cloud for Ultimate and PC Game Pass subscribers.
  • Launch content: the Early Access version ships with the 1-Star area fully accessible, with roughly five hours of unique content.
  • Early Access length: the game is expected to remain in Early Access for at least one year.
  • The full vision: the version 1.0 release will have at least 4-Star tiers of quests and areas, with each tier taking longer than the last, for around 40 hours of unique content in total.
  • The format: welcome to Scramble Knights Royale — a 32-player classic-adventure battle royale in which knights compete to be the last one standing and win the favour of the princess.
  • How a match starts: players jump off the starting boat and sail on the back of a turtle towards the shore, then explore an archipelago of puzzles, dungeons and treasures as the ring closes and forces every knight closer together.
  • Progression: find loot and complete quests to grow stronger, then spend gems at a blacksmith to upgrade weapons, armour and accessories and build a loadout for strategic combat.
  • Extras: a customisable Homestead, seasonal cosmetic rewards, online split-screen co-op, duo queue planned later this year — and if you die, you can watch the rest of the match as a chicken.
  • Price: listed at $12.99, with regional prices differing; the studio says there are no pay-to-win elements or paid progression plans right now, and the price is expected to rise as content is added.

Why it matters

Scramble Knights Online is a small-studio take on two crowded ideas — the MMO-lite adventure and the battle royale — and it stands out by making every run a short, quest-filled adventure rather than a plain last-player-standing shootout. Launching on Steam, Xbox and Game Pass at the same time also gives a multiplayer game like this its best shot at a healthy player base from day one, which matters far more for an online game than for a single-player launch. The honest caveat is the size of the starting offer: about five hours in the 1-Star area, with the promised 40-hour game still a year or more away.

What to watch next

Watch for the first big update, which the studio has planned for this winter after launch issues are dealt with, and for the promised roadmap showing how the higher Star tiers will arrive. Duo queue, planned for later this year, is another one to track for players who want to team up rather than go solo.
