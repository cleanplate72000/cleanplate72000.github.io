Aurora Gaming Win First-Ever MPL Crown

Aurora Gaming are MPL Philippines champions at last. The Mobile Legends: Bang Bang team beat Team Falcons 4-2 in the MPL Season 18 grand final on Sunday, October 25, at the PhilSports Arena, winning the franchise's first-ever domestic title.

Key facts

  • The result: fourth seeds Aurora Gaming beat second seeds Team Falcons 4-2 in a best-of-seven grand final.
  • The turning point: trailing 2-1, Aurora turned the series with surprise Estes picks in Games 4 and 5 — midlaner Kenneth "Yue" Tadeo in Game 4, then roamer Dylan "Light" Catipon in Game 5.
  • The finish: Jan "Domeng" Del Mundo's Clint delivered a late double kill in Game 6 to seal the title, and Domeng was named Finals MVP.
  • The road there: Aurora had already ended Team Liquid's three-season reign in the lower bracket final, after losing to Falcons in the upper bracket final and fighting back through the lower bracket.
  • Previous near-misses: this was Aurora's first MPL title after losing finals in Seasons 14 and 16.
  • What comes next: as reigning world champions, Aurora now head to Istanbul, Turkey, to defend their world title at the M8 World Championship.

Why it matters

Mobile Legends: Bang Bang is one of the biggest mobile esports in the world, and the Philippines is its heartland — so a first domestic crown for Aurora, the reigning world champions, is a major result, not a footnote. The series also showed how one bold draft pick can swing a final: the Estes selections in Games 4 and 5 broke Falcons' momentum and gave Aurora the platform for Domeng's match-winning Game 6. For Mobile Legends players, it is also a reminder that the game's competitive scene keeps producing new champions rather than the same winners every season.
