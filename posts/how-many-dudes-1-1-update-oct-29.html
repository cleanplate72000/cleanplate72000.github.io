How Many Dudes? Adds 7 Sports Dudes on Oct 29

How Many Dudes? is about to get a lot more sporting. Developer Butterscotch Shenanigans is adding seven new sports-themed dudes in the game's 1.1 update, arriving October 29 for all platforms.

How Many Dudes? 1.1 update: the key facts

  • The update: version 1.1, arriving October 29, as reported by Pocket Gamer on October 26, 2026.
  • Seven new dudes: Basketball, Football, Hockey, Tennis, Soccer, Coach and Broadcaster Dudes, each bringing their own sports-themed spin on the battles.
  • Challenge Mode: the update also adds a new Challenge Mode for players who want a tougher test.
  • Build tweaks: changes to the upgrades system promise more options for changing your build, including more ways to boost your stats.
  • The game so far: the chaotic battle sim has sold over 300,000 copies across multiple platforms, according to Pocket Gamer.
  • The premise: it builds on the viral question of how many ordinary people it would take to defeat a gorilla, then throws ever stranger opponents at your dudes, from wolves to gods.

Why it matters

Most games built on a viral joke fade fast. How Many Dudes? has done the opposite: it turned one absurd question into a full roguelike strategy game, sold hundreds of thousands of copies, and is still adding to it. A sports-themed set of fighters is a neat fit, too — it gives every player a recognisable favourite to throw into the chaos, and Challenge Mode gives returning players a reason to come back.

What to watch next

Watch for the 1.1 update landing on October 29 and for players' verdicts on Challenge Mode and the new dudes. For more on games reaching new audiences, see our Miyamoto Order of Culture story, and browse the Top 100 apps page.
