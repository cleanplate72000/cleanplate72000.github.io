Legends of Amberland II Hits Mobile Nov 5

Legends of Amberland II: The Song of Trees is coming to iOS and Android on November 5, 2026, developer Silver Lemur Games' follow-up to the first game's mobile release back in August.

Why this matters: a premium, one-time-purchase RPG is still a rarity on phones — and this one arrives with a full party, a ship to sail, and quests you can tackle in any order.

Legends of Amberland II on mobile: the key facts

  • Game: Legends of Amberland II: The Song of Trees, a first-person, turn-based dungeon crawler inspired by 1990s PC role-playing games.
  • Release date: November 5, 2026, on the App Store and Google Play.
  • Price: free to start, then a one-time $9.99 purchase to unlock the full game.
  • Party: build a party of up to seven heroes, hand-picking their classes and races or taking a pre-made group.
  • Exploration: move square by square with 90-degree turns; buy a ship and sail to uncharted islands.
  • Story: a new, self-contained story, so you do not need to have played the first game.
  • Style: a light, fairytale-like tone, with quests that can be tackled in any order, swift fights and quick travel to cut backtracking.
  • Background: The Song of Trees first released on PC in December 2023; a third entry, The Crimson Tower, reached Steam in March 2026.

Why it matters

Mobile RPGs are usually built around ongoing purchases, so a complete adventure for a single $9.99 unlock stands out — especially one that lets you run a seven-hero party and sail between islands instead of following a fixed path. Because the story is self-contained, new players can jump straight in, while returning players get the new ship-based exploration on top of the first game's dungeon crawling.

What to watch next

Watch for the store listings to go live ahead of November 5, 2026, and check the App Store or Google Play page for your device before buying the unlock. For more gaming news, see our Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis teaser story and browse the Top 100 apps page.
