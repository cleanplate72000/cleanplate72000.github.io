AI Collage Maker Review: Fast Frames (2026)

Quick verdict — 4.0 / 5. AI Collage Maker & Photo Frame does exactly what its name promises: it turns a handful of everyday photos into a tidy, shareable collage in a couple of minutes, with AI-arranged layouts and plenty of frames and templates to choose from. It is free, carries an Everyone rating, and is only about 26 MB. The caveats matter too: it contains ads, some of its promotional screenshots push AI video features rather than collages, and at least one unhappy reviewer says the app did not match what its advertising promised — so go in expecting a quick collage maker, not a full photo studio.

AI Collage Maker & Photo Frame, from developer ATQ - AI Lab, is one of the fastest-growing photography apps of October 2026. On its official Google Play listing it holds 4.5 stars from 19,892 ratings, with Play showing 1,000,000+ installs; independent tracker AppBrain puts the total higher, at 4.9 million installs, including 1.3 million in the last 30 days — roughly 42,000 installs a day — and ranks it number 1 in Top Free Photography in both Denmark and India, and number 13 overall among free apps in India. It has an Everyone content rating, is about 26 MB, requires Android 7.0 or higher, and its current version is 1.6.0, updated on 11 September 2026. It has been on Google Play since March 2026.

The pitch is simple, and the Play description sticks to it: turn everyday photos into eye-catching collages with AI, in just a few taps. You pick your photos, the app's AI arranges them into a layout, and you finish the result with a frame or template before saving or sharing it. It is aimed squarely at social posts, memories and stories — the kind of collage you make on the bus and post before you get off, rather than a project you spend an evening perfecting.

The app's own promo art shows the core idea at a glance: several photos arranged in grid, heart and circle frames, ready to share.

The Play Store description breaks the experience into five ideas. First, an AI photo collage maker: smart AI arranges your images into a layout, so you do not have to drag every photo into place yourself. Second, stylish photo frames: modern frame styles in heart, circle, grid and other creative shapes, meant to suit every moment and occasion. Third, trendy templates and layouts: ready-made, beautifully designed templates you can drop photos into, with fresh styles added over time. Fourth, easy and fast editing: a simple interface with drag-and-drop photos, plus control over spacing, layout and style, so a collage takes seconds rather than minutes. Fifth, save and share instantly: export a high-quality image, save it straight to your gallery, and share it on social platforms from there.

The template screen is where most people will start: a featured collage at the top and a grid of popular templates below it, each ready to fill with your own photos.

Key features

  • AI-arranged collages: the app suggests a layout for your photos instead of leaving you with a blank grid.
  • Frames in many shapes: heart, circle, grid and other creative frame styles for different occasions.
  • Ready-to-use templates: designed layouts for quick creations, from summer and travel themes to everyday memories.
  • Drag-and-drop editing: move photos between frames and adjust spacing, layout and style.
  • Built for sharing: export high-quality images, save to your gallery, and share to social apps.
  • Small and light: about 26 MB, and it runs on Android 7.0 and up, so it fits older phones too.

The My Gallery screen keeps your finished collages in one place, so a layout you liked is easy to find and re-share later.

Who is it for?

This is a game of two halves, and it helps to know which half you want. If you want a fast way to bundle holiday snaps, birthday photos or a set of couple and family memories into one tidy image, this is exactly the tool for the job — and it pairs naturally with a fuller editor for the photos themselves, like our Picsart review, which covers a much deeper photo-editing app. If, on the other hand, you are drawn in by the app's AI video promos — the dancing, football-star and memory-video clips that fill several of its store screenshots — be careful: those are a different feature from the collage maker at the heart of the app, and one September 2026 reviewer complained bluntly that every screen was an ad until the home menu, and that the app did not offer what its advertising had promised. That is a single voice among nearly 20,000 ratings, and the overall 4.5-star score says most users are happy — but it is a fair warning to judge the app on its collages, not on its flashiest adverts.

Pros
  • AI layouts make a good-looking collage in seconds
  • Plenty of frames and templates, including heart, circle and grid shapes
  • Everyone rating, free to download, only about 26 MB
  • Huge recent growth: 1.3 million installs in the last 30 days, number 1 in Photography in India and Denmark
  • Simple drag-and-drop editing and instant gallery saving
Cons
  • Contains ads — one reviewer found them heavy before reaching the home screen
  • Store screenshots also push AI video features, which can set the wrong expectation
  • A collage maker, not a full photo editor: no deep retouching here
  • Rating is strong but the app is still young, on Play only since March 2026

Pricing

AI Collage Maker & Photo Frame is free to download on Google Play (listed at 0 USD), and the listing confirms it contains ads. That is the whole cost of using it as far as the official listing goes: no subscription or paid edition is highlighted in the app's Play description, so the trade-off is simply putting up with adverts in exchange for a free collage maker. At about 26 MB it is also a light download, so it will not eat much storage or data.

Download AI Collage Maker & Photo Frame on Google Play

The bottom line: judged on what it actually is — a quick, free, family-safe collage maker with AI layouts and a good template library — AI Collage Maker & Photo Frame earns its place among October 2026's breakout photography apps. Its growth is real, its rating is strong, and its 26 MB size makes it an easy try. Just go in for the collages, expect ads, and you will get a genuinely useful little app for turning a camera roll into something worth sharing.
