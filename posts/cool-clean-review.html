Cool Clean Review: The Simple Storage Cleaner Climbing the Charts (2026)

Quick verdict — 4.0 / 5. Cool Clean does one job with no fuss: it shows you how full your phone is, finds files you probably do not need, and lets you clear them in a few taps. It will not transform your phone, but for anyone whose storage warning keeps popping up, its simplicity is the whole point.

Cool Clean is the breakout tools app from SkuppyWuppies, launched on Google Play in October 2026. In roughly two weeks it passed 100,000 installs — about 16,000 a day over its first month on the store — and climbed into the Top New Free charts in several countries, including #2 in Switzerland, #3 in Indonesia and #4 in Japan. It holds around 4.1 stars from more than 440 ratings and carries an Everyone content rating. That is a fast, clean start for a utility with no big brand behind it, and it is worth asking what it actually does — and what it does not.

All screenshots in this review are the developer's own promotional images from the official Google Play Store listing.

What Cool Clean does

The idea is deliberately small. Open the app and you get a clear storage readout — the listing's screenshots show a phone at 16% storage used and 66% RAM used — with a big Clean button underneath. Cool Clean checks your device storage, finds unnecessary files, and lets you clean the ones you select. There is no maze of menus to learn first, no account to create, and no long setup: the developer's own description stresses a simple, clear interface and a lightweight app, and that is exactly what the screenshots show.

That restraint matters, because storage cleaners live or die on trust. An app that promises to speed up your phone by magic, or that deletes things without showing you what they are, deserves suspicion. Cool Clean's approach is the opposite: it presents itself as a tidy-up tool, not a miracle cure. You check storage, you see what it found, and you clean what you choose. Used that way — clearing obvious junk and files you recognise — it is a genuinely handy tool for phones that are always running low on space.

Key features that matter

  • Storage check at a glance — see how much storage (and RAM) is in use without digging through Android's settings menus.
  • Finds unnecessary files — the app surfaces files you can safely clear, rather than asking you to hunt for them yourself.
  • Clean only what you select — you stay in control of what gets removed, which is the right way round for a cleaner.
  • App Manager — sort your installed apps by last used, size, install time or name, and uninstall the ones gathering dust.
  • Screenshot Cleaner and Notification Cleaner — extra shortcuts shown in the listing's screenshots for two of the most common sources of clutter.
  • Network Traffic view — a simple way to see what is using data, shown as a home-screen shortcut.
  • Lightweight and simple — around 39 MB, version 1.1.0 (updated 15 October 2026), with a clean design that does not get in its own way.

Who it is for

Cool Clean is an easy recommendation for anyone with a budget or older phone that is always complaining about storage, and for anyone who likes their phone tidy but does not want to spend an evening learning a complicated utility. Parents and less technical users will appreciate the one-button approach: open it, look at the numbers, tap Clean. It is also a sensible first stop before you start deleting photos or uninstalling apps you actually use — a quick clean of genuine junk often frees up more room than people expect.

It is not, however, a tool for power users who want deep system controls, scheduled deep-cleaning, battery optimisation or detailed file analysis. If you already manage your storage confidently through Android's own settings, Cool Clean will feel basic. And a word of honest caution that applies to every cleaner: do not let any app delete files you do not recognise, and do not expect a cleaner to make an old phone feel brand new. Android already manages cached files on its own; a cleaner helps with clutter, it does not rewrite your hardware.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Dead simple: storage readout, Clean button, done
  • 100,000+ installs in about two weeks, with top-new-free chart spots
  • App Manager makes it easy to spot and remove forgotten apps
  • Lightweight at around 39 MB, Everyone rating, free to download
  • You choose what to clean — no mystery deletions

Cons

  • Feature set is deliberately thin — power users will want more
  • 4.1 stars is good, not stellar, and the app is still very new
  • Will not speed up an old phone the way some cleaner ads imply
  • Long-term reliability is unproven after only a few weeks on the store

Pricing

Free to download on Google Play, with no upfront price shown on the store listing. As always, check the official store listing for the current details before downloading, since free apps sometimes add purchases later.

Alternatives worth knowing

  • Android's built-in storage settings — free, already on your phone, and enough if you only need an occasional clear-out.
  • Maze Out! - Tap Puzzle — if you came for something fun instead, our Maze Out! review covers another fast-rising 2026 hit, this time a puzzle game.

Final verdict

Cool Clean is the rare trending utility that is exactly what it promises: a simple, lightweight way to see what is filling your phone and clear the clutter. It earns its 4.0 out of 5 not by doing everything, but by doing its one job clearly and without fuss — and 100,000 installs in two weeks suggests a lot of people were waiting for exactly that. If your phone is always running low on space, this one is worth a try — grab it from the official Play Store link below, never from a third-party APK site.
