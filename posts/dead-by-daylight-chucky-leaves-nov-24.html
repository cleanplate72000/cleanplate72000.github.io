Chucky Leaves Dead by Daylight on Nov 24

Behaviour Interactive has announced that the Chucky Chapter will leave Dead by Daylight stores on November 24, 2026. From that date, the Chapter and its associated content will no longer be available to buy from in-game or first-party stores — though anything players have already unlocked will stay usable in the game.

Why this matters: Chucky is the third big licensed horror character to leave Dead by Daylight stores in under two years, after Hellraiser's Pinhead in 2025 and Halloween's Michael Myers in January 2026. For players, the message is simple: if you want the killer doll, the window to buy him closes on November 24.

Chucky's Dead by Daylight exit: the key facts

  • Removal date: November 24, 2026 — the Chucky Chapter and its associated content leave in-game and first-party stores
  • What stays: Any Chucky content unlocked before that date remains available to use in the game
  • What goes: The Killer, known in-game as The Good Guy, will no longer be available to buy, and his outfits and cosmetics will also leave sale; no future add-ons are planned
  • Support continues: Bug fixes for the character will still be supported after the removal
  • Last Chance sale: Behaviour is cutting prices on Chucky Chapter items by 50% from Monday, October 26, running until the November 24 removal date
  • How long he lasted: Chucky joined Dead by Daylight in 2023, so the Chapter is leaving the store nearly three years after its arrival
  • Why he is leaving: Behaviour has not stated a reason; fans often assume licensing is behind such removals, but that has not been confirmed in this case

What to watch next

Watch whether Behaviour names any replacement content around the removal date, and whether other long-running licensed Killers face the same fate. Dead by Daylight is a horror game for older players. For more gaming news, see our Home 14th Anniversary update story and the Top 100 apps page.
