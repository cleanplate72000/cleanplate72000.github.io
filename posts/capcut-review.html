CapCut Review: Is It Still Worth It in 2026?

CapCut is the video editor behind most of the short videos you scroll past every day. Made by Bytedance, it has passed 1 billion downloads on Google Play and currently sits at #1 in the Top Free Video Players & Editors chart in the US. But its Play Store rating is only around 3.5 stars from over 12 million ratings — so is CapCut actually good, or just popular? We tested the free version to find out.

Our verdict: 4.0/5. For free short-form video editing, nothing else on Android comes close — templates, auto captions and a huge effects library, all free to start. The 3.5-star store average is a fair warning that premium features sit behind Pro, but the free editor alone is genuinely powerful.

CapCut at a glance

  • Developer: Bytedance Pte. Ltd.
  • Price: Free, with optional Pro subscription (in-app purchases)
  • Downloads: 1,000,000,000+ on Google Play
  • Rating: ~3.5/5 from 12+ million ratings
  • Size: ~193 MB  |  Requires: Android 6.0 and up
  • Last updated: 19 October 2026

Key features that make CapCut trending

Templates. Thousands of ready-made templates turn your clips into trending-style edits in one tap — the main reason CapCut dominates TikTok and Reels editing.

Auto captions. CapCut generates subtitles from speech automatically, with styling options that match viral video trends.

AI tools. Features like AI translation (shown below), background removal, and text-to-speech keep the app ahead of most free rivals.

Full timeline editing. Multi-track timeline, chroma key, speed curves, filters, effects, stickers and a huge music library — desktop-style tools on your phone.

Free vs Pro: what do you actually pay for?

The free version covers the core editor, most templates, captions and effects — enough for most creators. Pro unlocks premium effects, templates, cloud storage and removes restrictions on some AI features. If you edit daily for an audience, Pro is worth considering; casual creators rarely need it.

Pros
  • Best free template library for short-form video
  • Auto captions and AI tools included free
  • Powerful multi-track timeline, chroma key, speed controls
  • 1B+ downloads means tutorials for everything
Cons
  • Best effects and templates increasingly Pro-only
  • ~193 MB download, heavy on low-end phones
  • 3.5-star rating shows many users hit paywalls

Who should download CapCut?

Download it if you make TikTok, Reels or Shorts, or you want a free editor that grows with you. Skip it if you only trim the occasional clip — a lighter editor like YouCut will feel faster. CapCut FAQ

Is CapCut free? Yes — the core video editor is free on Google Play, with an optional Pro subscription for premium features.

Is CapCut safe? CapCut is published by Bytedance on the official Play Store with over a billion installs. As with any editor, check the permissions it requests in your device settings.

Does CapCut work for TikTok and Reels? Yes — it is built for short-form vertical video, with templates and caption styles designed for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

What phones run CapCut? Android 6.0 and up, but a mid-range or better phone runs it far more smoothly given its ~193 MB size.
