Once Human's Isles of Abyss Update Is Out Now

Starry Studio's survival shooter Once Human has set sail with its Isles of Abyss update, out now on mobile as well as PC and consoles. The update swaps the mainland for open water, with a sentient, haunted ocean to explore, survive and fight back against.

Why this matters: Isles of Abyss is billed as Once Human's biggest update yet, and it changes how the game plays at a fundamental level — ships, sea hazards and server-wide boss phases replace the usual land-based survival loop, giving returning players a genuinely new world to tackle.

Once Human Isles of Abyss: key facts

  • Out now: the Isles of Abyss update is live, reported by Pocket Gamer on October 22, 2026
  • Platforms: available on iOS and Android, as well as PC and consoles
  • Ships: climb a nautical tech tree from simple sail-powered rafts all the way to massive battleships
  • Hazard zones: five new zones packed with dangers you must learn to resist before you can push further
  • Phase one bosses: the Towers of Calamity, massive biomechanical lighthouses infesting each region, which need teamwork to bring down
  • Phase two: new bosses and random Corruption Zones appear as players across the server make progress
  • Finale: the Sunken Lord, an enormous island-sized boss fought in a four-player co-op raid
  • Seasonal events: new events tie into 2027's Green Game Jam, including the Halloween-themed "Halloween Meets the Abyss" festival

What to watch next

Team up before you head out — the Towers of Calamity and the Sunken Lord raid are built for co-op, so solo sailors will have a much harder time. Keep an eye on the server-wide phase progress too, since later bosses only appear once the community has pushed through earlier phases.
