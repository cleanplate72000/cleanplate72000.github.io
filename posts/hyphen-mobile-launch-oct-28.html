Hyphen Brings Neon Mazes to Mobile Oct 28

Hyphen, the precision action-puzzle game about guiding a constantly rotating stick through neon mazes without touching anything, launches on mobile on 28 October 2026. Developer FarSpace Studios (Aremtec Group Ltd) announced the mobile launch on 26 October 2026, with the game arriving on iPhone, iPad and Android.

Hyphen on mobile: the key facts

  • Release date: 28 October 2026, as announced on 26 October 2026.
  • Platforms: iPhone, iPad and Android, alongside a Windows version on Steam.
  • The premise: guide a constantly rotating stick through intricate neon mazes, avoiding walls, moving obstacles, enemies and traps — do not touch anything.
  • Mobile content: all 36 handcrafted campaign levels, two unlockable minigames and an original 11-track soundtrack composed by Andrew Lloyd.
  • Controls: fully analogue touchscreen joystick controls adapted for phones and tablets, with controller support where available.
  • Community levels: shared across platforms — PC players create levels with the desktop Level Editor and publish them through Steam Workshop, and mobile players can discover and play those community challenges.
  • Price: a one-time £3.99 purchase on mobile, with no adverts, subscriptions or microtransactions.
  • The backstory: originally released on PC in 2015, Hyphen was unavailable for around seven years after its original company closed, before returning to Steam in 2026; the mobile editions are rebuilt using MonoGame, with visual improvements and the original gameplay preserved.
  • Windows update: on the same day, existing Steam owners get a free MonoGame update, and the Steam price drops from £15.25 to £7.99.

Why it matters

Hyphen is a rare thing on mobile: a premium puzzle game with no ads and no microtransactions, where the whole challenge is timing and patience rather than a shop. The cross-platform community levels also give it a long tail — the 36 campaign levels are only the start, since PC players keep building new mazes for mobile players to try. For anyone who likes a game that is easy to explain and hard to master, this is one to watch.
