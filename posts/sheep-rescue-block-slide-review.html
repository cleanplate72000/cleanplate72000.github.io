Sheep Rescue Review: Slide Puzzles (2026)

Quick verdict — 3.5 / 5. Sheep Rescue: Block Slide is a cute, genuinely clever sliding-block puzzle: slide the blocks, wall off the wolves, and watch the flock trot to safety. It is easy to pick up, free, and rated Everyone — just expect frequent ads, a still-small and divided rating base, and a chunky 120 MB download.

Sheep Rescue: Block Slide, from developer Lumos Play, arrived on Google Play in October 2026 and has already gathered 50,000+ installs in the Play bracket, with about 62,000 installs tracked in total, all in the last 30 days. On Google Play it holds about 4.1 stars from just under 600 ratings at the time of writing, and AppBrain's October 2026 snapshot put it at 3.9 stars from around 430 ratings — a reminder that this is still a young game whose average is moving. Its best chart position so far is #8 in Top New Free Puzzle in Saudi Arabia, with top-25 Puzzle placings in Mexico, Canada and France as well. That is real traction for a game barely a few weeks old, and it is why it earns a review here — with its rough edges stated plainly, not smoothed over.

How it plays

The premise is simple and immediately readable. The peaceful farm is in danger: hungry wolves are prowling around the fences, and the fluffy sheep are trapped. Your job is to slide blocks around the board to form solid barriers against the predators, opening a safe path so each sheep can escape through the gate. Every layout is its own little logic problem — slide the wrong block first and you may wall yourself in, while the right order clears the board cleanly.

There are no time limits, which is the game's best design decision. You can study the board, try a move order in your head, and change your mind without a countdown breathing down your neck. Levels start simple and grow progressively harder, so the first boards teach you the sliding mechanics and later ones genuinely test your spatial reasoning. The developer describes it as a mental workout, and that is fair: this is a planning puzzle, not a reflex game, and it suits short sessions on a commute or a quiet evening at home just as well as a longer sit-down.

Key features

  • Slide-block barriers: move blocks to trap wolves and free the flock — a fresh twist on the escape-puzzle genre with an adorable farm theme
  • No time limits: plan each board at your own pace, with no countdown pressure
  • Progressive difficulty: early levels teach the mechanics, later levels demand real forward planning
  • Charming farm presentation: vibrant visuals, cute cartoon characters and relaxing sound effects
  • Logic and spatial reasoning: every board rewards thinking a few moves ahead rather than fast fingers

What players actually say

The rating picture is divided, and that division is worth reporting honestly. On the positive side, one October 2026 reviewer called it a puzzle IQ game they really enjoy playing. On the other side, a two-star review from the same month praised the game's uniqueness — sheep as the blocks you navigate while wolves try to stop you — but complained that after five levels there are ads after every level, calling the ad rate greedy given that some levels take only about ten seconds to complete. AppBrain's rating histogram backs up the split: alongside over 200 five-star ratings sit a notable minority of one-star ratings. In other words, people who like the core puzzle tend to like it a lot, and people who bounce off it mostly bounce off the ads, not the gameplay.

Pros and cons

  • Easy to learn, genuinely clever block-sliding puzzles with a charming farm theme
  • No time limits, so it stays relaxing even as boards get harder
  • Free to download and rated Everyone, so it is safe to hand to younger players
  • Real early traction: 50,000+ installs and top-25 new Puzzle chart placings in several countries
  • Frequent ads — reviewers report an ad after every level once you pass the early stages
  • A divided rating base: a meaningful minority of one-star ratings alongside the five-star fans
  • Still a young game with a small rating base, so the average may move as more players arrive
  • About 120 MB — a fair size for a puzzle game, so download on Wi-Fi if you can

Pricing

Sheep Rescue: Block Slide is free to download on Google Play, and it contains ads. The listings checked for this review showed no in-app purchase price range, so treat any paid offer you meet inside the app on its own merits and check it before accepting. The current version is 1.1.3, updated 21 October 2026, and the download is about 119.8 MB — worth the space if the block-sliding loop clicks for you, but a chunky install for a casual puzzle.

Download

Download Sheep Rescue on Google Play

If you like a puzzle that rewards observation and planning over speed, Sheep Rescue: Block Slide is an easy recommendation with one clear warning attached: the puzzle itself is charming and clever, but the ads arrive thick and fast. Go in expecting that trade-off and you will enjoy freeing the flock.
