Broken Ranks Launches Worldwide on Android

Whitemoon Games has launched its free-to-play isometric MMORPG Broken Ranks worldwide on Android. The game is available now on Google Play, bringing its dark-fantasy world of Taern to phones for the first time alongside its existing PC version.

Key facts

  • Platforms: Android now, alongside PC. An iOS version is planned, but Whitemoon has given no release date for it yet.
  • Price: free to play on Google Play, with no ads in the game, according to the studio's mobile FAQ.
  • One world, one account: PC and Android players share the same servers and the same world, and a single account carries full progress across both platforms, with no separate economies.
  • New servers: the Android launch adds two new international servers, one European and one American, with more to follow if player numbers grow.
  • Pre-registration reward: players who pre-registered on Google Play get a free fur cap cosmetic in their backpack when they first log in.
  • The game itself: a turn-based MMORPG with tactical combat, quests, professions and guilds, in a grim, story-driven fantasy setting.
  • Right now: the in-game Forefather's Eve event runs from October 26 to November 2, so new Android players arrive just as it begins.

Why it matters

Most mobile MMORPGs are built for phones first and kept apart from their PC versions. Broken Ranks does the opposite: the phone version joins the same world, with the same progress, as the PC game that has run since 2022. For players who like slow, tactical role-playing games rather than fast auto-battlers, that makes this one of the more serious mobile MMORPG launches of the year. Note that it is a dark fantasy game with combat, so younger players should check the store age rating first.

What to watch next

Watch for the iOS release date, which is still unannounced, and for Whitemoon's planned move into Asian markets, with Korea and Japan named as target regions. If the new international servers fill up, the studio says it will add more gradually. Until then, Android players can start in Taern today and carry that same character over to PC whenever they like — see also our Monster Hunter Outlanders story for another big mobile role-playing launch arriving this week.
