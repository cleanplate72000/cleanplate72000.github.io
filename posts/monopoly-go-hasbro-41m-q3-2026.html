Monopoly Go Made Hasbro $41m in Q3 2026

Monopoly Go earned Hasbro $41 million in the third quarter of 2026, bringing the toy company's income from Scopely's mobile hit to $127 million for the first nine months of the year.

Why this matters: Monopoly Go is now a steady, repeatable earner for Hasbro — its quarterly licensing income has barely moved year on year, which is rare staying power for a mobile game.

Monopoly Go Q3 2026: the key facts

  • Q3 income for Hasbro: $41 million, reported in Hasbro's Q3 results on October 20, 2026
  • Quarterly trend: The $41 million matches Q1 2026 and sits slightly below Q2's $44 million
  • Nine-month total: $127 million, almost identical to the $126 million reported for the same period last year
  • How the money works: Hasbro licenses the Monopoly brand to Scopely, which builds and runs the game — so the $41 million is Hasbro's licensing share, not the game's total revenue
  • What players were doing in Q3: The quarter covered the Looney Tunes takeover, which ran from July 29 to September 23, after The Simpsons event
  • Current season: Scopely launched its Monster Mash season on September 23, with crossovers including KPop Demon Hunters and Ghostbusters, running until November 25
  • Scale of the game: Scopely says Monopoly Go has passed 300 million downloads, and Sensor Tower said in January that it was the fastest game to reach $6 billion in in-app purchase revenue
  • Hasbro's wider digital business: Digital and licensed gaming revenue rose 3% to $134.1 million in Q3, and 7% to $390.9 million year to date

What to watch next

Watch whether the Monster Mash season, running until November 25, lifts the Q4 figure above the steady $41 million quarterly pattern — and whether Hasbro's full-year Monopoly Go total beats last year's run rate.
