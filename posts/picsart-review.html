Picsart Review: Is It Still Worth It in 2026?

Quick verdict — 4.5 / 5

Yes — Picsart is still worth it in 2026 if you edit photos for social media, thumbnails, or a creator business. The free tier covers everyday edits; the paid Gold tier earns its money only if you use the AI tools and premium library every week. Casual one-photo-a-month editors can stay free, or use Snapseed.

What Picsart does

Picsart is an all-in-one creative app: photo editing, AI tools (background changer, object removal, AI enhancement), video editing, collage, drawing, stickers, and a huge library of templates for posts, stories, and thumbnails. Instead of three separate apps, most creators can live inside this one.

Key features that matter

  • AI background changer & object remover — the two tools creators actually use daily; clean results on product and portrait shots.
  • Templates & stickers — thousands of ready designs, so a good-looking post takes minutes, not an hour.
  • Photo + video in one app — edit the cover image and the clip in the same session.
  • Community & remix culture — discoverable edits and challenges that double as idea fuel.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • True all-rounder — photo, video, design, AI in one place
  • Free tier is genuinely usable, not a demo
  • Template library saves real time for daily posting
  • Creator programmes (like Earn with Picsart) reward active users

Cons

  • The best AI tools and premium assets need the paid Gold tier
  • The home screen pushes upgrades frequently
  • So many tools means a short learning curve at first
  • Heavy projects can feel slow on older phones

Free vs Gold: who should pay?

Stay free if you crop, filter, add text, and make the occasional collage. You will rarely hit a wall.

Consider Gold if you publish daily, sell products with your photos, or lean on AI background/object tools every week — that is where the subscription pays for itself in saved time. Always check the current price on the official store listing first; pricing and trial offers change.

Alternatives worth knowing

  • Canva — better if design templates matter more than photo retouching.
  • Adobe Lightroom — better for serious colour work on real photography.
  • Snapseed — better if you want a powerful editor that is completely free.

Final verdict

Picsart in 2026 is still the easiest recommendation for creators: one app that covers 90% of daily editing, with AI tools that genuinely save time. Download the free version from Google Play, run your next ten posts through it, and only upgrade if you keep hitting the Gold wall.
