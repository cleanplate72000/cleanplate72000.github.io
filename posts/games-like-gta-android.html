Best Games Like GTA on Android (2026)

First, the honest answer about GTA VI: there is no official GTA VI app on Google Play, and GTA VI is not available as an Android game. Any app or website offering a 'GTA 6 APK' download is fake, and installing one risks malware and stolen data. Do not download it. Every game in this list is different: it is a real app with its own official Google Play page, linked below, and nothing here links to an APK file.

So what can you actually play while waiting for GTA VI? The games below cover every type of GTA-style fun on Android: real GTA itself, open-world city action, a gangster story, driving and multiplayer city chaos, arcade action with police chases, a light and funny sandbox, and a building sandbox with no crime at all. Each entry gives the developer, the Play rating, the Play download bracket and the Play content rating, all checked on Google Play. Where a game is rated Mature 17+, that is stated plainly: those games are for older players only, not for children.

Also in this list: if you want more reviewed apps, see our Top 100 Apps page, which is updated as new reviews go live.

1. Real GTA on Android: Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas

Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas, from Rockstar Games, is the closest thing to GTA on a phone because it actually is GTA: the full classic San Andreas story, remade with touch controls. Play lists about 4.6 stars and 5M+ downloads, and it costs $6.99 as a single purchase. Its Play content rating is Mature 17+ (blood and gore, intense violence, strong language, strong sexual content, use of drugs), so it is for older players only.

How it is like GTA: it is the open world, the story missions, the cars, the cities and the freedom to roam that made the series famous. Where it differs: it is an older game, so the graphics and controls feel dated next to a modern console release, and the download is large. Still, if you want genuine GTA on Android, this is the real thing, bought once on Play, with no fake APK involved. Download Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas on Google Play

2. Open-world city action: Gangstar Vegas: World of Crime

Gangstar Vegas: World of Crime, from Gameloft SE, is the best-known GTA-style game made for phones. Play lists about 4.4 stars and 100M+ downloads, it is free with ads and in-app purchases, and its Play content rating is Mature 17+ (violence, blood, suggestive themes, drug reference, language, simulated gambling). Again, older players only.

How it is like GTA: a big open Las Vegas city, story missions, gang wars, car chases, and plenty of vehicles, weapons and side activities, including racing and even flying. Where it differs: it is free-to-play, so progress and the nicer vehicles and weapons lean on in-app purchases over time, and the tone is more over-the-top than a Rockstar story. If you want the biggest, most GTA-like phone original, start here. Download Gangstar Vegas on Google Play

3. Gangster story: Gangstar New Orleans

Gangstar New Orleans, also from Gameloft SE, is the story-led follow-up: a mafia-style open world set in New Orleans, from the French Quarter and the slums to the bayou. Play lists about 4.3 stars and 50M+ downloads, it is free on Play, and its content rating is Mature 17+, so it is for older players only.

How it is like GTA: a proper story campaign about rising through the underworld, turf wars against rival gangs, hundreds of vehicles and weapons, and a large city to explore freely between missions. Where it differs: the New Orleans setting, the gang-versus-gang turf war mode and the mansion and island building give it a different flavour from Vegas, and like Vegas it is a free-to-play game with in-game purchases. Pick this one if story and gang territory matter most to you. Download Gangstar New Orleans on Google Play

4. Driving and multiplayer city chaos: MadOut2 BigCityOnline

MadOut 2: Grand Auto Racing, usually called MadOut2 BigCityOnline, from MadOut Games LLC FZ, is the driving-first pick. Play lists about 4.2 stars and 100M+ downloads, it is free with ads and in-app purchases, and its Play content rating is Mature 17+ (violence, blood, language). Older players only.

How it is like GTA: a large open city, cars to drive and customise, racing, shooting, missions and a big multiplayer world where other players share the same streets. Where it differs: driving and multiplayer chaos come first, and the story is thinner than San Andreas or the Gangstar games. It also has a large download and an online focus, so it suits players who want to race, roam and mess about with other people rather than follow a long campaign. Download MadOut2 BigCityOnline on Google Play

5. Arcade action and police chases: Payback 2 - The Battle Sandbox

Payback 2 - The Battle Sandbox, from Apex Designs Games LLP, is the pick for short, fast sessions. Its own tagline is 'bite sized GTA'. Play lists about 4.5 stars and 100M+ downloads, it is free, and its Play content rating is Teen (violence, blood).

How it is like GTA: open streets, cars, gangs and police chases, packed into quick events rather than one long story: street brawls, heists, rocket races, tank duels, gang warfare and sprints across several cities, plus multiplayer and custom events. Where it differs: there is no long story campaign to follow, and the cartoonish look is deliberately lighter than GTA. That is also its strength: it is small, fast to pick up, and the best choice in this list if you only have a few minutes and want instant GTA-style chaos. Download Payback 2 on Google Play

6. Light, funny sandbox: Dude Theft Wars FPS Open world

Dude Theft Wars FPS Open world, from Poxel Studios Games, is the comedy option. Play lists about 4.3 stars and 100M+ downloads, it is free with ads and in-app purchases, and its Play content rating is Teen (violence, blood).

How it is like GTA: a free-roaming city where you can drive cars, fly planes, steal vehicles, cause chaos and get chased by the police, with both offline play and online multiplayer. Where it differs: the tone is deliberately silly, with blocky visuals and ragdoll physics, and there is no serious story holding it together. You make your own fun instead. It is a good middle ground in this list: GTA-style freedom without the darkest tone, and still rated Teen, so check the rating before handing it to a younger player. Download Dude Theft Wars on Google Play

7. Sandbox building without crime: Minecraft

Minecraft, from Mojang, is the odd one out on purpose: it gives you the open-world freedom people love about GTA, with none of the crime. Play lists about 4.4 stars and 50M+ downloads, it costs $6.99 with in-app purchases, and its Play content rating is Everyone 10+.

How it is like GTA: a completely open world where you set your own goals, explore, build, survive and play with friends, with endless things to do and no fixed path. Where it differs: everything else. You build and craft instead of stealing cars and running from the police, and the blocky world is creative rather than criminal. It belongs in this list because many players who search for GTA-style games really want freedom and a world to get lost in, and Minecraft delivers that in the most family-friendly way here. Download Minecraft on Google Play

Which one should you play first?

Want real GTA? Get San Andreas. Want the biggest phone-made GTA-style world? Get Gangstar Vegas. Want a gangster story? Get Gangstar New Orleans. Want driving and multiplayer chaos? Get MadOut2. Want quick arcade sessions with police chases? Get Payback 2. Want funny, free-form chaos? Get Dude Theft Wars. Want open-world freedom the whole family can play? Get Minecraft. Whichever you pick, get it only from its official Google Play page linked above, check the content rating first, and ignore every 'GTA 6 APK' you see: GTA VI is not on Android, and those downloads are not worth the risk.
