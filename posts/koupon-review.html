Koupon Review: Real Deals & Codes (2026)

Quick verdict — 4.0 / 5. Koupon: Real Deals & Codes is a genuinely useful free shopping companion: it tests promo codes before showing them, covers the big retailers in one search, and never touches your payment — checkout always happens on the retailer's own site. It loses a star because not every code works at checkout (some users report exactly that), prices shown in the app can differ from the retailer's checkout price, and the listing's own download claims do not line up with the install figures. It is rated Medium Maturity, so it is an app for teens and adults rather than young children.

Koupon: Real Deals & Codes, from developer Koupon, is a free shopping and coupon app that finds verified promo codes, discounts and price drops across Amazon, Walmart, eBay, Best Buy, Macy's and Woot — and, unusually, tests every code before you see it. The Play Store description puts the pitch plainly: most coupon apps list codes nobody has tested, while Koupon checks each one twice, first automatically and then with a human editor who catches misleading terms, regional limits and deals that are technically valid but not worth your time. A code only goes live when both checks agree, and every live deal keeps being re-checked and shows when it was last verified.

Why review it now? Because it is climbing the charts. On AppBrain's rankings, updated on 26 October 2026, Koupon sits at #6 in Top Free Shopping in the United States and #34 in Top Free overall in the US, with about 150,000 installs in the last 30 days — roughly 5,000 a day. On the live Play listing it holds about 4.8 stars from more than 17,000 ratings, with Play showing a 1,000,000+ install bracket (AppBrain's own tracking estimates about 1.4 million total installs). The app was last updated on 20 October 2026 to version 3.1.2, adding Halloween and Thanksgiving leaderboard support plus bug fixes. That is a steady run of updates for an app that has been on Google Play since January 2024.

How it works

Open Koupon and you get one search across the stores you already shop at, instead of five separate coupon sites. You can browse deals by discount percentage, price drop or category, follow brands you like, and set alerts for all-time lows, flash sales and trending products so the best prices reach you first. Brand coverage is broad — the listing names more than 3,000 brands, including Apple, Samsung, Nike, Dyson, LEGO, Sony, KitchenAid and Stanley — and categories span electronics, fashion, home and kitchen, beauty and health, baby, pet, toys, sports and outdoors, grocery and automotive.

The part that sets Koupon apart is the verification loop. The developer says its systems run over 10 million verification checks every day, and each deal page shows when it was last verified, so you can judge how fresh a discount is. If a code fails for you, a Report button on the deal page sends it straight back into the verification queue. Seasonal sales get the same treatment: Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Prime Day, Back to School and the holiday season are tracked live as codes and prices drop, rather than the day after.

Key features

  • Double-checked promo codes: every code is tested automatically and then reviewed by an editor before it goes live, and re-checked on a schedule afterwards.
  • Deal insights: see a product's discount history and deal quality before you buy, so you can tell a real bargain from an inflated "was" price.
  • Price-drop and flash-sale alerts: set alerts for all-time lows, flash sales and trending products.
  • 3,000+ brands and every major category: electronics, fashion, home and kitchen, beauty, baby, pet, toys, sports, grocery and automotive.
  • Seasonal sale tracking: live coverage of Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Prime Day, Back to School and holiday sales.
  • Retailer checkout: you apply codes at the retailer's own checkout. Koupon never processes your payment, and the developer discloses that it earns a commission on some purchases — which, it says, never changes the price you pay or the deals shown.

Pros and cons

Pros: genuinely tested codes rather than scraped lists; one search across Amazon, Walmart, eBay, Best Buy, Macy's and Woot; deal history and last-verified dates help you judge a bargain; checkout stays with the retailer, so no payment data goes to the app; small 45.3 MB download; frequently updated, with the latest version landing on 20 October 2026.

Cons: some users report codes that fail at checkout, or a price in the app that is almost double at the retailer's checkout — verification is good, not perfect. The listing's own numbers do not agree: it claims "over 5 million downloads" and "20M+ shoppers", while the Play install bracket is 1,000,000+ and AppBrain tracks about 1.4 million installs, so treat the marketing figures with caution. The app contains ads, and a few recent reviews complain about cancelled or out-of-stock orders — though those orders are the retailer's, not Koupon's. It is rated Medium Maturity, so it is not aimed at young children.

Pricing

Koupon is free on Google Play, with ads inside the app. The listing names no subscription and no in-app purchase price, and — importantly — you never pay through Koupon itself: purchases happen on the retailer's site, so the only money involved is what you spend at Amazon, Walmart or wherever you shop. If a code saves you nothing, you have lost nothing but a tap.

Who should download it?

Koupon suits anyone who already shops on Amazon, Walmart, eBay or Best Buy and is tired of dead coupon codes. If you check it before checkout, you will often find a working code or a price-drop alert you would have missed — and the last-verified date tells you whether to trust a deal. If you want guaranteed discounts, though, look elsewhere: no coupon app can promise every code works, and Koupon's own users say some do not. It is also a shopping tool, not a game or a kids' app, so the Medium Maturity rating is worth noting for family devices.
