AI Morii Review: AI Art Avatars (2026)

Quick verdict — 3.5 / 5. AI Morii: AI Art Generator turns a selfie into an anime portrait, a cartoon avatar or a fantasy character in a few taps, with more than 50 styles to try and a small 29.9 MB download. It is free, rated Everyone, and genuinely trending — but go in with your eyes open: reviewers repeatedly complain about frequent ads, and you should check any in-app offer carefully before paying, since one reviewer reported an unexpected charge.

AI Morii: AI Art Generator, from developer Amobear Application - Entertainment, has been on Google Play since June 2025 and is having a sharp second wind. Independent tracker AppBrain puts it at 1.2 million installs in total, with 230,000 of those in the last 30 days — roughly 7,700 installs a day — and 75,000 installs in the last 7 days, about nine times the week before. On Google Play it holds 4.3 stars from about 1,600 ratings, with Play showing the 1,000,000+ installs bracket, and it has reached #17 in Top Free Art & Design in India, plus top-35 spots in Austria, Norway, Finland and Denmark. Its latest version is 30_07092026, updated on 29 September 2026. That combination — a year-old app suddenly growing fast again — is exactly why it earns a review this week.

The pitch is simple: no design skills needed. Pick a photo, pick a style, and the app generates a new version of it. The Play description promises a three-step flow — choose a photo, choose a style, and share the result — aimed at profile pictures, stories and wallpapers rather than professional design work. That is an important distinction. AI Morii is a fun portrait-and-avatar maker, not a full photo editor, and it works best when you judge it on those terms.

The app's headline promise in one image: a selfie reimagined as a polished AI portrait, ready for a profile picture or wallpaper.

Key features

The Play listing groups the tools into four areas. First, the AI art generator itself: one-tap generation that turns selfies into anime, painting, sketch and fantasy avatars, with 50+ styles including cartoon, oil painting, cyberpunk, manga and futuristic looks. Second, an AI avatar creator with templates for superheroes, fairies, samurai and futuristic warriors — the kind of ready-made characters that work well as gaming or social avatars. Third, a photo editor toolkit for the basics: brightness, contrast, sharpness and saturation, so you can tidy a photo before or after the AI treatment. Fourth, one-tap filters and effects such as watercolor, neon and pixel art, with a real-time preview so you can see the change before you commit.

Fantasy styles are the showpiece: a plain portrait becomes a crowned, storybook-style character in the app's promotional examples.

Cartoonify is the friendliest feature for everyday use — a before-and-after cartoon version of a photo, without any editing skill.

Sharing is built in. The listing says results can be shared instantly to social media, which fits the app's real use case: a new profile picture, a birthday post, or a fun avatar for a group chat. The app is only about 29.9 MB, so it will not eat much storage, and it runs on phones and tablets according to its Play listing. For a free app, that lightness is a genuine plus — many AI photo apps are several times larger.

The template gallery is broad: fantasy, festive, futuristic and everyday styles sit side by side, so there is usually something beyond the first few looks.

Pros and cons

  • Pro: A genuinely wide style range — 50+ styles and more than 100 templates in the promotional gallery — so results do not all look the same.
  • Pro: Small download (29.9 MB), free to start, and rated Everyone, with real traction: 1.2 million installs and a top-20 Art & Design chart spot in India.
  • Pro: Simple three-step flow and a basic photo editor in the same app, so you can fix brightness or sharpness without opening another tool.
  • Con: Ads are the biggest complaint. Several recent reviewers describe ads after almost every option, including ads that block the view — expect interruptions in the free version.
  • Con: Pricing needs care. The listing is free to download and shows a price of 0 USD, but AppBrain's snapshot does not clearly list in-app purchase prices, and one September 2026 reviewer claimed a charge of 704 rupees for image generation. Read any offer screen fully before confirming a payment.
  • Con: AI results vary. A generated portrait is an interpretation, not a photograph, and faces, hands and text in AI images can look wrong — check the result before you post it as if it were real.
  • Con: Data safety deserves a note: the Play listing's data safety section says data is not encrypted in transit and cannot be deleted, and that the app may share location and app activity with third parties. Avoid uploading sensitive or private photos.

Pricing

Free to download on Google Play, with ads included. The store listing shows no upfront price, and the snapshot checked for this review did not list a clear in-app purchase range — which is itself a reason to be careful: if the app offers a subscription or a credit pack, the price will be on that offer screen, so read it before you tap confirm. There is no claim here that everything in the app is free forever, only that downloading it costs nothing.

Who it is for

AI Morii suits anyone who wants a quick, fun new profile picture or avatar without learning an editor — and who does not mind ads in exchange for a free app. It is a good fit for trying anime, cartoon and fantasy styles on your own photos, and for making avatars for games or social profiles. If you want precise editing control, print-quality portraits, or an ad-free experience, look elsewhere: a dedicated editor such as the one in our AI Collage Maker review is a better starting point for layout work, and a paid editor will be calmer than an ad-supported AI generator.

Final verdict

AI Morii earns its 3.5 out of 5 the honest way: a fun, genuinely popular AI portrait maker with a wide style range and a tiny download, held back by heavy ads and pricing that needs a careful read. The trending numbers are real — 230,000 installs in a month and nine times growth week-on-week do not happen by accident — but the app is best enjoyed as a free toy for avatars and profile pictures, not as a professional tool. Try it with a non-sensitive photo, skip any offer you did not expect, and grab it from the official Play Store link below, never from a third-party APK site.
