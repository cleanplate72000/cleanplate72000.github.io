Titanium Court Hits Mobile on October 29

Indie developer AP Thomson has announced that his surreal match-3 puzzler Titanium Court will launch on console and mobile devices on October 29. The game blends tile-matching puzzle mechanics with defensive strategy, wrapped in what its creator describes as a kooky, surreal narrative.

Why this matters: Titanium Court arrives on mobile carrying two of indie gaming's biggest honours — the Independent Games Festival's Excellence in Design award and the Seumas McNally Grand Prize — plus a 94% positive rating on Steam. That is a rare level of acclaim for a puzzle game heading to phones.

Titanium Court: what we know

  • Release date: October 29 (console and mobile)
  • Developer: AP Thomson
  • Genre: Surreal match-3 puzzler with defensive strategy gameplay
  • Awards: IGF Excellence in Design and the Seumas McNally Grand Prize
  • Steam reception: 94% positive reviews

What is Titanium Court?

Titanium Court takes the familiar tile-matching puzzle formula and pairs it with defensive strategy, all inside a surreal, offbeat world. The combination — plus its festival wins — has made it one of the most talked-about indie puzzlers ahead of its mobile debut.

What to watch next

Watch for the mobile store pages going live ahead of the October 29 launch, along with pricing details, which have not been announced yet. If you enjoy inventive puzzle games, this is one to put on your list — and you can catch up on our other gaming news, like Dispatch coming to mobile on December 9, while you wait.
