Canva Review: Is It Still the Best Free Design App in 2026?

Canva turned design into something anyone can do — and the numbers prove it. The app has passed 500 million downloads on Google Play with a 4.8-star rating from over 25 million reviews, making it one of the highest-rated creative apps ever. But with so many features now behind Pro, is the free version still worth it in 2026?

Our verdict: 4.7/5. For posters, social graphics, presentations and quick video edits, Canva's free tier is still the most generous design tool on Android. Pro adds background removal, premium templates and brand tools — but casual creators can go a long way without paying a cent.

Canva at a glance

  • Developer: Canva Pty Ltd
  • Price: Free, with optional Pro subscription (in-app purchases)
  • Downloads: 500,000,000+ on Google Play
  • Rating: 4.8/5 from 25+ million reviews
  • Best for: Social posts, posters, presentations, logos, video, print

Key features that make Canva trending

Millions of templates. Instagram posts, YouTube thumbnails, resumes, invitations, business cards — pick a template and your design is half done before you start.

AI design tools. Generate images, photos and elements from a text prompt, and use AI-assisted editing to speed up routine work.

Photo and video editing in one app. Canva now covers photo edits and short video edits too, so many creators never leave the app.

Works everywhere. Start a design on your phone and finish it on a laptop — everything syncs across devices.

Free vs Pro: what do you actually pay for?

The free version includes a huge template library, basic photo and video editing, and most everyday design tools — enough for students, small businesses and casual creators. Pro adds one-click background removal, premium templates and fonts, brand kits, and more cloud storage. Power users and businesses get real value from Pro; everyone else can stay free happily.

Pros
  • 4.8 stars from 25M+ reviews — users genuinely love it
  • Biggest free template library of any design app
  • AI generation, photo and video editing in one place
  • Syncs across phone, tablet and desktop
Cons
  • Best features (background remover, premium elements) need Pro
  • Free users must sift through premium elements
  • Small-screen editing feels cramped vs desktop

Who should download Canva?

Download it if you make social posts, posters, presentations or thumbnails and want professional results without design skills. If you mainly edit video, see our CapCut review or Edits review instead; for pure photo editing, our 10 best free photo editing apps has more options.

Canva FAQ

Is Canva free? Yes — Canva is free to install and use on Google Play, with an optional Pro subscription for premium features.

Is Canva good for beginners? Yes — templates and drag-and-drop tools make it one of the easiest design apps to start with.

Can I use Canva for YouTube thumbnails? Yes — Canva has ready-made thumbnail templates sized for YouTube, plus fonts and elements to customise them.

Does Canva work on desktop too? Yes — designs sync between the Android app and the desktop/web version.
