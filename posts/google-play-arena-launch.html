Google Play Arena Launches in India — Free Registrations Open, MS Dhoni Leads Mahi Clutchers

Google Play and NODWIN Gaming have launched Google Play Arena, a club-based mobile gaming championship for India. Free registrations opened on Friday, October 23, with open online qualifiers running over the coming weeks ahead of a Grand Finale on December 19–20, 2026.

Why this matters: Instead of building around a single game, Google Play Arena uses a club-based format across six titles — and anyone aged 18 and over can enter the free open qualifiers, with top performers earning a shot at representing a professional club at the finale.

Google Play Arena: what we know

  • Organisers: Google Play and NODWIN Gaming
  • Registrations: Free, opened October 23; open to gamers aged 18 and above
  • Format: Open online qualifiers, then a Grand Finale on December 19–20, 2026
  • Games (6): Battlegrounds Mobile India (BGMI), Free Fire MAX, Moba Legends: 5v5!, Real Cricket, Chess (Chess.com) and EA SPORTS FC™ Mobile
  • Clubs (6): Team S8UL, GodLike Esports, Orangutan Gaming, Revenant XSpark, Team Apex Gaming and Mahi Clutchers
  • MS Dhoni's club: Mahi Clutchers, built entirely from the ground up through the open qualifiers
  • Entry model: A pure skill-led competition — no entry fees and no pay-to-play mechanics, according to Google
  • NODWIN's role: Leading conceptualisation, planning, partnerships and execution

What is Google Play Arena?

The championship is designed to give India's mobile gaming community what mainstream sport enjoys: recognised professional clubs, a grassroots-to-professional ladder, and a stage where merit decides success. The announcement came in a Google blog post by Aditya Swamy, Managing Director, Google Play, and Kanika Kalra, Director, Consumer Apps & Platforms Marketing, Google India.

What to watch next

Watch for the open qualifier results and which grassroots players earn places with the six clubs — especially Mahi Clutchers, the one team being built from scratch. Google also says players can expect special community unlocks and exclusive game offers over the coming weeks. We will update this story as qualifier dates and finale details are confirmed.
