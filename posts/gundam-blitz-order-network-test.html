Gundam Blitz Order Test Runs Nov 18-24

Bandai Namco has announced Gundam Blitz Order, a free-to-play idle simulation game for iOS and Android, and opened sign-ups for its first network test — but applications close on October 26, so interested players need to act fast.

Why this matters: This is the first idle strategy game in the Gundam franchise, and the November test is the first chance to play it before its full worldwide launch, which has no release date yet.

Gundam Blitz Order: the key facts

  • Game: Gundam Blitz Order, from Bandai Namco
  • Genre: Free-to-play idle simulation game — the first idle strategy game in the Gundam franchise
  • Platforms: iOS and Android, with a worldwide release planned
  • Network test: November 18 to 24, 2026, in selected regions, with 500 places per region
  • Sign-up deadline: Applications close on October 26, 2026
  • English test: Open to residents of the United States and England
  • Gameplay: Build squads from mobile suits, warships and Mobile Armours drawn from across the Gundam series, then set the starting formation and let battles play out automatically
  • Progression: Enhancement materials are collected automatically, letting players level up a whole squad at once, and newly acquired units can join battles right away
  • Modes: Boss battles, warship escort missions and player-versus-player competitive arenas
  • Release date: Not announced yet

What to watch next

Watch for confirmation messages to successful test applicants ahead of the November 18 start, and for a full release date once the test wraps up. Because this is a closed test with limited places, signing up does not guarantee a spot. For more mobile gaming news, see our VAMPIR global launch story and the Top 100 apps page.
