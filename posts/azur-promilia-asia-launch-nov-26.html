Azur Promilia Asia Launch Set for Nov 26

Manjuu has set a release date for Azur Promilia in Asia: the fantasy role-playing game will launch on November 26, 2026, in China, Japan, South Korea and Taiwan, on PC and on Android and iOS mobile devices.

Why this matters: Players in those four regions finally have a firm date to plan around, while everyone else gets a clear wait: the English version is not part of this launch.

Azur Promilia: the key facts

  • Game: Azur Promilia, a fantasy RPG from Manjuu, the developer behind Azur Lane
  • Asian launch: November 26, 2026, in China, Japan, South Korea and Taiwan
  • Platforms: PC, Android and iOS mobile devices
  • New character: Lan Xiangzi, revealed alongside the date, with a ghostly theme and Japanese voice actress Ayane Kudo
  • Release trailer: A South Korean release-date trailer shows the Starborn avatar, Luruka, Symphoria and Terara exploring and fighting together
  • English version: Expected worldwide in Summer 2027, after this Asian launch

What to watch next

Watch for store listings and launch-time details in the four launch regions, and for more news on the English version ahead of Summer 2027. For more mobile gaming news, see our Apple Arcade November story and the Top 100 apps page.

Source: Siliconera's report on Manjuu's Asian release date (published 2026-10-23). Details as reported there; availability and details may change.
