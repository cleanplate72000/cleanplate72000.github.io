Crushed in Time Brings Holmes to Mobile

From the studio behind There Is No Game: Wrong Dimension, Draw Me a Pixel has brought Crushed in Time to iOS, starring a wacky Sherlock Holmes and Dr Watson in a comedy adventure that keeps breaking its own fourth wall.

Why this matters: Instead of a straight detective story, the game is about a game falling apart — its heroes must track down a missing character from their own newly launched title, which makes it a very different kind of mobile adventure.

Crushed in Time: the key facts

  • Game: Crushed in Time, from Draw Me a Pixel, a spin-off from There Is No Game: Wrong Dimension
  • Platform: iOS, out now
  • Price: $6.99 for the full game, as reported by Pocket Gamer
  • Premise: Sherlock and Watson's new game is missing a crucial NPC, so the duo must guide their way through Victorian England and the unfinished parts of the game itself to save it
  • Gameplay: Stretch, squash and flick Holmes, Watson and objects around them to solve puzzles, with graphics styles changing and unfinished areas to explore

What to watch next
