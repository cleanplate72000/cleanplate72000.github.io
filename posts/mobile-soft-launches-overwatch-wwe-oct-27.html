Mobile Soft Launches: Overwatch Rush, WWE

Some of mobile gaming's biggest names are quietly testing their next games in soft launch, and Mobilegamer.biz's updated soft-launch roundup pulls the latest tests together in one place. The headline names are Blizzard, PlayStation Mobile, Scopely and Rovio — but the list also shows how many familiar series are trying a new format on phones before a wider release.

Mobile soft launches: the key facts

  • Overwatch Rush (Blizzard): a made-for-mobile hero shooter, in soft launch in the Philippines, Malaysia and Indonesia, on Android and iOS.
  • Ratchet & Clank: Ranger Rumble (PlayStation Mobile): a multiplayer platformer arena shooter, now playable in France, with developer Oh BiBi confirming the launch; AppMagic also lists Denmark, Finland, Indonesia, Norway, Sweden and the Philippines, and pre-registration is open.
  • WWE Generations: Eras Collide (Scopely): a turn-based RPG where players build, battle and manage a team of WWE stars from different eras; soft launched on iOS only in Ireland and the Philippines, according to AppMagic.
  • Angry Birds Match (Sega/Rovio): Rovio's match-3 take on Angry Birds, available on iOS only and picking up downloads in the US, UK, Australia and Finland.
  • Plants vs. Zombies 3: Evolved (EA/PopCap): the merge-based Plants vs. Zombies game, soft launched on iOS and Android in the Philippines, Indonesia, the UK and several other markets, with Title Update 1.0.5 reworking the new-player tutorial and rebalancing rewards.
  • Hill Climb Racing 3 (Fingersoft): the physics-driven racing series' latest entry, introducing real-time multiplayer for the first time; soft launched on Google Play in the UK, Norway, Sweden and Finland.
  • Bloons Blitz (Ninja Kiwi): a Bloons-themed bullet-heaven game in the style of Vampire Survivors, following a Philippines-only soft launch in May; pre-registration is now open ahead of a global release on iOS and Android.
  • Minecraft Blast (King/Mojang): another test version of the block-blasting puzzler, released in Malaysia on Google Play only, with a new trailer suggesting Candy Crush-style map progression and a building-based meta.
  • What soft launch means: these games are in limited testing, not full global release, so regions, features and availability can change — Mobilegamer.biz notes that all details in its roundup are subject to change.

Why it matters

Soft launch is where big publishers find out whether a game works before spending on a global launch — and this roundup shows established series, from Overwatch and Ratchet & Clank to Angry Birds and Plants vs. Zombies, all being reworked for phones rather than simply ported. For players, the practical point is patience: a game appearing in one country's store does not mean it is available everywhere yet, and test versions can change a lot before global release.

What to watch next

Watch for Bloons Blitz's global launch date, whether Ranger Rumble and Overwatch Rush widen beyond their current test countries, and whether Plants vs. Zombies 3 moves from Early Access to a full release.
