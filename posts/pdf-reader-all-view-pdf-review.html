PDF Reader Review: All-View PDFs (2026)

Quick verdict — 4.0 / 5. PDF Reader - All View PDF is a light, genuinely useful document app: it opens PDFs fast, groups all your documents in one place, scans paper into PDFs, and bundles editing tools — merge, split, annotate and print — that usually live in separate apps. It is free, rated Everyone, and holds a 4.6-star rating, but its last update was in April 2026, so check the current Play listing before you rely on it for anything critical, and expect ads.

PDF Reader - All View PDF, from developer BEPO Media LLC, has been on Google Play since February 2026 and has already passed 2.5 million installs. AppBrain's trending list for 26 October 2026 puts it at 49,000 installs in the last 7 days — six times the week before — with 77,000 installs in the last 30 days, and it has reached #58 in the Top Free Tools chart in South Korea, #92 in India and #111 in Indonesia. Its rating stands at 4.6 stars from about 3,700 ratings on AppBrain (3,832 ratings on the live Play listing), the current version is 1.6, updated 19 April 2026, the download is a light 23.65 MB, and Google Play rates its content Everyone. It is free to download and, according to the listing data checked for this review, contains ads.

That combination — small download, high rating, fast recent growth — is exactly why it is trending now. Most people do not go looking for a PDF reader for fun; they go looking because a form, a ticket, a textbook or a work document will not open properly somewhere else. An app that promises to open anything quickly, keep every document format in one list, and handle the occasional edit without a second download is solving a problem most phone owners hit every week.

Fast opening, all documents in one place

The developer's first promise is speed: a lightweight engine that opens any PDF instantly, with fast loading and smooth scrolling even for large or complex files. That matters more than it sounds. A reader that stutters on a 200-page textbook or a scanned report is useless no matter how many tools it lists, and the Play description makes stable performance on big files a central claim. We cannot verify performance on your exact phone from the listing alone — a budget handset and a flagship will not behave identically — but the claim is specific, and the 4.6-star average suggests most users are not hitting constant crashes.

The second screenshot shows the app's home view, and it is the feature that will matter most day to day: a single All Document list. Recent files appear with their name, format icon and size — PDFs alongside DOC, XLS and PPT files — instead of forcing you to hunt through folders or remember which app opened which file. The developer says files are automatically grouped and organised, so you can find what you need without wasting time. For anyone whose phone is a graveyard of downloads, attachments and saved forms, one searchable, sorted list is a genuine quality-of-life improvement over the file manager.

Why it is trending

PDF Reader - All View PDF is trending for a simple reason: it is a small, free, well-rated utility that does several jobs at once, and utilities spread by chart position and word of mouth. Six times the previous week's installs is a sharp jump, and it comes on top of a 2.5-million install base — this is not a brand-new app spiking from zero, but an established reader finding a new audience. A top-100 tools ranking in several countries at once usually means the same thing: people who needed a reader this week tried this one, liked it, and did not go looking for another.

The other reason is the breadth of the toolkit for a 23.65 MB download. Readers that only open PDFs are easy to replace; an app that also scans, edits, merges and prints gives you fewer reasons to install anything else. That breadth is also the risk — a tool that tries to do everything can do each thing only adequately — so the honest test is whether the individual tools are good enough for everyday use, not whether the feature list is long.

Files, sorted smartly

The third screenshot, headed "Files, Sorted Smartly", shows the organised view in action: documents grouped with clear format badges, file sizes and dates, and a bookmark icon on each entry. That sorting is the difference between a reader and a document manager. A reader shows you the file you tap; a manager helps you find the file you forgot you had. Bookmarks in particular are worth using: if you open the same contract, timetable or study notes every day, bookmarking them once saves a search every time after.

Our advice is to spend two minutes organising on first install — bookmark the documents you open weekly, and delete or ignore the duplicates your downloads folder has accumulated. The app can group and sort for you, but it cannot know which of three near-identical "Report_final" files is the real one. That is a human decision, and it is worth making before you need the file in a hurry.

Scan, edit, merge, split and print

The fourth screenshot, "Scan Like Magic", shows the scanning side: a paper document turned into a clean PDF page inside the reader. The developer describes turning your device into a portable, high-quality document scanner, capturing clear, professional-looking scans you can save, convert or share. Scan quality in practice will depend on your camera and lighting — no app can rescue a dark, blurry photo — but for receipts, forms and single-page documents, a built-in scanner removes the need for a separate scanning app entirely.

The complete tool suite, as described on Play, covers editing text, annotating pages, printing documents, merging multiple files, splitting PDFs and organising pages, all in one workspace. Merging and splitting are the two tools most people actually need: combine several single-page scans into one PDF to send, or split a long document down to the pages someone asked for. Annotation — highlighting, notes, marks on a page — is what turns a reader into a study or work tool. Print support closes the loop for anyone who still needs paper copies. None of these features is unusual on its own; having them together in a free, 23 MB app is the selling point.

Key features

  • Fast, smooth PDF opening — a lightweight engine built for large or complex files, with smooth scrolling.
  • All-in-one document manager — PDFs, DOC, XLS and PPT files in a single, automatically grouped All Document list.
  • Intelligent scanning — turn paper documents into clear, shareable PDFs from your phone camera.
  • Complete PDF tool suite — edit text, annotate pages, print, merge files, split PDFs and organise pages.
  • Bookmarks and smart sorting — keep frequently used documents one tap away in an organised list.
  • Clean, user-friendly interface — the developer's stated focus is effortless navigation, not a wall of menus.
  • Light download — about 23.65 MB, so it will not eat the storage it is supposed to help you manage.
  • Everyone rating — a family-safe utility with no age-gated content.

  • Trending now: 49,000 installs in 7 days, six times the week before, per AppBrain's 26 October 2026 list
  • One app for reading, scanning, editing, merging, splitting, annotating and printing
  • Single All Document list for PDF, DOC, XLS and PPT files, with bookmarks and smart sorting
  • Free download, Everyone rating, and a light 23.65 MB install size
Cons

  • Contains ads, according to the listing data checked for this review
  • 4.6-star average rests on about 3,700 ratings — strong, but not yet a huge sample
  • Promo screenshots are marketing images, so the real interface may look plainer than the adverts
  • Scan quality depends on your camera and lighting; a scanner app cannot fix a bad photo

Pricing

PDF Reader - All View PDF is free to download on Google Play, and the Play listing checked for this review shows a price of 0 USD with no in-app purchase price range confirmed — so treat the free download as the only pricing the listing itself confirms, and judge any paid offer inside the app on its own merits before accepting it. The app does contain ads, so expect promotional interruptions in exchange for the free tools. For a utility you open to read or send a document, that trade-off is easy to live with, provided the ads do not interrupt a print or a scan at the wrong moment.

Download

Download PDF Reader - All View PDF on Google Play

If you need one app to open, organise, scan and lightly edit your documents, PDF Reader - All View PDF is a solid, light, well-rated choice — just confirm the version on Play first, given the April 2026 update date.
