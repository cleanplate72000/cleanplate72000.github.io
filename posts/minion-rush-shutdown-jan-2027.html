Minion Rush Shuts Down After 13 Years

Gameloft is winding down Minion Rush, its long-running Despicable Me endless runner, after a 13-year run that began in 2013. The game leaves app stores this week, so players who want it should act before the store removal date.

Key facts

  • What is ending: Minion Rush, Gameloft's Despicable Me endless runner, first released in 2013.
  • Store removal: the game will be withdrawn from app stores on October 30, 2026, and in-app purchases will be disabled on the same day, with no further content updates.
  • Farewell event: a special farewell event ran from October 1 to October 28, 2026, offering exclusive in-game rewards.
  • Servers shut down: online services end on January 29, 2027.
  • Offline play survives: players who already have the game installed will still be able to play an offline version after the servers close.
  • What goes offline: live events, leaderboards, cloud saves, social features and online account data will no longer be available.
  • Before the shutdown: players are encouraged to spend remaining in-game currency and unlock content first, as progression and unlock requirements have been adjusted to be faster and easier in the final months.
  • Refunds: purchases made before the shutdown will not be eligible for refunds except where required by applicable law or platform policies.
  • Scale: AppMagic estimates put the game at almost 500 million downloads and over $45.7 million in in-app purchase revenue — estimates, not Gameloft's own figures.

Why it matters

Very few mobile games last 13 years, and Minion Rush outlived most of its endless-runner rivals. The important detail for players is the split ending: the store deadline comes first, on October 30, but the game itself is not gone on that day. Anyone who already has it installed can keep playing online until January 29, 2027, and offline after that — so the real last chance is to download it before October 30, not before January.

What to watch next

Watch for Gameloft's final service-period details and whether any last content is unlocked before the servers close. For another recent mobile shutdown, see our Rainbow Six Mobile shutdown story, and browse the Top 100 apps page for games that are still going strong.

Source: Mobilegamer.biz, "Gameloft is closing Minion Rush after a 13-year run", corroborated by PocketGamer.biz, "Minion Rush to end online services in January 2027" (published September 29, 2026). Details may change — check the sources and Gameloft's official FAQ for the latest information.
