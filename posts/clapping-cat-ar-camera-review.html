Clapping Cat AR Camera Review: Clap (2026)

Quick verdict — 3.8 / 5. Clapping Cat AR Camera does exactly one thing — it puts a funny clapping, hand-shaking cat into your room through your phone camera — and it does that one thing well enough to have gone viral. It is free, rated Everyone, and genuinely good for a quick laugh, but it is a very thin app: one cat, a handful of actions, and ads. Download it for the meme, not for a deep game.

Clapping Cat AR Camera, from developer Hong Kong Lux Limo Service Limited, is one of the fastest risers on AppBrain's trending list for the week of 26 October 2026, with about 160,000 installs in the last 7 days. The app only launched on 5 October 2026, yet it has already reached about 170,000 installs in total — roughly 13,000 a day over the last 30 days — and it has topped the Top New Free Entertainment chart in Germany, reaching #1 there. On the live Google Play listing it holds about 4.3 stars from roughly 260 ratings. The reason is simple: it rides the viral "clap handshake cat" trend, the short video meme of a black cat that claps and shakes hands with its owner, and turns it into something you can film in your own living room.

The idea needs no explanation once you see it. Open the app, point your phone camera at a flat surface, and a life-sized black cat appears in your room. The cat claps its paws, offers a handshake, and reacts to your hand — the exact beats of the viral clips. You record the moment, add trending music or sounds, and share the finished video with friends or on social media. That is the whole app, and honestly, that is the whole appeal: a ten-second joke that lands every time, filmed wherever you happen to be standing.

What makes it work as a meme machine is how little friction there is. There is no account to create, no long tutorial, and no collection to grind through. You place the cat, you film it, you share it. The AR placement is the classic phone-camera trick — scan a floor or table, drop the cat in, and it stays put while you move around it — so the clips look surprisingly real to anyone scrolling past them. The built-in music and sound options matter here too: pairing the clap with a trending sound is half of what makes these clips spread, and the app keeps that step inside the recording flow rather than forcing you into a separate editor.

Key features

  • Clapping Cat: a playful virtual cat that claps and shakes hands, placed into your real surroundings through AR.
  • Funny cat videos: record short clips of the cat in your space and turn an ordinary moment into an entertaining video.
  • Trending music: add music and sounds to your cat videos to make them more engaging and shareable.
  • Easy sharing: save your favourite clips and share them with friends or on social media straight from the app.
  • Simple to use: no account, no tutorial wall — point, place, record, share.

It is worth being honest about the limits, because they are the difference between a fun download and a disappointing one. This is a first release — version 1.0.1, from 5 October 2026 — and it shows. There appears to be essentially one cat and a small set of actions: clap, handshake, and a few poses. There is no larger game here, no collection of cats to unlock, and no story mode. If you are expecting a virtual pet you raise over weeks, this is not it. The app also contains ads, which is the usual trade-off for a free meme app, and at about 73.7 MB it is a chunky download for something so simple. Finally, AR apps live or die by your phone's camera and sensors: on older or budget phones the tracking can wobble, and the illusion breaks with it.

Pros and cons

Pros: instantly funny and easy to understand; no account or tutorial; trending music built into recording; genuinely shareable clips; free and rated Everyone; a real viral trend, not a knock-off of one.

Cons: very thin — one cat and a few actions in this first version; contains ads; a large 73.7 MB download for a simple app; AR tracking depends on your phone; no collection, game or long-term progression yet.

Pricing

Clapping Cat AR Camera is free to download on Google Play and contains ads. The Play listing does not highlight in-app purchases, so the ads are the price of the free download. The app is rated Everyone, the current version is 1.0.1 (updated 5 October 2026), and the download is about 73.7 MB.

Download Clapping Cat AR Camera on Google Play

So, should you download it? If you have seen the clap handshake cat clips and wanted to make your own, yes — this is the quickest, cheapest way to do it, and the results are genuinely funny. Just go in with the right expectation: this is a ten-second joke machine, not a virtual pet or a game, and you will probably enjoy it most in short bursts, filming a clip or two for friends. If you like making short videos, also read our Photo Video Maker with Music review, another free tool for turning clips and photos into shareable videos. For a viral meme turned into an app, Clapping Cat AR Camera earns its moment — and its 3.8 out of 5.
