Genshin Impact 7.2 Hour of Ruin Out Nov 4

HoYoverse has confirmed that Genshin Impact Version 7.2, "Hour of Ruin", launches on 4 November 2026. The date was announced in the Version 7.2 Special Program stream on 23 October 2026, alongside a new trailer, two playable characters and a new explorable region in Snezhnaya.

Why this matters: Genshin Impact is one of the biggest games on mobile, and 7.2 pushes its Snezhnaya story forward with a new region, a new boss and two new Electro characters — a substantial update for players on Android and iOS as well as PC and consoles.

Genshin Impact 7.2: the key facts

  • What: Genshin Impact Version 7.2, titled "Hour of Ruin"
  • When: 4 November 2026
  • Announced by: HoYoverse, in the Version 7.2 Special Program stream on 23 October 2026
  • New characters: Mitya, a 5-star Electro Catalyst user, and Valeriy, a 4-star Electro character, both available from launch day
  • New story: The next Archon Quest chapter in Snezhnaya, as the Tsaritsa gathers representatives of all seven nations against the Nails of Retribution and the Holy Soil phenomenon
  • New region: Eastern Snezhnaya, including the underground city of Novokitezhgrad, plus a new Wavebinder boss
  • Banners: Phase one pairs Mitya and Valeriy with a Furina rerun; phase two brings back Zibai and Linnea, alongside a new Natlan Chronicled Wish
  • Rewards: Completing the Archon Quest within the version period grants 560 Primogems and Mitya's ascension materials, as reported
  • Platforms: Android, iOS, PC, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S
  • Free codes: The Special Program codes HourofRuin, ExpertMitya and PhotoTime give 300 Primogems plus bonus items in total, but expire at 00:00 (UTC-4) on 26 October 2026, so claim them quickly

What to watch next

Watch for the exact server maintenance times for 4 November, which HoYoverse has not pinned to a single hour, and for how the new Stellar-Conduct team options shape up once Mitya and Valeriy are playable.
