No Man's Sky Lands on iPhone and iPad Oct 28

Hello Games is bringing its vast space exploration game No Man's Sky to iPhone and iPad on October 28. The studio describes the mobile version as a side project it has worked on for nearly three years — and founder Sean Murray insists it is not a cut-down edition of the game.

Why this matters: No Man's Sky is one of gaming's great comeback stories, rebuilt over a decade of free updates. A full version on iPhone and iPad — free to try, with a single unlock price — puts an entire procedurally generated universe in your pocket.

No Man's Sky on iPhone and iPad: key facts

  • Release date: October 28, on iPhone and iPad; open for pre-order now
  • Price: free to try at launch, with a one-time $19.99 purchase to unlock the full game
  • Controls: touch controls, controllers, and mouse and keyboard support; can be cast to a TV
  • What's included: ten years of updates, including the recent Cosmos update, which lets players own and manage a space station
  • What's missing: multiplayer and planetary settlements are the only major features left out, according to the studio
  • Requirements: iOS or iPadOS 26 or later, a device with an A12 Bionic chip or newer, and around 3.8GB of storage
  • Tech: uses MetalFX and Apple's M-series chips

What to watch next

Pre-order now if you want it ready on launch day, and check your device meets the iOS 26 and A12 requirements first. Murray says Hello Games will support iOS based on player feedback and player numbers, as it did with the Switch version — so a strong launch could mean years of further updates.
