Xbox Game Pass Comes to T-Mobile Plans in November

Xbox Game Pass is coming to eligible T-Mobile customers in the US, through T-Mobile's new T-Mix plans — the first time Game Pass has been available through a wireless plan in the US.

Why this matters: instead of a separate subscription, Game Pass becomes a pickable benefit inside a phone plan — a new route into the service for players who already pay a carrier every month.

Xbox Game Pass on T-Mobile: the key facts

  • Announced: October 26, 2026, by T-Mobile, as reported by Xbox Wire.
  • Starting: beginning in November, eligible T-Mix customers can choose Game Pass Ultimate or Premium.
  • What it is: T-Mix gives US customers more choice over the benefits included in their wireless plan, and Xbox Game Pass is one of those benefits.
  • Where you can play: an instant library of games playable on devices players already own, including Xbox consoles, Smart TVs, PCs, and more.
  • First of its kind: Xbox says this is the first time Xbox Game Pass will be available through a wireless plan in the US.
  • Eligibility: Xbox does not spell out the full eligibility rules in its announcement — it points readers to T-Mobile's announcement for eligibility and how to choose Game Pass as part of T-Mix, so this post does not guess them.

Why it matters

Game Pass has always been sold as a games subscription. Putting it inside a wireless plan changes the decision: for eligible T-Mobile customers, it becomes one benefit among others in a plan they already hold, rather than another standalone bill. If carriers become a normal way to pick up a games subscription, rivals may follow — and the phone in your pocket becomes an even more direct route into a game library.

What to watch next

Watch T-Mobile's announcement for the eligibility details and how customers select Game Pass inside T-Mix, and whether the offer spreads beyond the first T-Mix rollout in November. For more gaming news, see our Kettle Street story and browse the Top 100 apps page.

Source: Xbox Wire, "XBOX Game Pass Is Coming to Eligible T-Mobile Customers" (October 26, 2026). Details may change — check the source for the latest information.
