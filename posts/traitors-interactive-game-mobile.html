The Traitors Game Lands on iOS, Android

The Traitors: Interactive Game is out now on iOS and Android, bringing the hit reality TV format to phones. Developed by Fusebox Games, the interactive fiction game puts you inside the Traitors Castle, competing alongside 22 other players across a 12-day journey of missions, alliances, deception and banishment.

Why this matters: The Traitors is one of TV's biggest formats, and this is its first full interactive mobile game — a social-strategy game where reading other players matters more than reflexes, and every choice can get you banished or carry you to the prize.

The Traitors on mobile: the key facts

  • Out now: Released worldwide earlier in October 2026 on iOS and Android
  • Developer: Fusebox Games, a UK studio owned by Nazara Technologies, previously behind Love Island and Big Brother mobile games
  • Licence: Made in partnership with All3Media International and Banijay Rights
  • The setup: Enter the Traitors Castle with 22 other players for a 12-day game, guided by host Vivienne
  • Pick your trait: Start as a Leader, Chameleon or Wildcard — your trait and daily wardrobe choices shape how other players see you
  • Daily missions: Complete missions to build the prize pot and earn Shields, which protect you from that night's "murder"
  • The Round Table: Vote tactically to banish suspects — or, as a secret Traitor, steer suspicion away from yourself
  • Price: Free to download, with in-app purchases

What to watch next

Watch for seasonal events and new content tied to future TV series of The Traitors, and for how the game's live-service side develops — a social game like this lives or dies on its player community.
