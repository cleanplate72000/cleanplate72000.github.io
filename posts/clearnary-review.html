ClearNary Review: Swipe to Clean Up (2026)

Quick verdict — 4 / 5. ClearNary is one of the fastest-growing Android cleaners of October 2026 for a simple reason: it turns the dullest phone chore — deleting duplicate photos — into a quick swipe session. It is free, light at 18 MB, and does its scanning on your device. Just remember its own warning: deletions are permanent, so swipe carefully.

ClearNary: AI Storage Cleaner from Sliver AI Photo Editor is having a remarkable autumn. Launched on Google Play in August 2026, it passed 500,000 installs on 20 October and now sits at around 690,000 installs, with roughly 650,000 of those arriving in the last 30 days — about 22,000 new installs a day, according to AppBrain's tracking. Google Play users rate it 4.3 stars from nearly 5,000 ratings, and it carries an Everyone content rating. For a utility from a developer most people had never heard of two months ago, that is a breakout — and the reason is visible the moment you open it.

All screenshots in this review are the developer's own promotional images from the official Google Play Store listing.

What ClearNary does

ClearNary attacks one specific problem: a gallery that has quietly eaten your phone's storage. Burst shots, near-identical photos, screenshots you forgot, screen recordings, and old files you will never open again. Instead of asking you to scroll through thousands of thumbnails, it groups the clutter and lets you deal with it in batches — most distinctively with a swipe gesture. Swipe right to keep a photo, swipe left to delete it, and work through a pile of duplicates in minutes rather than an evening.

That swipe-to-clean idea is the app's real selling point. Most cleaners show you a list and a delete button; ClearNary turns the review itself into the interaction. It is fast, it is oddly satisfying, and it lowers the barrier to actually doing the cleanup instead of putting it off until the next "storage full" warning.

Key features

Duplicate and blurry photo cleaner. The AI engine scans your gallery for identical shots, similar photos and blurry images, then groups them so you can keep the best one and drop the rest. The promotional screenshots show a set of 114 duplicate photos totalling just 53 KB of clutter in one group — small on its own, but these groups add up across a whole gallery.

Video organiser and large-video cleaner. Videos are usually the real storage hogs, and ClearNary filters them by size and type — including screen recordings — so you can spot the biggest files first instead of deleting photos one by one.

Large and old file finder. Beyond photos and videos, the app scans for redundant documents, downloads and old files. One screenshot shows 528 files totalling 7.72 GB, broken down into Photos (162.79 MB), Videos (3.44 GB) and Other (4.13 GB) — a useful reminder that the biggest wins are rarely in the camera roll.

Smart media viewer. A date-sorted grid lets you browse photos and videos from newest to oldest, so you can find what is eating your memory without opening a separate gallery app.

Local, private scanning. The developer states that all scanning and media processing happens on your device, with no data collection. For an app that looks through your photos, that is the right promise — and one worth holding it to.

Pros and cons

Pros: genuinely fast growth with a solid 4.3-star rating; the swipe-to-clean review is quicker and less tedious than list-based cleaners; tiny 18 MB download; free with ads; clear category breakdowns show where storage actually went; on-device scanning with no data collection claimed.

Cons: contains ads; deletions are permanent with no recovery or cloud backup, so one careless swipe can cost you a photo; the "AI" labelling is doing some heavy lifting — the core job is grouping similar files, not magic; and as with every cleaner, the space it frees depends entirely on how cluttered your phone already is.

Pricing

ClearNary is free to download and contains ads. There is no mention of a paid tier on the Play listing, so the ads are the trade-off for a free cleaner — a fair deal at this size, though heavy users may find the ad breaks annoying during long swipe sessions.

How it compares

If you want a broader storage tool that also scans junk files and manages apps, our Smart Clean Pro review covers another fast-rising October cleaner. ClearNary is the more focused of the two: it does one job — gallery and file cleanup — and its swipe review makes that one job faster than a checklist ever will.

The honest take

ClearNary earns its growth. The swipe-to-clean flow is a genuinely better way to clear duplicates, the category breakdowns stop you deleting blindly, and 18 MB is a small price for a tool that can free gigabytes. The one rule to follow is the developer's own: deletions are permanent, so review before you confirm — keep the best shot, delete the rest, and never swipe through a set while half asleep. Used that way, it is one of the most useful free utilities on Play right now. Grab it from the official Play Store link below, never from a third-party APK site.
