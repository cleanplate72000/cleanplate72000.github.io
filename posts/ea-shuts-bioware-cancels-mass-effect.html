EA Shuts BioWare, Cancels Next Mass Effect

Electronic Arts is closing BioWare, the studio behind Mass Effect and Dragon Age, after more than 30 years — and early development on the next Mass Effect game is ending with it. The news was discussed on the October 24 episode of PocketGamer.biz's Week in Mobile Games podcast, which also covers Supercell's completed takeover of Metacore and Mika Games' $10m soft-launch funding.

Why this matters: BioWare helped define story-driven role-playing games, so its closure ends one of gaming's longest-running studios — and it leaves the future of Mass Effect as a game series uncertain, even as its screen adaptation is reportedly still moving ahead.

EA closes BioWare: key facts

  • What closed: BioWare, the studio behind Mass Effect and Dragon Age, after more than 30 years
  • What was cancelled: early development on the next Mass Effect game, first announced in 2020 and widely called Mass Effect 5
  • Scale of the cuts: around 400 job cuts across EA, as reported in the PocketGamer.biz podcast discussion
  • BioWare's own words: the studio said its focus is on supporting its team through the transition
  • Also in the same episode: Supercell has completed its full takeover of Metacore, and Mika Games has raised $10m for soft-launch testing

What to watch next

Watch for what EA says next about the Mass Effect franchise itself — the closure announcement did not settle that question. IGN has reported that Amazon MGM Studios' live-action Mass Effect television series remains in development, but that is a separate project from the cancelled game.
