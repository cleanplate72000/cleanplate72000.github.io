Dice Burst Review: Merge Dice Fun (2026)

Quick verdict — 4.0 / 5. Dice Burst: Merge Block Puzzle is a small, sharp number puzzle with one great idea: merge three matching dice into the next number, make a 6, and watch it burst its whole row and column. It is free, offline, tiny at about 22 MB, and genuinely relaxing — just expect ads, a still-small rating base, and a game that gets crowded fast once the board fills up.

Dice Burst: Merge Block Puzzle, from developer Staple Games, arrived on Google Play in October 2026 and has already been tracked at around 47,000 installs, all in the last 30 days — roughly 4,300 installs a day. On the AppBrain snapshot dated 23 October 2026 it holds 4.1 stars from about 300 ratings, and it is climbing the new-puzzle charts, reaching #10 Top New Free Puzzle in Mexico, #11 in Turkey, #14 in India, #18 in Norway and #19 in Saudi Arabia. For a game barely two weeks old, that is a fast start — and the reason it earns a review here this week.

The pitch is the whole game in one line, from the developer itself: “Drop dice. Merge three of a kind. Make a 6 and BURST its whole row and column!” If you like block puzzles, merge games, or number games in the spirit of 2048, Dice Burst puts all three in the same board — and it does so without timers, energy bars, or waiting around.

How it plays

You play on a 6x6 board. Pieces made of dice drop in from above, and you drag each piece onto the board. Put three matching dice together and they merge into the next number: three 1s make a 2, three 2s make a 3, and so on. Merge three 5s into a 6 and that 6 BURSTS, clearing its entire row and column in a shower of points — the screenshot above shows scores of +3,280, +2,970 and +1,350 flying off a single burst.

That burst is the whole strategy. A careless drop fills the board with low dice you cannot merge; a planned drop sets up a chain, where one merge feeds the next and the score multiplies. The game calls these chains out proudly — “CHAIN x3” on one screenshot, “COMBO x8” and “UNBELIEVABLE!” on another — and chasing a bigger chain is what keeps a run going after the novelty wears off. When there is no room left for your pieces, the run ends, and you try to beat your high score.

Game modes

Dice Burst is not just one endless board. It ships with three modes, which is generous for a 22 MB free game:

  • Classic — an endless high-score dice puzzle that grows with you. This is the mode for a quick break or a long, relaxing session.
  • Journey — 100 levels across 10 colourful worlds, with star goals and boss levels to beat. This is the mode for players who want progress, not just a high score.
  • Daily Challenge — a fresh puzzle every day, with the same board for every player. Keep your streak alive and compare how you did against everyone else on the same board.

On top of that, there are 10 bright themes to unlock as you play, so the board itself changes look as you improve. The developer also stresses that the game is fully offline — no Wi-Fi or internet needed — which makes it a genuine commute or travel game rather than one that quietly demands a connection.

Why it is trending

Dice Burst is trending because its idea reads in a single screenshot. Merge games are everywhere, but a game where the goal is a visible, satisfying explosion — make a 6, clear a row and a column — is instantly understandable, and the bright dice, big scores and chain celebrations make it look fun before you even install it. At roughly 4,300 installs a day over its first two weeks, it is growing the way only a genuinely easy-to-recommend puzzle game does.

It also helps that the game respects your time and your phone. There are no timers, no energy system and no waiting, the download is only about 22 MB, and version 1.08 (updated 22 October 2026) runs on phones and tablets. One early reviewer on Google Play gave it 3 stars for originality alone, noting it is “not the same type, ppl copying others games” — a fair summary of why a small new puzzle can stand out in a crowded store.

Key features

  • Merge-to-burst core loop — three matching dice merge up; a 6 bursts its whole row and column.
  • 6x6 block-puzzle board — drag dice pieces onto the board, plan ahead, and keep space for the next piece.
  • Chain reactions and combos — back-to-back merges build combos and huge scores.
  • Three modes — endless Classic, 100-level Journey across 10 worlds, and a Daily Challenge with a shared board.
  • Fully offline play — no Wi-Fi needed, no timers, no energy, no waiting.
  • 10 unlockable themes — bright new board looks to earn as you play.
  • Tiny download — about 22 MB, so it is easy to try on a whim.
  • Everyone rating — Google Play rates the app's content as suitable for everyone.

Pros and cons

Pros
  • One clear, satisfying idea — merge to 6, burst the board — that is fun within a minute
  • Three real modes (Classic, 100-level Journey, Daily Challenge) for a tiny free game
  • Fully offline, no timers or energy — a genuine pick-up-and-play puzzle
  • Only about 22 MB, with 10 themes to unlock and a fast-growing player base
  • Everyone content rating, and a quick run fits a coffee break or a commute
Cons
  • Contains ads, as the developer states plainly: “Dice Burst is free to play and supported by ads”
  • Still a very young game: about 300 ratings is a small base, so the 4.1 average may move as more players arrive
  • The board fills up fast — a few careless drops can end a promising run
  • One core loop done well, rather than a variety of different puzzle types
  • No in-app purchase price range was shown in the listing data checked for this review, so treat any paid offer inside the app on its own merits

Pricing

Dice Burst: Merge Block Puzzle is free to download on Google Play, and it contains ads. The listing data checked for this review shows it as free with ads and no in-app purchase price range, so check any paid offer you meet inside the app before accepting it. The current version is 1.08, updated 22 October 2026, and the download is only about 22 MB — small enough that trying it costs you almost nothing but a few minutes.

Download

If you like a number puzzle that rewards planning over speed, Dice Burst is an easy recommendation: a fresh merge-to-burst twist, a 4.1-star average, and a loop — drop, merge, burst — that stays satisfying well past the first few boards.
