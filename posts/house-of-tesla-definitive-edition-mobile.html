House of Tesla Definitive Edition Hits Mobile

From the creator of The House of Da Vinci, Blue Brain Games has brought The House of Tesla: Definitive Edition to Android and iOS, sending players into Nikola Tesla's abandoned Wardenclyffe facility.

Why this matters: The House of Da Vinci series built a loyal following for its hands-on, puzzle-box style adventures, and this Definitive Edition gives mobile players a complete Tesla-themed mystery to work through — no console or PC needed.

House of Tesla: the key facts

  • Game: The House of Tesla: Definitive Edition, from Blue Brain Games, creator of The House of Da Vinci
  • Platforms: Android and iOS
  • Setting: Nikola Tesla's abandoned Wardenclyffe facility
  • Structure: Five chapters inspired by Tesla's plans and real-world locations
  • Gameplay: Explore unsettling rooms, discover wireless electricity experiments, and influence the current to open new paths

What to watch next

Watch for player reaction to the Definitive Edition's puzzles and whether Blue Brain Games expands the Tesla story further. For more mobile gaming news, see our Dispatch mobile release story and the Top 100 apps page.
