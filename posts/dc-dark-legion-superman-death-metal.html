DC: Dark Legion Adds Superman Death Metal

DC: Dark Legion has opened a Halloween-themed suite of events, and the headline prize is a new champion: Superman Death Metal. Following the Death Metal versions of Batman and Wonder Woman, players can now add this dark take on the Man of Steel to their lineup.

Why this matters: Death Metal is one of DC's biggest recent comic events, and Dark Legion keeps mining its roster — a free-to-earn Superman Death Metal gives existing players a strong reason to return for Halloween, and gives new players a striking hero to build a squad around.

DC: Dark Legion Superman Death Metal event: key facts

  • How to recruit him: send squads of heroes out to gather the materials needed to build a portal and rescue Superman Death Metal, then gather shards to summon him
  • Helping hands: DC's paranormal pair Zatanna and John Constantine assist across the Halloween-themed events
  • Team-up: the Death Metal variants of Wonder Woman and Batman can fight alongside Superman Death Metal
  • More supernatural tasks: Solar Sonar and Mystic Alignment — uncover the Disk of Ra and illuminate an occult board for rewards
  • Free code: WEBGIFT is valid from October 25 to October 31
  • Platforms: iOS and Android

What to watch next

The WEBGIFT code window opens on October 25, so claim it early in the week rather than leaving it to Halloween night. If you like story-driven games heading to phones, our Dispatch mobile news covers another big release arriving this December.

Source: Pocket Gamer, "DC: Dark Legion's latest event lets you grab Superman Death Metal to add to your lineup" (published October 23, 2026). Details as reported there; event dates and rewards may change.
