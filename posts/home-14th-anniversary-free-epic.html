Home Turns 14: Free on Epic Until Oct 29

Pixel-art horror Home has turned 14, and developer Benjamin Rivers is marking the anniversary with a new update and a free offer on mobile. The 14th Anniversary update is out now on iOS and Android, and for the first time the game is available on the Epic Games Store, free on mobile from October 22 to October 29, 2026.

Key facts

  • What: Home's 14th Anniversary update, with a free Epic Games Store mobile release.
  • Free window: October 22 to October 29, 2026, on the Epic Games Store for mobile.
  • Platforms: iOS and Android, via the Epic Games Store on mobile.
  • New in the update: Postmortem Mode, a developer commentary that changes with the choices you make, carried over from the Switch version.
  • Also added: a behind-the-scenes gallery, better controller support, and 60 FPS.
  • The game: a short pixel-art horror mystery — you wake up in a stranger's house during a storm, someone has been murdered, and working out who and how is the whole game. There are no enemies to fight and no inventory to manage.
  • History: Home first launched on PC in 2012, reached iPad a year later, and is still being updated by its original developer fourteen years on.

Why it matters

Fourteen years of updates from a solo developer is rare, and this anniversary package is a good entry point: a short mystery you can finish in an evening, rather than another live-service game demanding weeks of commitment. Postmortem Mode also adds a reason to replay it — the commentary shifts with your choices, just as the mystery itself does, since Home never settles on a single answer for you.

What to watch next

The free window closes on October 29, so anyone interested should claim it before then. Note that the Epic Games Store on a phone needs a separate install with a few extra permission steps, and that Home is a horror murder mystery — check the store age rating before handing it to younger players.
