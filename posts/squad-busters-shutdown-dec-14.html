Supercell's Squad Busters Ends Dec 14

Supercell has confirmed the end dates for Squad Busters: the multiplayer mobile game will be removed from the App Store and Google Play on December 1, 2026, and it will stop being playable on December 14, 2026.

Why this matters: If you still play Squad Busters, December 14 is now a firm deadline — after that date there is no way back in, and Supercell has ruled out an offline version.

Squad Busters shutdown: the key facts

  • Removed from stores: December 1, 2026, from the App Store and Google Play
  • Stops being playable: December 14, 2026, when all in-game events and timers end
  • Installed players: Anyone who already has the game installed can keep playing until December 14
  • Why it is closing: Supercell said it could not see “a clear path forward” after several reworks, including its Heroes update, and further changes would have made it almost a completely new game
  • Background: Squad Busters launched worldwide in May 2024, bringing together characters from Clash of Clans, Clash Royale, Brawl Stars, Hay Day and Boom Beach
  • In-app purchases: Switched off since the end of development was announced last October, and the window to transfer purchase value to other Supercell games closed at the end of 2025
  • No offline fallback: Supercell has ruled out offline play, private servers and releasing the game's code, saying it contains content and characters from across its other titles
  • The team: Supercell said earlier this year that the Squad Busters team remains at the company, with most already helping other teams

What to watch next

Keep the game installed on the device you plan to play on — once it leaves the stores on December 1, a deleted copy cannot easily be reinstalled for the final two weeks. Save any screenshots or clips you want to keep before December 14, when player profiles and statistics become inaccessible.
