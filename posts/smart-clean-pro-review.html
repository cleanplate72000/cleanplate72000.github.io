Smart Clean Pro Review: Tidy Phone (2026)

Quick verdict — 3.5 / 5. Smart Clean Pro is a genuinely handy storage tidy-up tool with a fast-growing audience — but its mixed user reviews mean you should use it carefully: let it find the junk, then delete only the files you recognise.

Smart Clean Pro from JTR Labs is one of October 2026's fastest-rising Android utilities. It launched on Google Play in October 2026 and, according to AppBrain's tracking, picked up around 160,000 installs in its first 30 days — roughly 14,000 a day — climbing to #10 in Japan's Top Free Tools chart and #24 in the United States Tools chart. Google Play itself lists it at 100,000+ downloads with an Everyone content rating. That is a striking debut for a no-name utility — but the user reviews are more divided than the chart positions suggest, and this review looks at both sides.

All screenshots in this review are the developer's own promotional images from the official Google Play Store listing.

What Smart Clean Pro does

The pitch is simple: your phone fills up with files you never asked for — cached data, leftover files from uninstalled apps, duplicate photos and videos you forgot you took — and Smart Clean Pro finds them for you. Open the app and you get a clear readout of what it found, a Clean button, and a set of shortcut tiles for the individual tools. The developer's own Play Store description stresses the same point twice: this is a storage management tool, and all deletions require your confirmation. Nothing is deleted silently, and the app only scans and manages files locally on the device.

That confirmation step is the most important feature in the whole app, and it is worth taking seriously. Storage cleaners live or die on trust: a tool that deletes things without showing you what they are deserves suspicion, however good its charts look. Smart Clean Pro's approach — show what was found, let you pick, then clean — is the right way round. Used that way, clearing obvious junk and duplicates you recognise, it is a genuinely useful tool for phones that are always running low on space.

Key features that matter

  • Junk File Scan — finds non-essential files such as cache, ad junk and residual files left behind by other apps.
  • Large File Manager — helps you locate large videos, documents and other storage-heavy items that are quietly eating your space.
  • App Manager — shows installed apps and their storage usage, so you can review and uninstall apps manually instead of guessing which ones are the biggest.
  • Duplicate Photo Cleaner — groups similar and duplicate photos, keeps the best copy, and lets you delete the rest in one tap.
  • Network Traffic view — shows how much mobile data each app has used this month, with a Stop option per app.
  • Screenshot, Video and Notification Cleaners — extra shortcuts shown on the home screen for the clutter that builds up fastest.
  • Safe Cleanup promise — every deletion needs your confirmation, and scanning happens locally on the device.
  • Lightweight and free — around 39 MB, version 1.0.0 (updated 2 October 2026), free to download, Everyone rating.

What the screenshots actually show

The listing's own screenshots are refreshingly concrete. The home screen shows 503.8 MB of junk files found, with storage 16% used and RAM 72% used, plus tiles for Network Traffic, Notification Cleaner, App Manager, Screenshot Cleaner, Duplicate Photo Cleaner, Video Cleaner, Big File Cleaner, RAM Status and Device Scan. The Duplicate Photo screen groups near-identical photos of the same subject and offers to delete the spares. The Network Traffic screen breaks down a month's data use — 44.3 GB in the example — app by app, with a Stop button beside each one. And the App Manager sorts apps by last used, size or install time, showing, for example, LINE at 269.7 MB, so you can spot the apps you forgot you had.

The honest caveats

Here is where the review has to be blunt. Google Play's own page shows a mixed picture: around 3.7 stars from 686 reviews at the time of writing, well below the higher snapshot figure shown by some tracking sites. The most helpful review on the listing says the app made the reviewer's phone run faster; another highly-rated review calls it terrible and says it took over the phone. A small number of reviewers report aggressive behaviour. That split is normal for a brand-new cleaner — early adopters either love the quick win or hate an unexpected prompt — but it means you should not install it blindly.

Three rules keep you safe with any cleaner, this one included. First, never let it delete a file you do not recognise — if you are unsure what something is, leave it. Second, do not expect it to make an old phone feel new: Android already manages cached files on its own, and a cleaner tidies clutter, it does not upgrade your hardware. Third, back up photos before a big duplicate clean, even when the app promises to keep the best copy. Follow those, and Smart Clean Pro is a handy tool; ignore them, and any cleaner can cause regret.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Fastest-rising cleaner of October 2026 — around 160,000 installs in its first month
  • Clear home screen: junk found, storage and RAM at a glance, one Clean button
  • Every deletion needs your confirmation — nothing is removed silently
  • Duplicate Photo and Large File tools tackle the two biggest space-wasters
  • Network Traffic view is a genuinely useful extra most cleaners skip
  • Lightweight at around 39 MB, free to download, Everyone rating

Cons

  • Mixed reviews — around 3.7 stars on Play, with some users reporting aggressive behaviour
  • Very new: version 1.0.0, so long-term reliability is unproven
  • Will not speed up an old phone the way some cleaner ads imply
  • Feature set overlaps heavily with other cleaners, including our Cool Clean review

Pricing

Free to download on Google Play, with no upfront price on the store listing. AppBrain's snapshot of the listing shows no ads flag and no in-app purchases listed, but that can change — check the official Play Store listing for the current details before downloading, since free apps sometimes add purchases later.

Who it is for

Smart Clean Pro suits anyone whose phone is always warning about low storage and who wants one app to find the junk, the duplicates and the forgotten apps in a single pass. It is also a sensible first stop before you start deleting photos by hand or uninstalling apps you actually use — a quick scan often frees up more room than expected. It is not for power users who want deep system controls or scheduled deep-cleaning, and it is not a substitute for Android's own storage settings, which already handle the basics for free.

Final verdict

Smart Clean Pro earns its 3.5 out of 5 the honest way: a clear, useful tool with real traction — 160,000 installs in a month and top-10 Tools chart spots do not happen by accident — held back by mixed early reviews and the usual cleaner caveats. If your phone is always running low on space, it is worth a try, used carefully: let it find the junk, check what it found, and delete only what you recognise.
