Football Manager 27 Touch on Apple Arcade

Sega's Football Manager 27 Touch is coming to Apple Arcade. The football management sim launches on November 10 for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV, giving subscribers the full Touch experience on every Apple device.

Why this matters: FM Touch is the pick-up-and-play version of Football Manager, and an Apple Arcade launch puts it in front of subscribers at no extra cost — with saves carrying over from past titles, returning managers can pick up right where they left off.

FM27 Touch: what we know

  • Release date: November 10, on Apple Arcade
  • Publisher: Sega
  • Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV
  • Save carry-over: Players can continue saves from past titles and carry progress across devices via the cloud
  • App Store page: The existing FM26 Touch page will update to FM27 Touch automatically on launch day

What is FM Touch?

Football Manager Touch is the streamlined edition of Sega's long-running football management series: you take charge of a club, handle transfers, tactics and training, and guide your team through the season — without the full depth of the desktop game. On Apple Arcade it sits alongside the service's other sports titles, playable across phone, tablet, Mac and TV with progress synced in the cloud.

What to watch next

Watch for the FM26 Touch App Store page flipping over to FM27 Touch on November 10 — existing players should not need to download a separate app. We will update this story if Sega confirms any new features for the Touch edition.
