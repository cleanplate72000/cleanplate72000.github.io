Hair Salon Makeover Review: Salon Fun (2026)

Quick verdict — 4.0 / 5. Hair Salon Makeover Studio is a bright, relaxing salon game: wash, cut, curl, straighten, dry and colour a model's hair, then finish the look with makeup, an outfit and accessories. It is free, easy to pick up and genuinely charming — just expect plenty of ads, the game's most common complaint.

Hair Salon Makeover Studio, from developer MR. PROXY CORE, launched on Google Play in August 2026 and has already passed 2.6 million installs tracked in total, with around 1.7 million of those in the last 30 days — roughly 57,000 installs a day. On Google Play it holds 4.3 stars from about 5,000 ratings, and it is climbing the free casual charts, reaching #16 Top Free Casual in Mexico with top-50 casual showings in Slovakia, Brazil, Canada and Germany. For a salon game barely two months old, that is a fast start — and the reason it earns a review here this week.

The idea is simple and immediately appealing: you run your own beauty studio. Each model arrives ready for a transformation, and you work through the salon routine step by step — wash the hair, choose a cut, curl or straighten it, dry it, and pick a colour, from soft natural tones to bold purple and pink. Then comes the rest of the makeover: makeup, a fashionable outfit and accessories, before the finished look goes into your personal photo album. The developer's own pitch is straightforward: “Create stylish haircuts, colors, makeup looks, and fashion makeovers.”

How it plays

The loop follows a real salon visit, which is part of the charm. You start with the hair itself: wash it, pick up the scissors, then shape it with a straightener, a curler or a dryer. The screenshots show the tool table clearly — scissors, straightener, brush and dryer laid out on a pink counter — and each tool does what you would expect, so there is no tutorial wall to climb before the fun starts. Colour is where the game lets loose: the purple braid, the pink-and-yellow pigtails and the sleek black bun in the store screenshots show how far apart two finished looks can be.

Once the hair is done, the makeover continues. Makeup, outfits and accessories turn a good hairstyle into a complete look, and the photo album gives you a reason to keep going: each finished model is a small creative project worth saving. There are no timers pushing you along, and the game works offline, so a makeover fits a bus ride, a waiting room or a quiet evening just as well. At about 61 MB, version 1.1.1 (updated 28 September 2026), it is a light download for a polished casual game.

Why it is trending

Hair Salon Makeover Studio is trending because it takes a proven dress-up idea and makes it instantly readable. Salon games live or die on their first screenshot, and a model with a finished purple braid or a pink-and-yellow hairstyle reads in a second — no explanation needed. Players seem to agree: one reviewer calls it very nice and says they are glad they installed it, while another praises it warmly. At roughly 57,000 installs a day over the last 30 days, it is growing the way only a genuinely easy-to-recommend casual game does.

It also helps that the game respects short sessions. A single makeover takes a few minutes, the goal is always visible — a finished, photo-ready look — and you can dip in for one model or a whole salon full. The offline play is a real bonus here: no connection needed, no waiting for a server, just the salon.

Key features

  • Full salon routine — wash, cut, curl, straighten, dry and colour hair with a complete set of salon tools.
  • Bold colours and styles — natural tones or vivid purple, pink and two-tone looks, plus braids, buns and pigtails.
  • Makeup, outfits and accessories — finish every look with makeup, a fashionable outfit and matching accessories.
  • Photo album for finished looks — save each completed makeover in your personal album.
  • Relaxing offline gameplay — no connection needed and no timers pushing you along.
  • Easy controls — tools laid out on the counter, each doing what you would expect.
  • Many models to style — each new model is a fresh creative project.
  • Everyone rating — Google Play rates the app's content as suitable for everyone.

Pros and cons

Pros
  • Complete salon loop — wash, cut, style, colour, makeup and outfit in one relaxing game
  • Strong momentum: 2.6 million tracked installs, 1.7 million in the last 30 days, 4.3 stars from about 5,000 ratings
  • Instantly readable looks — bold hairstyles that read in a single screenshot
  • Offline play and short sessions — a makeover fits a coffee break or a commute
  • Everyone content rating, and a light 61 MB download
Cons
  • Ads are the top complaint — reviewers report frequent pop-up ads, one saying an ad appears every few seconds
  • One reviewer says the in-game characters differ from the promotional pictures — judge it by the gameplay, not just the key art
  • One game type done well, rather than a variety of modes — know what you are downloading
  • Play's data-safety section notes the app may share app activity and device IDs, data is not encrypted, and data cannot be deleted
  • Still a young game: about 5,000 ratings is a small base, so the 4.3 average may move as more players arrive

Pricing

Hair Salon Makeover Studio is free to download on Google Play, and it contains ads. The listing data checked for this review shows it as free with ads and no in-app purchase price range, so treat any paid offer you meet inside the app on its own merits and check it before accepting. The current version is 1.1.1, updated 28 September 2026, and the download is about 61 MB — worth it if the salon loop clicks for you.

Download

Download Hair Salon Makeover Studio on Google Play

If you like a creative game that rewards styling over speed, Hair Salon Makeover Studio is an easy recommendation: a complete salon routine, a 4.3-star average, and a loop — wash, style, dress up, save the look — that stays satisfying well past the first few models.
