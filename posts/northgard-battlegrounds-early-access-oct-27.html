Northgard Battlegrounds Hits Early Access

Northgard Battlegrounds, the Viking city-building auto-battler from Shiro Games and Funday Games, enters Steam Early Access today, 27 October 2026. Announced by the Northgard Battlegrounds team on Steam, the spin-off takes the world of the 5-million-selling strategy game Northgard and turns it into a player-versus-player auto-battler where you build the settlement and your Vikings do the fighting.

Northgard Battlegrounds: the key facts

  • Early Access date: 27 October 2026, on PC via Steam.
  • Who made it: developed by Funday Games, the team behind Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor, and published by Shiro Unlimited, the publishing label of Northgard creator Shiro Games.
  • How it plays: each round, players expand a Viking settlement, placing buildings, stacking adjacency bonuses and spending Lore on boons from the Norse gods to strengthen their economy and army.
  • The clans: lead the Raven, Stag or Wolf clan, each with its own strengths, and combine clan abilities, Fate Cards and structures to build a powerful warband.
  • The gods: earn the favour of Odin, Baldr and Thor, whose boons can boost your settlement and fighters.
  • The battles: combat runs automatically and asynchronously against other players — you plan the settlement, your Vikings fight.
  • The road here: a demo launched on 6 October 2026, with the Wolf clan added on 19 October during Steam Next Fest, ahead of today's Early Access release.

Why it matters

Northgard Battlegrounds is a genuine change of genre, not a sequel: it keeps the Viking settlement building that made Northgard a hit, but hands the fighting over to auto-battler rules. That makes it a test of whether a strategy audience will follow a familiar world into a very different kind of competition, where placement and synergy matter more than battlefield reflexes. With three clans, god boons and Fate Cards to combine, the Early Access period will show whether its builds stay varied or settle into a few dominant strategies.
