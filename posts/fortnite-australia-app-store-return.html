Fortnite Returns to Australian App Store

Epic Games has brought Fortnite back to the Australian App Store, ending its long absence from Apple's official storefront in Australia. The return follows a Federal Court of Australia ruling that Apple violated competition law by restricting app distribution and payment options on iOS.

Why this matters: Australian iPhone and iPad players can once again download Fortnite directly from the App Store, and the ruling points to much wider changes to how iOS works in Australia next year.

Fortnite's Australian return: the key facts

  • What: Fortnite is back on the Australian App Store for iPhone and iPad
  • Why now: The Federal Court of Australia found Apple had violated competition law over iOS app distribution and in-app payments
  • Who announced it: Epic Games, in a statement on social media, called the ruling great news for developers and iOS users in Australia
  • Background: Epic had kept Fortnite off the Australian App Store while seeking further court orders relating to Apple's payment terms, even after it returned to the store in most other territories
  • Apple's response: Apple has said it will challenge the decision, seeking a stay and appealing the outcome
  • What changes in March 2027: iOS will open to third-party app stores in Australia, users will be able to download apps directly from developers, and developers will be able to offer alternative payment systems
  • Payments: Apple cannot require developers to offer Apple's in-app purchases, cannot block developers from pointing users to purchases outside apps, and is barred from charging junk fees on purchases in third-party app stores

What to watch next

Watch whether Apple's stay and appeal delay any of the March 2027 changes, and whether other developers follow Epic in offering alternative stores or payment options in Australia. For more Fortnite news, see our One Piece joins Fortnite's Treasure Hunters story, and browse the Top 100 apps page.
