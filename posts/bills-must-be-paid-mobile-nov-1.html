Bills Must Be Paid Hits Mobile on November 1

Bills Must Be Paid, the piggy-bank smashing roguelite from developer Rike Games, is coming to mobile on November 1 — for iOS and Android, as a one-time $5.99 purchase.

Why this matters: it is a premium mobile game with no subscription attached — you pay once, then smash, upgrade and come back stronger.

Bills Must Be Paid: the key facts

  • Release date: November 1, on iOS and Android, as confirmed by Pocket Gamer on October 26, 2026.
  • Price: $5.99 as a one-time purchase.
  • Developer: Rike Games, and this is its debut premium game.
  • What you do: smash piggy banks to get cash, while watching your stamina and keeping ahead of ever-increasing costs.
  • The twist: you fail, then come back stronger — at least in the hand department — with Balatro-style roguelite progression and upgrades.
  • Upgrades: rings for your hand, hammers, and various piggy banks that offer random loot or try to evade your hammer.
  • Style: an incremental game mixed with moment-to-moment smashing, aimed at players who like climbing numbers and active challenges.

Why it matters

Most incremental games on mobile are free-to-play with a long tail of in-app purchases. A one-time $5.99 price puts Bills Must Be Paid in a smaller, clearer group: the whole game, up front, with progression driven by upgrades and runs rather than a shop. For players tired of stamina timers that only move with a payment, that is the headline — even if stamina still matters inside the game itself.

What to watch next

Watch for the store listings on November 1 to confirm the price in your region, and for early player reaction to how the stamina and upgrade loop holds up over longer sessions. For more gaming news, see our Xbox Game Pass on T-Mobile story and browse the Top 100 apps page.
