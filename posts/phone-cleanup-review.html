Phone Cleanup Review: AI Storage Saver (2026)

Quick verdict — 4.0 / 5. Phone Cleanup: AI Cleaner does one job well: it finds the duplicate photos, look-alike videos and forgotten screenshots eating your storage, and lets you clear them with a swipe. It is free, rated Everyone, and genuinely fast-growing — but check each selection before you delete, and expect ads along the way.

Phone Cleanup: AI Cleaner, from developer CoreTools Lab, has been on Google Play since May 2026 and is already one of the fastest-growing tools apps of the autumn. AppBrain tracks 890,000 installs in total, with 410,000 of those in the last 30 days — roughly 14,000 new installs a day — and the app has reached #25 in the Top Free Tools chart in India and #42 in South Korea. Its live Google Play rating stands at 4.5 stars from 1,463 ratings, the current version is 1.2.2 (updated 14 September 2026), the download is about 53.6 MB, and Google Play rates its content Everyone. It is free to download and, according to AppBrain, contains ads.

The pitch is simple, and it is aimed at a problem almost every phone owner recognises: storage that fills up quietly, then suddenly refuses to take another photo. Instead of asking you to hunt through your gallery folder by folder, Phone Cleanup scans your media, groups together the files that are wasting space, and shows you exactly how much room each clean-up would free. The key art makes the point with a phone showing 117.9 GB used out of 128 GB and 14,000 files waiting to be cleaned — an illustrative promo figure, but a familiar one for anyone whose phone has ever flashed a storage warning at the worst moment.

How it works

Open the app and you get a storage overview first: how much space is used, how many files the app has found, and a big AI Smart Clean button. Below that, a Manual Clean section splits the work into Photos and Videos, each asking for access before it scans. That two-track design is the app's best idea. If you are in a hurry, the smart scan gives you a quick summary; if you want control, the manual sections let you go category by category and decide for yourself.

The main dashboard, shown in the second screenshot, keeps the same layout: storage used at the top, the AI Smart Clean shortcut underneath, and the manual photo and video sections below. There is nothing to configure before your first scan, no account screen in the way, and no tutorial wall — you grant access, it scans, and you see the results. For a utility app, that lack of friction matters: most people open a cleaner because their phone is already full, not because they want a project.

Why it is trending

Phone Cleanup is trending for the least glamorous reason in the app world: it solves a boring, universal problem, and it does so without a subscription wall at the front door. Storage cleaners surge whenever phones fill up — after holidays, after a new baby, after a year of screenshots — and a free, Everyone-rated cleaner with a 4.5-star early rating spreads fast by word of mouth and by chart position. Four hundred thousand installs in a single month, on top of a top-25 tools ranking in India, is exactly that pattern playing out.

The other reason is that its feature list matches what people actually complain about. Duplicates are the classic storage thief: the same photo saved three times, the burst-mode set where only one shot is good, the video you recorded twice. A cleaner that groups those together and tells you the saving in megabytes — rather than just saying "junk found" — feels honest, and honesty is in short supply in this category. The developer's promise that all processing happens locally on your device, with nothing uploaded, is also a genuine selling point for a tool that needs access to your photos.

Duplicate photos, handled properly

The duplicate-photos screen is where the app earns its keep. It groups copies together, marks one in each set as "Best", and shows the total at the top — in the screenshot, 1,020 photos taking up 2.3 GB, with a selection that would free 140 MB. You can select all, deselect all, or pick individual shots, and the delete button states the saving plainly before you tap it. That transparency matters, because deleting photos is the one action in this app you cannot easily undo.

Our advice, and the developer's own design, agree here: review before you delete. The "Best" pick is the app's guess, not a guarantee — a slightly blurrier shot of a laughing friend can be the one you actually want. Swipe through each group, keep the version with the best expression or framing, and only then confirm. The same caution applies to the similar-photos and blurry-photos categories the Play description lists: the app finds candidates, but you are the final judge of which memories matter.

Video clean-up and compression

Videos are where the real space hides, and the app treats them as a separate job. It groups similar videos, duplicate videos, short recordings and old screen recordings, and again states the saving up front — the screenshot shows 170 videos with a four-video selection freeing 360 MB. If your storage warning is really a video problem, this screen alone can buy you months of breathing room.

The app also offers photo and video compression, with a choice of compression level and the option to manage or replace the originals. Treat the marketing line "without compromising quality" with healthy scepticism: any compression trades some quality for size, by definition. The sensible approach is to compress copies of videos you want to keep for reference, and keep full-quality originals of anything irreplaceable. Used that way, compression is a useful second tool alongside deletion, not a replacement for it.

Key features

  • AI Smart Clean — a quick scan summarising what is filling your storage, with one-tap access to the results.
  • Duplicate and similar photo detection — groups copies, look-alikes and blurry shots, marking a "Best" pick in each set.
  • Video clean-up — similar videos, duplicates, short recordings and screen recordings, each with its space saving shown.
  • Swipe to clean — swipe left to remove a file, swipe right to keep it or review it later.
  • Photo and video compression — shrink large media at a compression level you choose, and manage the originals.
  • Storage insights — a clear overview of how your photos and videos are using your phone's space.
  • On-device processing — the developer states that all data processing happens locally, with nothing uploaded.
  • Everyone rating — a family-safe utility with no age-gated content.

Pros and cons

Pros
  • Fast-growing and well-rated: 890,000 installs, 410,000 in the last 30 days, 4.5 stars
  • Shows the exact space saving before every deletion — no vague "junk found" claims
  • Manual photo and video sections give you full control alongside the quick smart scan
  • On-device processing claim: your photos are not uploaded to a server for cleaning
  • Free download, Everyone rating, and a modest 53.6 MB install size
Cons
  • Contains ads, according to the Play listing data checked for this review
  • 4.5-star average rests on only ~1,463 ratings so far — early days, not a settled verdict
  • Deleting photos is permanent: the "Best" pick is a guess, so review each group first
  • Compression always trades some quality for size, despite the "without compromising quality" claim
  • Broad photo and video access is required — only grant it if you are comfortable with a cleaner browsing your gallery

Pricing

Phone Cleanup: AI Cleaner is free to download on Google Play, and the listing data checked for this review shows no in-app purchase price range — so treat the free download as the only pricing the listing itself confirms, and judge any paid offer inside the app on its own merits before accepting it. The app does contain ads, so expect promotional interruptions in exchange for the free clean-up tools. For a utility you may only open when storage gets tight, that trade-off is easy to live with.

Download

Download Phone Cleanup: AI Cleaner on Google Play

If your phone is warning you about storage, Phone Cleanup is a solid first stop: free, fast-growing, and refreshingly upfront about how much space each clean-up actually saves. Just take thirty seconds to review each group before you delete — your future self will thank you for keeping the right version of every photo.
