Monster Hunter Rise Spotted on Mobile

Monster Hunter Rise looks headed to iOS and Android as a Netflix game. Store listings for the Capcom hunting hit have appeared on Google Play and the App Store, spotted by Eurogamer, but neither Netflix nor Capcom has officially announced the mobile version yet — so treat this as unconfirmed for now.

Why this matters: Rise is one of Capcom's biggest hits, with more than 20 million copies sold across Switch, PC, Xbox and PlayStation. A Netflix version would put a full console-scale Monster Hunter in subscribers' pockets at no extra cost beyond their membership.

Monster Hunter Rise on mobile: the key facts

  • What was spotted: Listings for Monster Hunter Rise appeared on Google Play and the App Store on October 7, 2026, and on tracking site AppMagic the same day
  • Publisher: Netflix is listed as the publisher, with Capcom credited as the creator
  • Price: Free to download, but a Netflix membership is required to play
  • Release date: None given yet — no date appears in the listings
  • Challenge and Event Quests: Quests previously sold as paid or temporary downloadable content are now unlocked through progression
  • Recovery Button: A new button automatically picks the best item to use in a fight, based on the hunter's health and status
  • Controls: Touch controls rebuilt for phones and tablets, including each weapon's control scheme
  • Original game: Monster Hunter Rise launched on Nintendo Switch in 2021 and later came to PC, Xbox and PlayStation

What to watch next

Watch for an official announcement from Netflix or Capcom confirming the release date and whether the game will be available in all Netflix regions. Until then, the listings are the only evidence, and details could change.
